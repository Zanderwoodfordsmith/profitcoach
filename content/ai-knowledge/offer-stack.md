# Offer Stack — Profit Coach

> What the Profit Coach / BOSS brand sells, to whom, at what price, and how the revenue streams connect. Every AI employee for this brand gets this document.

---

## Revenue Architecture Overview

BOSS has two revenue streams at launch, with future streams planned:

```
NOW:    BOSS 6-Month Transformation     → £4,950
NOW:    £495 Strategy Call               → £495
LATER:  Board Groups (BOSS + Human)     → £2,000/month
LATER:  Coach Licensing                  → £99–199/month per seat
```

No free tier. No low-ticket front end. The diagnostic is free — it IS the sales funnel.

---

## Primary Offer: BOSS 6-Month Transformation

### Who It's For

Business owners doing £200K–£5M+ revenue who want to diagnose what's broken, know what to fix first, and track whether it's working. Not a course. Not coaching. A bounded system with measurable outcomes.

### The Promise

In 6 months: diagnose your business, fix the highest-impact issues in sequence, and track the results. Two full 90-day cycles. Before/after data makes renewal obvious, not sold.

### Pricing & Terms

| Option | Price | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Full pay | £4,950 | One payment |
| 3-pay | £1,750 × 3 | Three instalments |
| 6-pay (checkout save) | £895 × 6 | Shown only to checkout abandoners |
| Monthly | £995/month (min 3 months) | Makes 6-month package look like obvious value |

No tiers at launch. One price, one decision, one programme. Tiers come after 50+ members.

### What's Included

- BOSS Diagnostic (50 questions, 5 levels, 9 categories + Owner Performance, scored to 100)
- BOSS Wheel (radar chart with before/after slider) and BOSS Grid (red/yellow/green detail)
- ROI Proof Engine (funnel multiplier showing £ gap + time recapture + margin insight)
- 90-Day Plan Builder (top 3 priorities per quarter, AI-suggested from diagnostic)
- BOSS Playbooks (single-page each: problem, framework, action steps, template, expected result)
- Weekly Scoreboard (team fills in 10 min, owner reads 30 sec, retention hook)
- AI Coach (Phase 2 — fed with all coaching methodology + owner's data)

### What BOSS Does NOT Do

- No task management (that's ClickUp's job)
- No to-do lists, no project tracking
- No habit tracking, no personal development wheel (Phase 3/4 upsell)
- No community at launch (build when 200+ users)
- No video courses, no modules, no curriculum

### Founding Member Strategy

20 founding members. Exclusive, not discounted. £4,950 for six months. Founding members get:
- Locked-in pricing for life
- First access to every new feature
- Direct line to shape what BOSS becomes

20 × £4,950 = £99,000 in revenue before building a single feature beyond the diagnostic.

### Renewal

At month 5, show the before/after BOSS wheel. The data sells the renewal. Offer another 6 months at £4,950, or pay for 6 get 12 (loyalty reward).

## The £495 Strategy Call

A paid 1:1 walking the business owner through their BOSS diagnostic results. Positioned on the results page with an embedded calendar and payment. No separate sales page — the diagnostic results ARE the sales page.

Three purposes:
1. Revenue from the call itself
2. Upsell path to the full BOSS programme
3. Market research on objections and buyer psychology

Can be delivered by Zander/Pam directly, or by BCA coaches (see Partner Model below).

## BCA Coach Partner Model

BCA coaches become a distribution channel for BOSS. This is the single most important strategic relationship in the architecture.

### The Three Revenue Streams for Coaches

| Stream | Amount | How It Works |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Strategy Call** | £495/call | Coach runs the paid deep dive using BOSS diagnostic. Their calendar, their link. They keep the full £495. |
| **1:1 Coaching** | £2,000/month | Some call attendees become the coach's own clients. Coach keeps 100%. BOSS framework structures delivery. |
| **BOSS Referral** | £1,500 flat | If the prospect joins BOSS central instead of 1:1 coaching, coach earns £1,500 commission. Paid in line with payment plan. |

### Example: 8 Calls Per Month
- 8 × £495 = £3,960 from calls
- 2 become 1:1 clients at £2,000/month = £4,000 recurring
- 2 join BOSS central = 2 × £1,500 = £3,000 commission
- **Total first month: ~£10,960**

### Quality Controls
- Standard call script built around BOSS diagnostic output
- Coaches cannot discount BOSS — price is always £4,950
- Zander controls checkout, billing, onboarding
- Poor call quality damages the BOSS brand — process must be systematised

## Key Decisions (Logged)

- **£4,950 price point:** Low enough for a £200K+ business. High enough for premium. ROI from funnel multiplier = 62:1 ratio.
- **No tiers at launch:** One price, one decision. Tiers after 50+ members.
- **Founding members exclusive, not discounted:** Locked pricing + first access. Feels smart, not cheap.
- **£1,500 flat coach commission:** Simpler than 30%. Easy to calculate.
- **Coaches cannot discount BOSS:** Protects brand premium.
- **Results page embeds £495 call:** No separate sales page. Reduces friction to near zero.
