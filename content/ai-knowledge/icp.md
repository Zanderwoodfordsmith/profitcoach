# Ideal Client Profile (Compact) — Profit Coach

> Who BOSS serves, in brief. Every AI employee for this brand gets this document. For full psychographic detail, see ICP Deep Dive (Revenue Intelligence).

---

## Who We Serve

Owner-led businesses doing **£1M+ revenue**. The ideal client sits between **£2M and £20M**, with £1M–£10M the core of the market. They have real operations, a team, and enough revenue for the investment to be a straightforward decision.

### The Ideal BOSS Buyer

- **Revenue:** £1M+ (ideal: £2M–£20M; core: £1M–£10M). Public copy prints "£1M+".
- **Sector:** Trades, engineering, manufacturing, construction, services. Owner-led matters more than sector.
- **Role:** Owner-operator, still heavily involved in the day-to-day
- **Situation:** Doing everything themselves, can't focus on what matters, know something's broken but not sure what to fix first
- **Mindset:** Want tools and answers, not therapy or motivation. Entrepreneurs want specific, operational, solvable problems addressed.

### Data-Backed Insights (from 336 Stryv + 39 StryvX surveys + 81 app responses)

- **54% want 2–3x income** — growth ambition is the norm in the audience data (note: survey base is the Stryv audience, not confirmed BOSS buyers)
- **72% cite fear as primary block** — not ignorance, not money. They know what to do but can't get themselves to do it. BOSS provides clarity that dissolves fear.
- **Top pain: Time/Productivity (19%)** — doing everything themselves, can't delegate or prioritise. This is a systems problem BOSS solves directly.

## Why This Customer (Not Coaches, Not Corporate)

The BOSS sale is fundamentally different from the BCA sale:

| | BCA (Push Sale) | BOSS (Pull Sale) |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Director considering a career change | Owner with an existing business |
| Decision | Identity decision (terrifying) | Mathematical decision (logical) |
| Selling | Emotional rescue mission | Data-driven — they see the score and the £ gap |
| Conversion | Structurally painful | Structurally easier |

The buyer already has a business. They already know something's wrong. They see their score, see £180K in unrealised revenue, see the three things to fix. The decision isn't existential. It's mathematical.
