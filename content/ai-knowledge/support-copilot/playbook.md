# Support playbook

Facts the support copilot can use. Keep each answer to one line. Update this when a fix ships or a link changes. If a fact is not here, the copilot should ask staff or leave a placeholder.

## Where things live

- New platform (since May 2026): https://theprofitcoach.com/login. Training, campaigns, prospects, conversations, community and support all live here.
- "This platform is no longer active" means they opened the old training platform (old academy hub / Disco). Send them to the new login above.
- Old CRM (Pro Coach CRM): https://app.procoachplatform.com. Existing automations, calendars and BOSS nurture emails still run there while coaches move across. They do not have to move until they are ready.
- Group calls: Monday and Thursday. Same Zoom link as always: https://businesscoachacademy.com/calls. Recordings are in the classroom.
- Coach directory: https://www.theprofitcoach.com/directory. Coaches choose what shows from their profile settings.
- Brand logos (print ready): https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1HOJrtvNCSk3qUoqiQXxQvUeKh2ns89Oo?usp=sharing
- Support calls: use the SUPPORT_CALL_URL given in the prompt as a markdown link: [Book a support call](URL).

## Access

- Forgot password: use "Forgot password" on the login page and check spam. If no email arrives, staff reset it. Never send a password in a ticket.
- Old links in lessons that go to Disco or ask for a different login are out of date. Thank them, say we are updating that lesson, and give the new location if known.

## Calendars and bookings

- Booking has no Zoom or meeting link: the calendar has no location set, or the location app is not connected. Fix in the calendar's settings by choosing the meeting location (Zoom or Google Meet).
- Wrong time on a booking: check the time zone in their calendar settings and profile.
- Two people on one call (old CRM): book the main contact, then add the second person to the Google Calendar invite. They will not get reminder emails. For self-booking, turn on "allow guests" in calendar settings.
- Zoom Scheduler Basic ends 23 Oct 2026. It does not affect CRM automations. If they use Zoom links on calendars they need a paid Zoom plan or Google Meet.

## Prospects, pool and campaigns

- Sales Navigator "invalid URL": they pasted a Sales Navigator link into the LinkedIn search import (or the reverse). Use the Sales Navigator import option. If that option is greyed out, reconnect Sales Navigator in Settings → Integrations.
- Sales Navigator saved search error: ask for the exact URL they used so we can check the filters.
- LinkedIn connection import: it imports what LinkedIn returns, which can be fewer than their total connections. Ask for the numbers if it looks wrong.
- Gmail or Outlook can be connected in Settings → Integrations to send from their own email.
- LinkedIn or email messages missing in Conversations: check Settings → Integrations shows the account connected. If it does, staff need to check.

## BOSS Scorecard

- Where completed scorecards show: Get Clients → Prospects, open the person, BOSS Score.
- Email alert when a prospect completes the scorecard: say it is coming if NOTES confirm it, otherwise say it is logged.
- Nurture email placeholders blank: their CRM profile is missing the value (for example a booking link). Staff can check which field.

## Pricing (non-members)
