# Business Profile — Profit Coach

> What the Profit Coach / BOSS brand is, how it works, and where it's heading. Every AI employee for this brand gets this document.

---

## What This Brand Is

**Profit Coach** (operating the **BOSS** — Business Owner Strategy System) is a diagnostic-led business transformation platform for owner-led businesses doing £1M+ revenue. It diagnoses what's broken, sequences what to fix first, and tracks whether it's working.

- **Stage:** Early / building (Feb 2026). Not yet launched publicly.
- **Business model:** Diagnostic → paid strategy call → 6-month bounded programme
- **Primary market:** UK business owners, expanding to US
- **Relationship to BCA:** BCA trains coaches. Profit Coach serves the business owners those coaches work with. BCA coaches become a distribution channel for BOSS.

**This is not** a course, a mastermind, coaching, or SaaS in the traditional sense. It's a bounded system with a start, a process, and measurable outcomes.

## How We Deliver

- **The diagnostic sells:** By the time an owner has seen their score, funnel multiplier, and priority gaps, they've had a £5,000 strategy consultation for free. The sale is maths, not convincing.
- **Core infrastructure:** BOSS diagnostic platform, scoreboard, 90-day plan builder, AI coach (in development), playbooks
- **No video courses, no modules, no curriculum.** BOSS is the strategist telling them where to aim. They use their own tools to execute.

## Strategic Direction

### The Shift
- BCA continues on steady state (7–10 new clients/month, reduced ad spend, highly exclusive)
- BOSS / Profit Coach becomes the growth focus and potentially the larger business
- BCA coaches become distribution for BOSS — the diagnostic becomes the sales tool coaches never had

### Validation Gate
Can we sell 10 business owners into BOSS at £4,950 before building any additional features beyond the diagnostic? If yes, everything scales. If no, we need to know that before we build.

### Phased Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Month 1):** Polish diagnostic, sell 20 founding members, extract coaching intelligence from Pam
- **Phase 2 (Months 2–4):** AI coach, content engine, 90-day plan, £495 call structure live
- **Phase 3 (Months 5–8):** Coach licensing, board groups with Pam/Mark, community at 200+ users
- **Phase 4 (Months 9+):** Life wheel, team assessments, advanced AI, platform expansion
