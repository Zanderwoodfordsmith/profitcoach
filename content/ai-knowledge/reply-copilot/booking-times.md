# Offering call times

Used by Ava when an interested prospect is ready to talk. Two specific times usually book more calls than a bare calendar link: it is one easy decision instead of a task. The calendar link goes in as the fallback.

## The offer

- Offer exactly **two** times, on **two different days**, ideally one morning and one afternoon.
- Both inside the next **1 to 3 working days**. Close enough that the interest is still warm, far enough that it doesn't feel desperate.
- Never sooner than **3 hours** from now.
- Use the coach's real calendar (ours or GHL, whichever the coach has switched on). If no real free times can be read, send the booking link instead of guessing.
- Name the time zone once ("UK time").
- If neither time works, give the booking link so they can pick.

Shape:

> Great, {first_name}. Would Tuesday at 10:30 or Wednesday at 2pm (UK time) work for a 20-minute call? If neither suits, grab any time here: {link}

## Which times

Show-up rates are best mid-week, mid-morning or early afternoon.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Prefer Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday | Highest show-up |
| Prefer 10:00 to 11:30 and 14:00 to 15:30 | After the morning rush, before end-of-day firefighting |
| Avoid Monday before 12:00 | Owners are planning the week |
| Avoid Friday after 12:00 | Low show-up, people winding down |
| No weekends, nothing before 9:00 or after 17:30, nothing 12:30 to 13:30 | Working hours, no lunch slots |

## After the offer

- They pick a time: Ava alerts the coach to confirm and book it (Ava does not book on her own yet).
- They ask for other times: send the booking link.
- No reply: they move into the Reply interested follow-up campaign.
