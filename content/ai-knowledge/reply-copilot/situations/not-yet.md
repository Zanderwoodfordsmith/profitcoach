# Situation: not yet

They said not yet, maybe later, busy, or "now isn't the time".

## Play

Do not argue timing. Ask what they are actually focused on. That often uncovers a real problem, or tells you to check back later.

If they name a focus, offer a relevant thought or the scorecard as a 3-minute snapshot they can use anyway. If they want space, own the loop: you will check back in a few weeks.

## Example shape

Is it not yet because you've got something in particular you're currently focused on?

## Avoid

"Just bumping this." Guilt. A calendar link.
