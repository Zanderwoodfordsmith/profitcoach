# Situation: quiet

They showed interest, got a resource, or said they would look, then went silent.

## Play

Silence is usually life, not a no. Nudge with something new: did the last message make sense, did the scorecard work, or a similar-owner line. Binary is easy.

If they already had several nudges, leave the door open and say you will check back in a couple of weeks.

## Example shapes

Just checking, did my last message make sense?
Or is {issue} not the focus right now?

or

Did you get a chance to take the scorecard, or did it slip down the list?

## Avoid

"Just circling back." "I hate to bother you." Repeating the original pitch word for word.
