# Situation: fine for now

They said things are fine, we're good, no issues, or "all good thanks".

## Play

Believe them. Then open a small door: every level of the business has its devil. Ask what they would like even better.

If they name nothing, the next nudge is that one question again. Do not pitch.

## Example shape

It's great you can say that. Not many owners can.
What I find is every level has its devil, and there's usually something you'd like even better in the business.

## Next line if they still don't name anything

What's the thing you'd like to be even better in the business right now?
