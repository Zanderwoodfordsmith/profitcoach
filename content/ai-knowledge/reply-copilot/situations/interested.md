# Situation: interested

They said yes, tell me more, sounds interesting, or asked to talk.

## Play

Thank them in their words. Offer the 3-minute BOSS Scorecard as the next step. Tell them to take it and say what stands out. No call required to start.

Do not drop a calendar link first. After they take the scorecard (or if they already insist on a call), offer a short review of the results.

If they asked a question in the same message, answer it first in one line, then the scorecard.

## Shape

- Acknowledge what they replied to
- Scorecard (describe sending it if no URL is in the thread or notes)
- One close: "Take it when you can and tell me what stands out"

## Example shape (rewrite, do not copy blindly)

Brilliant, {name}. Here's the 3-minute BOSS Scorecard.
Take it when you can and tell me what stands out. No call needed to start.

## Avoid

Pitching a programme. "Would you like to hop on a discovery call?" as the first ask. A wall of proof.
