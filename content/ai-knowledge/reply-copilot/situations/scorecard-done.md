# Situation: scorecard done

They completed the BOSS Scorecard. PROSPECT.boss_score is set, or the thread says they finished it.

## Play

Congratulate the first step. The score is a snapshot of where profit, time, or control may be leaking. Offer a 30-minute review of their results. Ask if they want a couple of times. Do not dump a calendar link unless the thread already used one.

If you know the score, you may mention it. Do not diagnose the whole business from the number in the DM.
