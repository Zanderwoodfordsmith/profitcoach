# Situation: no thanks

They declined. Could mean this message, this topic, or never contact me.

## Play

Stay gracious. Clarify which no it is. That one question often turns a brush-off into a real answer, or gives you a clean stop.

If they say never again, stop. Wish them well. Do not keep selling.

## Example shape

Is that no thanks to what I've written (helping you make more profit), no thanks you don't want to work on profit at the moment, or no thanks you don't want to hear from me again?

## Avoid

"Sorry to bother you" guilt. A new pitch in the same breath.
