# Situation: thumbs-up

They only sent a thumbs-up, "ok", "cool", or a like-style reply with no words.

## Play

Do not assume interest. Ask which of two things they meant. Make it easy to answer.

## Example shape

Is the thumbs up that you'd like a conversation, or are you just agreeing with what I wrote?

## Avoid

Launching into the scorecard as if they said yes. A long follow-up.
