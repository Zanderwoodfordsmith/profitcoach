# Situation: objection

They named a blocker: cost, time, already have an accountant/coach, tried this before, team won't buy in.

## Play

1. Acknowledge the concern as valid.
2. Ask what sits under it (one question).
3. Only then offer a relevant proof line if the coach actually has one, or the scorecard as a no-call way to see if there is even a gap.

Do not use "but" after the empathy line. New sentence.

## Example shapes

I hear you. Timing is always tight when {their hesitation}.
What would need to change for this to feel like the right next step?

or

Totally get why {concern} feels like a hurdle.
When you say that, is it more {option A} or {option B}?
