# Situation: question

They asked how it works, what it costs, who it is for, or "send me info".

## Play

Answer the actual question in one or two lines. Then give a next step: the scorecard, or one clarifying question. Do not dump a brochure.

If you do not know price or logistics from coach notes, do not invent them. Say the scorecard shows whether a conversation is even useful.

## Shape

- Direct answer
- One value line (system, not therapy)
- One question or the scorecard
