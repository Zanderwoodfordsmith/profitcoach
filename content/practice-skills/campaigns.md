# Outreach campaigns

Three campaigns go out to owners who have not spoken to the coach yet, or not lately. Each is tailored to the core client and the avatar's pains, using the coach's real proof.

Pam's Starbucks rule, for every message: an owner standing in a queue can answer it in a few seconds. Ask a yes or no, or a choice of two. Never an open question that needs thought, such as "What would you like the business to look like in 12 months?"

## A. Connection campaign (proof-led)
For new owners from a list, 2nd and 3rd degree. The main way coaches get calls. It has its own skill: `connection-campaign.md`, with the note and the follow-ups in `connection-campaign/`.

## B. Messaging campaign (conversation into the BOSS Scorecard)
For owners the coach can message without a connection request: connections who fit the avatar but have never spoken to them, and open InMail. Softer than the connection campaign.
- First message: "Hi {first_name}, are you focused on growing the business at the moment, or more on protecting what you have built?"
- Then an insight tied to their industry: every business has a weakest link and most owners guess which one it is.
- Then offer the 3-minute BOSS Scorecard, which finds the root constraint, not the symptoms.
- Final: leave the link with no pressure.

Open InMail: subject line, greeting, industry and location, two or three emoji-numbered hooks, proof, "I've combined that system with other methods to create The {Industry} PROFIT System", the big problem, the common industry struggle, the crossroads, the result recap, "Interested?"

Cold email can follow the same shape later. It is optional and not written yet.

## C. Reactivation campaign
For people the coach already knows: former colleagues, suppliers, clients from past roles, and connections who replied once and went quiet. Their network already trusts them, so this restarts a relationship. It never opens with a pitch. There is more room than a cold note, so it can sound like them.
- Reconnect: personal, one line of shared history, a question about them. No mention of coaching.
- What I do now (about 5 days later): who they help now, in the buyer's words, the kind of coach they are in plain words, and one real proof point. A hobby or story they named can sit in one line if it fits. Never invent one.
- Soft ask (about 2 weeks later): do they know an owner who would want this, or would they like the BOSS Scorecard for their own business.
Start with the warmest 50. Personalise every first line.

## Red flags to avoid
Not personalised. Generic language any coach could write. Abstract results. Selling coaching ("Would you like to discuss how I can support you"). Wasted words. Flattening every coach into the same tone: match the kind of coach they are (boot camp, hugs, a mix).

Use {first_name}, {company} and {assessment_url} tokens exactly. Anything the coach fills in by hand goes in [square brackets].
