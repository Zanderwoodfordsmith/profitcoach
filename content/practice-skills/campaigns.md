# Outreach campaigns

Two proven sequences. Write both, tailored to the core client and the avatar's pains, using the coach's real proof.

## A. Connector campaign (proof-led)
Connection request (under 275 characters), 10-step checklist:
1. Under 275 characters. 2. "Hi {first_name}," 3. Specific callout: "I see you run a UK {industry} company." 4. A hook as a question: "Are you looking to {main desire}?" 5. "I ask because" 6. "a simple system" 7. Specific proof with numbers and a timeframe. 8. The mechanism, tied to the hook. 9. The owner's language. 10. Interest CTA: "Is this of interest?"

Client proof: "I ask because {Company} used a simple system to {result} in under {timeframe} by {mechanism}."
Career proof: "I ask because during my {X} years at {Company} I used a simple system to {result} in under {timeframe} by {mechanism}."

Follow-ups:
- Message 1 (30 minutes after connecting): repeat the proof, name the big problem, say many businesses in the {industry} industry struggle with it ("They're {main problem} instead of {main desire}"), the PROFIT System removed it, the result, "And I believe with the right system in place, your business can do the same." Close: "Are you looking to {main desire}? Would you be interested in learning more?"
- Message 2 (1 day): short check-in.
- Message 3 (2 days): acknowledge they are busy or jaded by coach messages. "It never hurts to have a conversation."
- Message 4 (4 days): ask about the call.
- Message 5 (2 weeks): final, light urgency, tied to the main desire.

Open InMail variant: greeting, industry and location, two or three emoji-numbered hooks, proof, "I've combined that system with other methods to create The {Industry} PROFIT System", the big problem, the common industry struggle, the crossroads, the result recap, "Interested?"

## B. Conversation starter into the BOSS Scorecard (softer, current)
- After connecting: "Hi {first_name}, great connect. Are you focused on growing the business at the moment, or more on protecting what you have built?"
- Then an insight tied to their industry: every business has a weakest link and most owners guess which one it is.
- Then offer the 3-minute BOSS Scorecard, which finds the root constraint, not the symptoms.
- Final: leave the link with no pressure.

## Red flags to avoid
Not personalised. Generic language any coach could write. Abstract results. Selling coaching ("Would you like to discuss how I can support you"). Wasted words.

Use {first_name}, {company} and {assessment_url} tokens exactly.
