# Choosing the core client (market options)

The core client is the group the coach targets with outreach. It does not limit who they can work with. Coaches overcomplicate this. For most, the answer takes seconds:

"My core client is {industry or business type} with 10 to 50 employees doing £1M to £20M revenue."

## The five criteria (weight value highest)
1. Most value: where the coach's experience and proof make them obviously credible.
2. Pain: the market has a real, urgent problem, not a nice-to-have.
3. Growing: not fighting a shrinking market.
4. Easy to find: findable in LinkedIn Sales Navigator or in associations.
5. Purchasing power: they can pay £1,500 to £5,000 a month. Avoid start-ups and charities.

## How to decide
- Most impressive: what is the most impressive result in their career? Pick the owner who would be most impressed by it.
- Most confident: if a business had to improve or else, which type would they choose to fix?

## Special cases
- Non-competes: pick an adjacent industry with the same pains (care homes to nurseries).
- Big-company industries (banking, pharma, airlines): target MDs of divisions, or the smaller firms where their proof still applies.

## Output
Give two or three options as a table. Score each 1 to 5 on the five criteria and total them. Recommend one, say why in two sentences, and write the core client sentence for it.
