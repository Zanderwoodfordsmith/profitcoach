# Value session and the sales conversation

## The value session (what gets more people on calls)
A free, pitch-free 30-minute session that gives the owner real value. Name it for what it does (Pam's is the Quick Wins Profit Maximiser). Build it around the coach's superpower and the avatar's main pain.

Run sheet (from Pam's Value Session Mastery Workbook):
1. Warm rapport, two to three minutes. "Great to meet you, thanks for making the time. I'd love to understand more about where you're at right now and where you'd like to go." Ask what made them book.
2. Key question 1, the current challenge: "What's the biggest challenge or opportunity you're facing right now that's stopping your business from being where you want it to be?"
3. Key question 2, the desired future: "If we were having this same conversation 12 months from now, what would need to have changed in your business for you to feel genuinely happy with your progress?" This creates the gap.
4. Trust builder 1, the personal driver: "Why is solving this important to you personally, beyond just the numbers?" (family, freedom, pride, legacy).
5. Trust builder 2, past efforts: "What have you tried so far, and how has that worked out?" Respect their journey. Repeat it back.
6. Bridge to value: "Based on what you've told me, would you like me to share a couple of insights and strategies that will make a real difference?"
4. The audit, fifteen minutes. The coach's questions, scored red, amber, green. Coach, do not just ask. Give one or two things they can act on. If there is a lot of red, reframe it as opportunity.
7. The magic question, after the aha moment: "Would you like to see how I help my clients solve exactly this and get results faster?" Wait for the yes.
8. Bridge into the offer: "Based on what you've told me about {their challenge} and your vision for {their outcome}, here's how I typically work with clients in your situation." Three things, tied to their pain, their goal, and their personal driver. Add proof. Then the gentle close: "Does that sound like the kind of support that would make a real difference for you?"
9. Next steps with clarity, not pressure.
10. If not now: no objection handling. "No problem at all. The important thing is you've got clarity on your next steps. If it's useful, I can check in with you in two weeks." Send a short report.

## The launch kit for the value session
Name it for what it does (Pam's: Quick Wins Profit Maximiser). Write, in the coach's voice:
- Launch email: subject line, the frustration, what they get in 30 minutes (three bullets that move away from a pain and towards a gain), what they walk away with, "No fluff. No pitch. No catch.", one real testimonial if the coach has one, and a booking link placeholder.
- Nudge email: short, "just bubbling this back up", a few spots left.
- LinkedIn DM invite: two or three lines, ends "want the link?"
- LinkedIn DM nudge: one line.
- LinkedIn Featured section description: three lines.
- Calendar page description: four lines.
Limited spots (for example five a month) is fine because it is true: a coach only has so many hours.

## The pitch (tailored call script)
- Transition statement, then "For you specifically, here is the game plan."
- Reflect back two or three of their pains in their words.
- A simple three-step system as the bridge from where they are to where they want to be. Pause for questions after each step.
- Results they can expect, with proof.
- How to get started and the price (monthly or six months).
- 80% of the pitch is the same every time. 20% is tailored to this owner.
- Speak in absolutes. Slow down. Sell the outcome, not the features.

## Objections
Find the real objection first. Cover money, time to think, partner, timing and fear with calm, respectful answers that go back to the cost of standing still.
