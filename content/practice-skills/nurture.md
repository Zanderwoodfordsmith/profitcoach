# Ongoing nurture

For connections who are not ready yet. Keep giving until the timing is right for them.

## VIP nurture (top 100 prospects): INSIGHT framework
Introduction (something specific you noticed), Need (an industry challenge), Solution (a quick, useful insight), Interest (brief proof), Gain (the benefit), Help (offer more), Take action (one small next step).

Send 2 to 4 a month, about every two weeks. Always personalise the first line. Match the kind of coach they are (boot camp, hugs, a mix). These messages have room: use a story, book, film or hobby they named on Your Voice as the way into at least one tool. Never invent one. Each message gives away one BCA tool with three emoji-numbered benefits and a link placeholder:
- Profit Maximiser: the table that shows how cutting expenses at a given margin changes profit (at a 10% margin, an 11% cut in expenses doubles profit). Follow up a few days later with the five-step expense review ("Does this help me get or keep customers?").
- Cashflow Forecaster ("Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is king"), then the Cashflow Maximiser (five methods, more than 25 tactics).
- Revenue Growth Accelerator: forecast revenue and keep the team focused on invoicing and sales.
- Time Value Tracker: sort the week into £10, £100 and £1,000 jobs.
- Pairwise Prioritisation: a five-minute tool for too many priorities.

## Nurture rhythm
A light cadence for people who said "not now": value every 2 to 3 weeks (a post, a playbook, a scorecard insight), and a check-in at 90 days.
