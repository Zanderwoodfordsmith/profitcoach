# Pain points

Pain points come from the avatar. They are the raw material for every message, post, newsletter and sales call.

## For each pain, capture
- The pain in one line, in the owner's words.
- What they say out loud (a quote you would hear on a call).
- What it costs them (money, time, family, health, reputation).
- The desire it flips into (the outcome they want instead).
- Which profit lever or pillar fixes it.

## Also write
- What keeps them awake at 3am (three lines, first person).
- Buying triggers: events that make them act now.
- Objections they will raise and the honest answer.
- Main desire hooks: two or three short phrases that finish "Are you looking to ...?" Use them in the connection message. Examples of the right shape: "win more profitable jobs", "take a proper holiday without the phone going", "stop being the bottleneck".

Rank pains by how often owners in this market say them out loud. Put the top three first.
