# The next 90 days (launch plan)

Reverse-engineer from the income goal. £10k a month is about six clients. Clients come from calls, calls from conversations, conversations from campaigns.

## Shape of the plan
- Weeks 1 to 2: foundations locked. Core client and avatar decided, LinkedIn rewritten, value session named, BOSS Scorecard link live, calendar connected.
- Weeks 2 to 4: first campaign live (connector or conversation starter), prospect list of 1,000 or more, replies handled daily.
- Weeks 4 to 8: calls booked every week, value sessions run, first client signed, content four times a month.
- Weeks 8 to 12: second campaign angle tested, price reviewed after 2 to 4 clients, first newsletter issue, referrals asked for.

## Output
A timeline by week, the weekly numbers to hit (connection requests, accepted, replies, calls, clients), the one objective for the quarter, the likely constraint, and three things to do this week.
