# The avatar

The avatar is one specific person inside the core client market. Everything downstream (pain points, messages, profile, content) is written to this person.

## Build it in this order
1. Firmographics: industry, location, revenue, team size, role title.
2. The persona: a first name and an alliterative headline ("Site-Bound Steve: MD of a 30-person scaffolding firm"), age range, background in two or three sentences.
3. A day in their life: a short, present-tense scene at a specific time of day that ends on a realisation.
4. Internal monologue: first person, in their words, at their level of self-awareness. It ends confused, not with coach insight.
5. Dreams, fears, past failures, suspicions (of coaches, consultants, "systems"), and enemies (what they blame).
6. Triggering events: what finally makes them say "enough, I need help".
7. Industry vocabulary: what they call customers, staff, jobs and money.
8. Their quote: first person, outward-facing, ready to act.

## Rules
- Use real industry nouns. If the coach's industry is known, lean on it.
- Fears are emotional. Challenges are structural. Keep them apart.
- Keep each list to three to five items.
- Everything must be consistent with the coach's proof, so the proof lands with this person.
