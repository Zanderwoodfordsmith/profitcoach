# Replies and reply interested

## Principles
- Never sell in a reply. Build interest in the outcome.
- Ask about them. Do not talk about your system first.
- Never assume budget, problems or needs.
- Neutral is not no. Ask one more question.
- Plain words. No coach speak.
- Never make them feel stupid or talked down to. Respect what they have built.

## Common replies and what to send
Write a reply for each: "What is it exactly?", "How much is it?", "Not right now", "We're doing fine", "Who are you?", a neutral one-liner, and "Send me some info". Each reply moves to one next step: a question, the BOSS Scorecard, the value session, or a call on two named days.

Price question early: use the anchor script ("Our top coaching clients invest up to £50,000 a year, but you can start for under £1,000 a month..."). Never quote below the coach's minimum fee; adjust the second number if it is higher.

## Reply interested
When they say yes or show interest, speed wins. Reply within the hour.
- First reply: thank them, name the value session and what they walk away with, and offer two named days inside the coach's prospect call times. If they would rather start alone, give the BOSS Scorecard.
- If they have not booked after 2 days, a short nudge. After 3 more days, one last offer, then move them to nurture.
- Once booked: a confirmation with what to bring, a reminder the day before, and a kind follow-up if they do not show.
