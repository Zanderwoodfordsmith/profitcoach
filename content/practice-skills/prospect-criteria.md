# Prospect criteria (the list)

Turn the core client and avatar into a search the team can build in LinkedIn Sales Navigator.

## Base search (always)
- Connections: 2nd and 3rd degree.
- Location: the coach's country (or state, region).
- Headcount: 11 to 50 by default, add 1 to 10 or 51 to 200 if the market needs it.
- Current job title: Owner, Co-owner, Founder, Co-founder, CEO, Managing Director, Co-Managing Director, Managing Partner, plus any industry title (Principal, Head Architect, Senior Partner).
- Exclude in current title AND current company: coach, coaching, consultant, consulting, consultants, psychologist, recruiter, recruiting, recruitment, recruit. (Keep recruiters if they are the market.)

## Then narrow
- Industry filters and keywords that match the core client, including the words owners use about themselves.
- Signals: posted in last 30 days, changed jobs, company growth.
- Who to leave out: anything in the coach's "will not take" list, and competitors of any non-compete.

## Output
A table of filters (filter, include, exclude), a list of keyword strings to try, a target list size, and three example company types that fit. Add the KPIs to watch: connection acceptance above 30% and replies above 10% of accepted.
