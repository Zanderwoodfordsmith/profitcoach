# Current role (Experience)

Their title and business, one line on who they help and the result, then three or four bullets of what they're called in for, then credentials.

Also suggest core skills and milestones: four skills tied to the problems we message about; three or four career milestones with numbers.

## Good

Peter, after. Title: Founder, Director School

> I help owners of manufacturing businesses grow profit, not just turnover, by fixing the structure, the team and the numbers.
>
> Most owners I meet aren't failing. They're running a good business, working too hard, and the structure hasn't kept up with the growth.
>
> What I do:
> - Build a leadership team that looks forward, not back
> - Get the business ready for equity, a sale or the next stage
> - Sort out shareholder and director fallouts
> - Turn round businesses close to the edge
>
> Chartered Director and Chartered Surveyor.

Why it works: a plain title, who and the result in the first line, the owner let off the hook, then what he's called in for. Credentials last.

## Past roles

Each past role backs up the profile's claims. Write it for an owner checking whether this person has done it before, not as a job description.
- Two to four short lines: what the organisation was (size, what it did), what they ran, then one or two results with a number and a timeframe from this coach's record.
- Lead with the result where there is one ("Took the Foundation from a £53,000 loss to a £37,000 profit in 12 months").
- Board, trustee and volunteer roles: one line on what the organisation does and what they bring to the board.
- Early roles: one line, unless they hold a result an owner would care about.
- No pronoun, the same in every role: "Led the community team", never "I led".
- Only facts from this coach's record. If the record holds no result for this role, write what the organisation does and what they ran (from the title), then one line in [square brackets] asking for it, e.g. "[Add a result from this role: what changed, with a number and a timeframe]". Never fill the gap with claims that sound right ("grew the reach", "built the structure", "kept standards consistent").
- Everyday words, no list of duties.

## Bad

Peter, before. Title: Founder Coachsultant

> www.directorschool.co.uk
> I run Director School, which is basically what it sounds like. I work with directors and boards to help them get sharper at how they lead, make decisions, and avoid the mistakes that quietly sink businesses.
> I've spent over forty years building and advising across property, social enterprise, housing, manufacturing, research, and retail — you name it — and a lot of what I teach comes from what I've seen go wrong firsthand, not just what looks good in a textbook.
> From star-ups to big corporates, I know from the coal face how they work. You'll see how it applies to your business - what you need and what you can ignore. Each business is unique so don't follow the crowd.

Why it fails: a made-up title nobody searches for. Opens on a link that doesn't work in Experience. All about him, no who, no result, no number.
