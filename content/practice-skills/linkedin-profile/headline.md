# Headline

Under 220 characters: a simple promise plus proof. Who they help, the result, a number. Never a job title alone. Write five, score them (specific, curious, "this is for me"), give the best three and say which you recommend.

## Formulas (write options with different angles)
- Transformation + audience: "Helping {avatar} {outcome}, {method or differentiator}"
- Outcome + constraint removed: "{Outcome} for {avatar}, without {thing they hate}"
- Authority + specificity: "{Credibility marker} | {what you do for whom}"
- Pain-point lead: "{Pain}? I help {avatar} fix that."
- Method name: "Creator of {method} | Helping {avatar} {outcome}"
Avoid vague labels (consultant, strategist), generic outcomes (driving growth), and buzzwords (passionate, results-driven).

## Good

> Helping UK manufacturing owners grow profit, not just turnover | Touchscreen maker: £3m to £16m in 4 years | Chartered Director

Peter, after. Who (UK manufacturing owners), the result in their words (profit, not just turnover), one real result with a number and a timeframe, one credential last.

## Bad

> Founder Coach of Director School | Increase Profit & Effectiveness | Business Strategist for Profit

Peter, before. Opens on a job title nobody searches for. "Increase profit & effectiveness" could be anyone. No who, no number, "profit" twice.

> Business Coach | Helping leaders transform

Coach-speak. Nothing an owner can picture.
