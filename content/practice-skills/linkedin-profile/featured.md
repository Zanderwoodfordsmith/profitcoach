# Featured

Three items, in this order. A visitor first asks "who is this?", then "how does it work?", then "what do I do next?".

1. **Their story** (a post they publish, then pin). Either a short timeline of the businesses they ran or fixed, the hard years and what changed, each line with an age or year and a number; or "3 lessons from {years} in business", with one lesson being that every business has one thing holding it back, and fixing that first. Written for an owner, not a CV: no list of job titles. If their story is thin, use the lessons. It ends with one line pointing to the free BOSS Score. Needs a strong image or text card: on a phone only the first item and a half show.
2. **How it works**: what working with them looks like, from first call to result, as an explainer post or document. Once they have a client result, a proof post takes this slot.
3. **The BOSS Score** as a link item: `https://www.theprofitcoach.com/score/{slug}?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=featured`, captioned with the result it gives ("13 quick questions, shows your level").

Later their newsletter replaces slot 2. Discreet coaches: newsletter first, then their story, then the BOSS Score.

## Story post shapes

Timeline (each line short, one number where there is one):
> {age or year}: {what they started or left}.
> {age or year}: {the hard moment}.
> {age or year}: {what changed, with a number}.
> ...then two or three lines on what it taught them, then the BOSS Score line.

Lessons:
> {years} years running and fixing businesses. 3 lessons that can save you time:
> #1 ...
> #2 Every business has one thing holding it back. Find it and fix that first.
> #3 ...

These show the shape only. Use the coach's own story and numbers.

## Bad
- A "book a call" link first. Too big an ask before they know who you are, and never for a discreet coach.
- The company website first. It's about the firm, not why to trust the person.
- An old article or award with no link to the owner's problem.
- The BOSS Score with no caption saying what they get.
