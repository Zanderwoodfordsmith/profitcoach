# Campaign types and volume

## The four kinds (CROP)

| Kind | Who it is for | Usual shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Connection** (the connector campaign) | New people from a list: 2nd and 3rd degree owners | Connection request with a short note, then four or five follow-ups over two weeks. The main way coaches get calls. |
| **Reactivation** | People who connected or replied before but went quiet | One to three short messages that restart the conversation |
| **Ongoing nurture** | Connections who are not ready yet | A message every week or two that gives value (a tool, an insight, the BOSS Scorecard) |
| **Positive replies** | People who said yes or showed interest | Quick, personal replies that move them to a value session or the scorecard |

Published templates for each kind live in the Campaign library (admin). `list_campaign_templates` shows the ones available.

Every coach also gets starter campaigns (Connector, Nurture, Reply interested, and email follow-ups for the scorecard). They start as drafts.

## Volume

Three levels, from the account down:

1. **Account sending** (Campaigns page, account sending): connection requests per week for the LinkedIn account. Recommended 100 a week when the LinkedIn SSI score is under 70, up to 200 when it is 70 or more. This is the ceiling for everything.
2. **Campaign priority**: low, medium or high. A high-priority campaign gets a bigger share of the account's daily invites and runs first.
3. **Campaign daily limits**: invites and messages per day for this campaign (default 20 each).

"Turn it up" usually means: priority one step higher, or daily limits up by about half, as long as the account total allows it. Go gradually. LinkedIn notices sudden jumps.
