# Lead sources a coach can do more of

When the constraint is volume (not enough people), this is the menu. Six ways to get more people interested. The first four are things the coach does themselves. The last two are other people bringing them clients. They are adapted from Hormozi's lead frameworks, minus the ones a one-person practice can't use (staff, agencies, an affiliate scheme).

Start with what already works for them, then add one. Don't suggest starting everything at once.

| Source | What it is | What good looks like (Scoreboard) | Where in the app |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm outreach** | People they already know: past colleagues, old clients, their LinkedIn connections. A personal note asking who they know, not a pitch. | Far better than cold. Count it as inbound: about 5 in 10 reply with interest or a name. | `find-prospects` (their 1st-degree connections), then `campaigns` |
| **Cold outreach** | Owners who don't know them: LinkedIn connection campaigns, email, phone. The default engine. | About 10 interested in 1,000 (1%). The best notes get 20 (2%). | `find-prospects`, `campaigns`, `campaign-messaging` |
| **Content** | LinkedIn posts and the newsletter, so owners come to them. Slow to start, then it adds up. | Not tracked yet. It makes cold and warm outreach answer more, because people have seen them. | `linkedin-marketing` |
| **Paid ads** | Paying to put an offer in front of owners. | Not BCA's default. Only once cold outreach turns people into calls, and only with money they can lose. | None in the app. Hand to BCA if they want help. |
| **Referrals from clients** | Asking happy clients who else they know, at a good moment (a result, a review). Weeks 8 to 12 of the plan. | The best leads there are. About 5 in 10 become interested, so a few a month matter a lot. | None to send them. Add them as a lead source on the Scoreboard. |
| **Partners** | People who already have the owners' trust: accountants, bookkeepers, solicitors, bank managers, heads of trade associations and local business groups. One good partner can send several owners a year. | Count them like referrals. Start with two or three conversations, and offer them something useful for their clients (a talk, the BOSS Score). | None yet. Add them as a lead source on the Scoreboard. |

## How to use it

- **Cold first, if the numbers say volume.** Most coaches who are short of clients aren't reaching enough people, and many expect LinkedIn to book calls from a few hundred invites. Show them the sums: at 1%, 400 people a month is 4 interested.
- **Referrals and partners for the next step.** 1,000 people to 10 interested is fine for cold. It is far too few for referrals or a partner: those should run at about half. If a referral source is giving 1 in 10, the ask or the timing is wrong, not the volume.
- **One more source at a time.** Pick the one that fits what they already do well. A coach who already posts should post more, not start ads.
