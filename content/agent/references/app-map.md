# App map (Get Calls and Win Clients)

Coach pages start with `/coach`. Admin pages show the coach the admin is viewing as. Labels below are the ones on screen. The sidebar has **Get Calls** (finding people and starting conversations) and **Win Clients** (from the booked call to a yes).

| Page key | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| `campaigns` | Get Calls → **Campaigns** tab | Every campaign with its Off/On switch, people and progress. **Create campaign** (top right), **Settings** for account sending. |
| `campaign` | Click a campaign's name | The **Off/On** switch next to the name. Tabs: **Overview** (people by status, **Add prospects**), **Prospects** (the people), **Steps** (the sequence). **Settings** (top right): priority and daily limits. |
| `pool` | Get Calls → **Prospects** tab | Everyone imported (it used to be called the Pool). Each list is a tab along the top (**All** first). **Import** (top right). Tick people, or the box at the top of the column for the whole page. Each person also has **+ Add** in the Campaign column. |
| `magnets` | Get Calls → **Lead Magnets** tab | The tools and pages campaigns send people to |
| `conversations` | Get Calls → **Conversations** | The LinkedIn inbox with replies from campaigns |
| `calls` | Win Clients → **Calls** | Booked calls, with the recording and transcript once a call is done |
| `prospects` | Win Clients → **Pipeline** | The pipeline (not the Get Calls → Prospects tab, which is everyone imported): people who replied or showed interest, with their stage |
| `content` | Get Calls → **Content** | LinkedIn posts and scheduling |
| `links` | Get Calls → **Toolkit** (the Links tab) | Share links: scorecard, booking page, lead magnets |
| `blueprint` | **Practice** (Blueprint) | The coach's Practice Blueprint: avatar, pain points, campaign messaging, LinkedIn newsletter |
| `call-practice` | Win Clients → **Call Practice** | Practise a sales call out loud with an AI owner, then get a score and a report |
| `first-campaign` | **First Campaign** | The guided setup for a first campaign: ICP, avatar, messages, starter list |

## How-tos

- **Import from Sales Navigator**: Prospects → **Import** → **Sales Navigator**. Paste a search URL or build one from filters, pick how many people. It lands on a new list (a new tab in Prospects).
- **Google Maps**: Prospects → **Import** → **Google Maps**. Country, then state (US) or city, then what to search for and how many.
- **Google Search**: Prospects → **Import** → **Google Search**. Same place and size as Maps, from Google results rather than map listings.
- **Other imports**: Prospects → **Import** → **1st degree** (their connections), **Upload CSV**, **Add one person**, **Custom URL**.
- **Add a list to a campaign**: open the campaign → **Add prospects** → choose the list. Or: Prospects → click the list's tab → tick the box at the top of the column → add them to the campaign.
- **Remove, pause, resume or move people**: open the campaign → **Prospects** tab → tick them → **Remove**, **Pause**, **Resume** or **Move**.
- **Change the messages**: open the campaign → **Steps** tab.
- **Priority and daily limits**: open the campaign → **Settings** (top right).
- **Turn a campaign on**: the **Off/On** switch next to its name (or on the Campaigns list). It needs a connected LinkedIn account, steps and people.
- **Connect LinkedIn**: Get Calls → Campaigns → **Connect LinkedIn** (top right, next to Create campaign; it shows until LinkedIn is connected). Once connected, that spot shows **Settings** for account sending.
