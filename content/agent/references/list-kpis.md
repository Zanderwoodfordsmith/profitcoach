# Prospect list KPIs

From the classroom lesson "Evaluating prospect list KPIs" (Get Calls).

## Size
Every automated test is an A/B split: two variants of 300 people, so 600 per test. Under 300 per variant, the results are not reliable.

| List size | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Under 600 | Poor. 500 can just work (250 per variant). Look for more people soon. |
| 600 to 1,199 | Okay. Enough for the first test, which takes about three weeks with the ramp-up. |
| 1,200 to 1,799 | Great. Six to seven weeks of outreach. |
| 1,800 and up | Even better. Several tests without hunting for more people. |

A list of 2,000 businesses can run six months of prospecting, because people who do not connect the first time can be tried again later.

## Quality
Open ten profiles from the list. 8 out of 10 good matches (right kind of company, an owner or director) is the bar. 70 to 80 percent is okay; below 70, tidy the list by hand or tighten the search. If a filter would cut the list to a tenth for a small quality gain, keep the bigger list and remove the bad ones by hand.
