# Profit System pitch: the core you learn

Same system. Their problem. Their roadmap. Their result.

## The five facts (before you pitch)

1. **Priority** — what they most want help with (the Golden Thread)
2. **Pain** — what is happening because it is not solved
3. **Desired result** — what they want instead
4. **Number** — revenue, profit, customers, hours, margin, team size, if they have one
5. **Personal why** — why it matters beyond the business. They may want systems so they can go away for two weeks without the phone ringing. That is what you are selling.

## Tests (use on every draft)

- **Three-link rule.** Every step: because you told me / what we will do / which means.
- **So-what.** After each step, keep going until you reach the result they actually want.
- **Could I say this to anyone?** If yes, it is still too generic.
- **AI guardrail.** Only their facts, numbers, language and examples. Never invent.

Core rule: never explain a step without answering "How does this help THIS prospect solve THEIR problem?"

## The system (never changes)

**Vision — where are we going?**
From "I've got loads going on" to "I know what we are building and what happens next."

**Velocity — how do we increase the speed of profitable growth?**
Profit, cash, revenue, marketing, sales, products, operations. Not growth at any cost.

**Value — how do we build value beyond the owner's personal effort?**
The business becomes more measurable, manageable, scalable and less dependent on them. They can grow it, keep it, step away, put management in, or sell it.

### The nine steps

**Vision**
1. Alignment Accelerator — where are we now, what is in the way, what needs fixing first
2. Detailed Strategy — where we are going, what the business must look like
3. Dynamic Planning — 90-day priorities: what, who, when, how we will know

**Velocity**
4. Profit and Cash — activity and revenue become actual profit and cash
5. Revenue and Marketing — attract, convert and keep enough of the right customers
6. Operations and Product — deliver what it sells, efficiently and consistently

**Value**
7. Financials and KPIs — the numbers that show what is working
8. Innovation and Management — it keeps improving without everything coming back to them
9. Team and Leadership — capability, accountability and leadership, so it does not depend on one person

## The core script (learn this)

Say it in your own words. Keep the order, the check, the pillar names, the step names, and the close. The bracketed lines are what you swap for this person.

**Opening**

Okay, [name], based on everything you have shared, the main thing you want to achieve is [desired outcome].

At the moment [main problem] is getting in the way, and you have said that is causing [impact]. What you really want instead is [desired future]. And for you personally, that would mean [personal benefit].

Have I understood that correctly?

PAUSE.

Perfect. I want to show you how we would use the Profit System to help you achieve that. There are three pillars. Vision, Velocity and Value. Inside those is a nine-step roadmap. Rather than a generic overview, I will show you how every part helps you solve [main problem] and achieve [desired outcome]. Sound good?

**Vision**

Step 1, Alignment Accelerator. The first thing we do is establish exactly where you are now and what is really causing [main problem], because the obvious problem is not always the real problem. We look at what is working, what is not, where the biggest blockage sits, and which issue will make the greatest difference. For you we would look at [their examples]. That means you are not guessing. You know what needs fixing first to reach [desired outcome].

Step 2, Detailed Strategy. Then we get clear on where you are going. We build the longer-term strategy around [desired outcome]. That means deciding [their strategic choices]. Instead of reacting to whatever lands on your desk, we are designing the business required for [desired future].

Step 3, Dynamic Planning. Then we turn that into action. Your 90-day plan is built around improving [main problem]. What needs doing, who owns it, when it happens, what success looks like. You do not leave with another list of ideas. You know what the next 90 days must do.

**Velocity**

Step 4, Profit and Cash. Solving this still has to create a healthier business. We look at how [main problem] is hitting profit, margin, cash, costs, capacity and lost opportunity. There is little point fixing [main problem] if you do not have more profit and cash to show for it.

Step 5, Revenue and Marketing. This connects to [main problem] because [their link]. We look at the right customers, the revenue engine, sales activity, conversion and repeat business. The aim is the revenue needed for [desired outcome].

Step 6, Operations and Product. The business has to deliver what it sells, efficiently and consistently. For you that is [their delivery, capacity or product issue]. We improve the process so [desired outcome] is sustainable, not a new problem somewhere else.

**Value**

Step 7, Financials and KPIs. We pick the drivers behind [desired outcome]. You know which numbers say we are improving, where we are slipping, and where to focus. For you those might include [their measures]. That is control over the problem you asked me to solve.

Step 8, Innovation and Management. We do not fix it once. We put management in so the business keeps improving. For you that means [their management link], so [main problem] does not come back in six months.

Step 9, Team and Leadership. [Desired outcome] cannot depend entirely on you. For you that means [their team link]. Responsibility, accountability, decision-making, leadership, performance. The team helps create the result, rather than everything landing back on your desk.

**Bringing it together**

So, [name], we are not treating [main problem] as one isolated issue. We get clear on where you are going, we create the plan, we improve the commercial engine, we make sure the operation can support it, we put the numbers in, we strengthen management, and we develop the team. The short-term result is [short-term outcome]. Longer term you are building a business that [long-term outcome], which gives you [personal benefit].

How do you feel about the structure and approach specifically?

## What they should memorise

These lines do not change. Practise them until they are automatic.

- The opening check and the pause
- Vision, Velocity, Value, one line each
- The nine step names, in order
- Because you told me / what we will do / which means
- "How do you feel about the structure and approach specifically?"

The rest is filled from the five facts on the day.
