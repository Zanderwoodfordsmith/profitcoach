# Profit System pitch: the core you learn

Same system. Their problem. Their roadmap. Their result.

## The five facts (before you pitch)

1. **Priority** — what they most want help with (the Golden Thread)
2. **Pain** — what is happening because it is not solved
3. **Desired result** — what they want instead
4. **Number** — revenue, profit, customers, hours, margin, team size, if they have one
5. **Personal why** — why it matters beyond the business. They may want systems so they can go away for two weeks without the phone ringing. That is what you are selling.

## Tests (use on every draft)

- **Three-link rule.** Every step carries the logic: because you told me / what we will do / which means. It is a test, not a phrase. Vary how each step opens ("You said...", "This is where...", "For you...") and never start every step with "because you told me".
- **So-what.** After each step, keep going until you reach the result they actually want.
- **Could I say this to anyone?** If yes, it is still too generic.
- **AI guardrail.** Only their facts, numbers, language and examples. Never invent.

Core rule: never explain a step without answering "How does this help THIS prospect solve THEIR problem?"

## The system (never changes)

**Vision — where are we going?**
From "I've got loads going on" to "I know what we are building and what happens next."

**Velocity — how do we increase the speed of profitable growth?**
Profit, cash, revenue, marketing, sales, products, operations. Not growth at any cost.

**Value — how do we build value beyond the owner's personal effort?**
The business becomes more measurable, manageable, scalable and less dependent on them. They can grow it, keep it, step away, put management in, or sell it.

### The nine steps

**Vision**
1. Aligned Vision — where they are now, where they want to get to, and what the business is for: goals, purpose, everyone pulling the same way
2. Defined Strategy — how they get there: the ideal customer, the core offer, the business model, how it grows
3. Disciplined Planning — 90-day priorities (what, who, when, how we will know), with a rhythm of meetings and reviews so it gets done

**Velocity**
4. Profit & Cash Flow — pricing, costs and cash flow, so activity and revenue become actual profit and cash
5. Revenue & Marketing — positioning, winning customers, follow-up and conversion: enough of the right customers
6. Operations & Delivery — deliver what it sells well and consistently, so customers stay and buy again

**Value**
7. Financials & Metrics — clean books, the KPIs and a dashboard that show what is working
8. Infrastructure & Systems — processes, systems and automation, so the work runs the same way every time without coming back to them
9. Team & Leadership — the right people, performance, leaders and culture, so it does not depend on one person

## The core script (learn this)

Say it in your own words. Keep the order, the check, the pillar names, the step names, and the close. The bracketed lines are what you swap for this person.

**Opening**

Okay, [name], based on everything you have shared, the main thing you want to achieve is [desired outcome].

At the moment [main problem] is getting in the way, and you have said that is causing [impact]. What you really want instead is [desired future]. And for you personally, that would mean [personal benefit].

Have I understood that correctly?

PAUSE.

Perfect. I want to show you how we would use the Profit System to help you achieve that. There are three pillars. Vision, Velocity and Value. Inside those is a nine-step roadmap. Rather than a generic overview, I will show you how every part helps you solve [main problem] and achieve [desired outcome]. Sound good?

**Vision**

Step 1, Aligned Vision. The first thing we do is establish exactly where you are now, where you want to get to, and what is really causing [main problem], because the obvious problem is not always the real problem. We agree what [desired outcome] actually looks like and why it matters to you, so everything after this points the same way. For you we would look at [their examples]. That means you are not guessing. You know what needs fixing first to reach [desired outcome].

Step 2, Defined Strategy. Then we decide how you get there. Who your best customers are, what you sell them, and how the business makes its money and grows, all built around [desired outcome]. For you that means deciding [their strategic choices]. Instead of reacting to whatever lands on your desk, we are designing the business required for [desired future].

Step 3, Disciplined Planning. Then we turn that into action. Your 90-day plan is built around improving [main problem]. What needs doing, who owns it, when it happens, what success looks like. And we set a rhythm of short reviews so it actually gets done. You do not leave with another list of ideas. You know what the next 90 days must do.

**Velocity**

Step 4, Profit and Cash Flow. Solving this still has to create a healthier business. We look at how [main problem] is hitting your pricing, margin, costs and cash flow. There is little point fixing [main problem] if you do not have more profit and cash to show for it.

Step 5, Revenue and Marketing. This connects to [main problem] because [their link]. We look at how you are positioned, how you win customers, how you follow up and how many you convert. The aim is the revenue needed for [desired outcome].

Step 6, Operations and Delivery. The business has to deliver what it sells, well and consistently, so customers stay and come back. For you that is [their delivery, capacity or customer issue]. We improve how the work gets delivered so [desired outcome] is sustainable, not a new problem somewhere else.

**Value**

Step 7, Financials and Metrics. We get the books clean and pick the few numbers behind [desired outcome], on a simple dashboard. You know which numbers say we are improving, where we are slipping, and where to focus. For you those might include [their measures]. That is control over the problem you asked me to solve.

Step 8, Infrastructure and Systems. We do not fix it once. We put the processes and systems in, and automate what we can, so the work runs the same way every time without coming back to you. For you that means [their systems link], so [main problem] does not come back in six months.

Step 9, Team and Leadership. [Desired outcome] cannot depend entirely on you. For you that means [their team link]. Responsibility, accountability, decision-making, leadership, performance. The team helps create the result, rather than everything landing back on your desk.

**Bringing it together**

So, [name], we are not treating [main problem] as one isolated issue. We get clear on where you are going, we create the plan, we improve the commercial engine, we make sure the operation can support it, we put the numbers in, we build the systems, and we develop the team. The short-term result is [short-term outcome]. Longer term you are building a business that [long-term outcome], which gives you [personal benefit].

Then stop, and ask (the call script has it as the first line of After the pitch, so it is only asked once):

How do you feel about the structure and approach specifically?

## What they should memorise

These lines do not change. Practise them until they are automatic.

- The opening check and the pause
- Vision, Velocity, Value, one line each
- The nine step names, in order
- Because you told me / what we will do / which means
- "How do you feel about the structure and approach specifically?"

The rest is filled from the five facts on the day.
