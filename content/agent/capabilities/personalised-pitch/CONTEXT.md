# Personalised pitch

## What this is
Two jobs, same skill.

1. **Learn the core.** The opening check, the three pillars, the nine step names, the three-link rhythm, and the close. These stay the same every call so they can practise.
2. **Tailor this prospect.** After a discovery or value session, rewrite the "for you" line in every step so the same system sounds like this person's solution.

The framework never changes. The story running through it does. In the pitch the first pillar is Vision (where we are going), then Velocity, then Value. Do not call the first pillar Control.

## Before you write
You need the five facts from this prospect, in their words:

1. Priority (the Golden Thread)
2. Pain (what it is costing them)
3. Desired result
4. A number if they have one (revenue, profit, hours, customers, margin)
5. Personal why

Ask for what is missing. One or two questions, not a form. If they only want to practise, say so and use one of the five example threads. Label that draft **practice**, not a real pitch.

`read_section` on `sales:script` if they already have a learnable script. `get_coach_brain` for their voice, avatar and real proof. Use their proof in the close only when it is in the record.

## The process

**Learn / practise**
Give them the core script from the reference, spoken, short. Then a drill: they pick a Golden Thread, you show three random steps, they have to answer "how does that help MY problem?" If they stall, the connection is not strong enough.

**Tailor this prospect**
1. State the Golden Thread in one line.
2. Write the full spoken pitch, all nine steps, using the core they already know. Every step uses the three-link rule: because you told me / what we will do / which means.
3. Under the pitch, two short lists: **Same every call** (the parts to keep practising) and **Changed for [name]** (the one line that made each step theirs).
4. Run the two tests on your own draft. If a paragraph could be said to a different prospect, rewrite that step.
5. End the pitch with: "How do you feel about the structure and approach specifically?"

## Facts
Prospect facts come only from what they pasted or said. Never invent a number, example, customer, or motivation. If a step needs a detail they did not give, put a square-bracket prompt, do not guess.

Coach proof comes only from the Blueprint. If there is no real result, leave it out.

## What NOT to do
- Do not give a tour of the Profit System. Every step must answer: how does this help THIS person solve THEIR problem?
- Do not skip a step because it feels less obvious. Think harder and make the connection.
- Do not replace their learnable core with a brand-new script each time. Adapt it.
- Do not overwrite `sales:script` from here. That page is the script they practise. This draft is for this call. If they want the core saved on the Blueprint, say so and open `improve-section`.
- No jargon, no hype, no em dashes. Spoken English they can say aloud.
