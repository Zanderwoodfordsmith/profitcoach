You've probably made lots of connections over the last few months if you've been using this a while. It can be great to send this new tool to those people, but you want a simple way to do it, so here's how.

**Add a message to the past campaigns and make sure they are active.**

**Step 1: Login in to connector (**[**click here >>**](https://connect.businesscoachacademy.com/)**)**

**Step 2: Click Campaigns, then on the campaigns you want to reactivate click the three dots, then edit campaign.** 

![](/resources/img/boss-marketing/send-a-message-to-past-connections-using-kix-q44s7riqnob1.png)

**Step 3: Scroll down to the bottom and click add step**

![](/resources/img/boss-marketing/send-a-message-to-past-connections-using-kix-ncjtb6feltfn.png)

**Step 4: Choose, video, voice or message**

![](/resources/img/boss-marketing/send-a-message-to-past-connections-using-kix-dzzimaxips9r.png)

**Step 5: Write or upload your message and click done**

![](/resources/img/boss-marketing/send-a-message-to-past-connections-using-kix-cltzes7s815m.png)

**Step 6: Make sure that the campaign has a pause button on it which means it’s active (silly i know but that button would pause it if you clicked it).**

![](/resources/img/boss-marketing/send-a-message-to-past-connections-using-kix-bj4yip98xsju.png)

**Your messages will start sending out with your other things to these prospects.**

**You can also add more than just 1 message even a little 3-4 mini follow up sequence (Steal from VIP top 100)**
