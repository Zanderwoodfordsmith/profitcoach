The BOSS Scorecard is a brilliant lead magnet campaign because it gives you something simple to promote, valuable for the business owner, and easy to convert into a 30-minute review.

The key is this:

Do not just promote the Scorecard once. Promote the *problems it diagnoses* for 13 weeks.

Then repeat each quarter.

It gives you 13 different angles instead of repeating “take my assessment” every week. Much stronger. 🔥

## 13-Week Marketing Plan

### Promoting the BOSS Scorecard Assessment

#### Campaign Objective

To use the 3-minute BOSS Scorecard Assessment as a lead magnet to start conversations with business owners, generate scorecard completions, and book 30-minute BOSS Score Reviews.

The scorecard should be promoted as a simple, practical way for business owners to understand:

- Where their business is working well
- Where things could work even better
- Where the business may be too reliant on them
- What to focus on first over the next 90 days
- How to create more profit, more control and more freedom
The aim is to move prospects from:

LinkedIn content → Scorecard → 30-minute review/Deeper coaching conversation → Client

## Core Campaign Message

Most business owners do not have an effort problem.

They are already working hard.

The real issue is that the business may be relying too heavily on them across decisions, team, systems, sales, marketing, operations and performance.

The BOSS Scorecard gives them a quick score out of 100 and shows where the business could work even better.

## Campaign Assets Needed

Create or adapt the following assets:

- LinkedIn profile update
- Featured section link to the BOSS Scorecard
- VIP 100 direct message campaign
- Weekly LinkedIn newsletter
- Weekly LinkedIn posts
- Weekly email to their list
- Follow-up message for people who complete the Scorecard
- Booking page for the 30-minute BOSS Score Review
- Simple tracking sheet

## Weekly Marketing Rhythm

Each week, complete the following:

### LinkedIn Activity

- Send 50 VIP 100 messages
- Post 3 times per week
- Comment on 10 ideal client posts per day
- Follow up with anyone who engages
- Invite relevant prospects to take the Scorecard

### Email Activity

- Send 1 weekly email to existing contacts
- Resend to non-openers 2-3 days later with a different subject line

### Newsletter Activity

- Publish 1 LinkedIn newsletter per week
- Include the BOSS Scorecard link
- Invite readers to book a 30-minute BOSS Score Review after completing it

### Review Activity

- Review Scorecard completions weekly
- Follow up personally
- Invite suitable prospects to the 30-minute review

## Campaign Summary

The BOSS Scorecard is not just a lead magnet.

It is the first step in a coaching conversation.

It gives the business owner a reason to reflect, a score to be curious about, and a natural next step into a 30-minute review.

The strongest campaign message is:

You are already working hard. Now let’s make sure the business is giving you enough back.

Take the 3-minute BOSS Scorecard and find out what your business scores out of 100.
