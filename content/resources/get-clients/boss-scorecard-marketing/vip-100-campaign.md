## Who Should Be on the VIP 100 List?

Identify 100 warm or relevant prospects, including:

- Existing LinkedIn connections
- Past clients
- Previous enquiries
- Referral partners
- Business owners they know locally
- People who have engaged with their content
- People in their target sector
- People who fit the ideal client profile
This is not a cold spam list.

It is a focused relationship list.

Make sure to add personalisation to the first line so they know they are not being spammed.
