## How to Use These Playbooks

These are simple steps to follow when people reply to you on LinkedIn.

Read each step carefully.

Do them in order.

Remember: Speed matters. Call the same day they reply.

## Playbook 1: When Someone Shows Interest

**Use this when:**

- They reply saying they're interested
- They answer your question about their business
- They tell you what they want to improve

### Same Day Actions (Day 0)

1. **Call them immediately.** Don't wait. Do it now. Get their number from their profile. Go their website and try and find.
1. If they don't answer, leave a voicemail.
1. Send this LinkedIn message right away:
*Hi [Name],*

*I saw your message about [their specific response]. I called a couple of times and left you a message.*

*This is something I help my clients with regularly. Would you be opposed to a short call?*

1. Try calling again later that same day.

#### Alternative Softer Message

Use this if you want to be gentler:

*Hi [Name],*

*I saw your message about [their specific thing]. Is it just something specific at the moment, or is it how it always is?*

*This helps them open up more.*

## The Follow-Up Sequence

If they don't respond or answer, follow this exact schedule:

### Day 1

- Call them again
- Send another LinkedIn message. Personalised based on their profile or company website.

### Day 2

- Call them again
- Send another LinkedIn message

### Day 3

- Send just one message (no call)

### Day 4

- Call them one more time
- Then wait 5-7 days

### After 5-7 Days

- Call one final time
- Send a final message
- Use the message below

#### Final Message Template

*Hi [Name],*

*Rather than continue the answerphone ping pong, do let me know when's the best time to get hold of you, and I'll do my best to accommodate.*

**Important:** After this, move them into long-term nurture, stop following up personal until they reply again. Move on to other prospects.

## Playbook 2: Different Types of Responses

Use these exact responses for each situation:

### When They Say: "Not yet" or "Maybe later"

*Is it not yet because you've got something in particular that you're currently focused on?*

### When They Give a Thumbs Up 👍

*Is the thumbs up that you would like to have a call with me/interested, or is it a thumbs up you're just agreeing with my comments?*

### When They Say: "Fine for now" or “We’re good now”

*It's great that you can say that. Not many business owners can.*

*What I find is that every level has its devil, and usually there's something that you'd like to be even better in the business.*

**Important:** Leave it open-ended. Don't ask a direct question. Let them think and respond.

They will usually write back with something they want to improve.

#### Follow-Up After They Respond

When they tell you what they want to improve, send:

*If they don’t quite answer, you can answer what they say and add…*

*What's the thing you'd like to be even better in your business right now?*

### When They Say: "No thanks"

**Your response:**

*Is that no thanks to what I've written (like helping you make more profit)? Or no thanks you don't want to make more profit at the moment? Or no thanks you don't want to hear from me ever again?*

**Why this works:** It makes them clarify what they really mean. Often they'll soften their response.

## Key Principles to Remember

1. **Speed is everything.** Call the same day they respond.
1. **Always make it easy for them.** Ask questions they can answer quickly.
1. **Reference their specific words.** If they said 'time,' mention 'time.' If they said 'flexibility,' mention 'flexibility.'
1. **Be persistent but polite.** Follow the schedule. Don't give up too early.
1. **Leave things open-ended.** Let them think and respond naturally.
1. **Know when to stop.** After the final message, move on.
