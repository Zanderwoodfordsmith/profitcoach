### Lead Metrics

- Scorecard clicks
- Scorecard completions
- Review calls booked

- Sales conversations started
- Clients enrolled

### Conversion Metrics

- Scorecard completion to review booking rate
- Review booking to attended call rate
- Review call to sales conversation rate
- Sales conversation to client rate

- 100 LinkedIn comments per week

## Important Messaging Guidance

Avoid saying:

- Your business is broken
- You are doing it wrong
- You are leaving money on the table
- You have failed to build systems
- Your team is the problem
Instead, say:

- Things could work even better
- The business may be relying too much on you
- You may not be getting enough to show for the effort
- The team may need clearer systems, standards and accountability
- There may be a higher-leverage priority to focus on first
