## Before you start

Even though the coaching sessions are numbered at the start this does not mean you have to stick to them exactly like this. It’s much better to get everything done properly even if things drag over into other sessions. The only caveat to that would be, try to make at least the 2nd session about the thing that they feel is most crucial to get sorted in the business.

## What Is This

Structured outline for your 1st business coaching session to run with a client Objective

Address initial issues/opportunities and completing the Profit-Fun Matrix & Dashboard.

## Why Is It Important

- It’s vital to provide maximum value immediately
- Helps position the long term coaching engagement and
- identify areas requiring focus and attention.

## Session Structure:

1. Introduction (5m)
1. Ideal vs Current Business Status Overview (10m)
1. Overview Profit System (5m)
1. Deep Dive into Dashboard (1h)
1. Creating a Plan of Action (10m)

## 1. Introduction (5m)

*Start with a brief introduction to each other.*

- Greeting and Rapport Building: Welcome the client warmly.
- Give a quick introduction about yourself. *Keep your own intro about yourself around 2-3 minutes.*
- Session Overview: Briefly outline what will be covered in the session to set clear expectations. These will be:
- Client's Goals for the session
- Questions about the client
- Questions about the business & Completing Profit-Fun Matrix
- Completing the Dashboard
- Introduce the coaching sheet

**Questions to Ask:**

1. Tell me a bit about yourself: where you grew up, your family, what led you to starting your own business and how you have found that?
1. What specifically do you want to cover and what specific outcomes do you want to achieve from this first session?

## 2. Ideal vs Current Business Status Overview (10m)

- Start with the end in mind using the Profit-fun matrix (PFM) to assess where they currently are on the 1-5 scale for profit and fun.
- Business Performance Overview: Ask them to give an overview of the current state of the business, including financials, team performance, and key challenges.
- Wins and Challenges: Identify recent successes and ongoing challenges.

**Questions to Ask while going through Profit-Fun matrix:**

**Current Business**

- On Scale of 1-5 how profitable is your business right now?
- On Scale of 1-5 how fun is your business right now?
   - What has been your biggest win in the past quarter?
   - What challenges are you facing right now?
   - How is your team performing, and are there any specific team-related issues?
   - Do you know your key business metrics currently, and how they compare to your goals?

**Ideal Business**

*Put these answers in level 5 on profit-fun growth Matrix*

- How much profit does your ideal business generate?
- With what margins?
- So that would be (Calculate revenue and add it in (Profit/margin). Revenue is that about right. Check if they are happy and if not adjust.

If they are just saying “well as much as possible”, ask them how much in the next 3 years.

 - What does your ideal business look like in terms of fun?
- How many hours are you working per week if you are working at all?
- What are you focused on?
- How does the business run?

**You don’t need to fill out all levels of the PFM but get at least level 5 done.**

## 3. Profit System Overview (5m)

- Explain that the way to help bridge that gap between where they are now and their ideal is through using the profit system so just going to take 5 minutes to share it so they have absolute clarity.
- Go through 5 levels (relate it back to the levels in the PFM), 3 Pillars & 9 steps (you can learn this in other parts of the system). Present it in your style.

Question to Ask

- After each framework ask a question that checks their clarity of understanding  “does that make sense?, “Does that resonate?”.

After Presenting the 5 levels ask:

- Which level do you feel you are at currently? (have a brief discussion)
- Ok and why do you feel that’s the case?

After presenting the 3 pillars of straight onto the 9 steps then ask

- Which areas do you feel you may need the most focus? (have a brief discussion)

**- Are there any areas in particular you feel we should address immediately in the next session? (Most important question of the whole session - you have to find out their issues)**

If they have an answer great, if they don’t have an answer it makes it an even simpler transition to the Business growth Matrix.

## 3. Deep Dive into Business Growth Matrix (45m)

   - Explain the purpose of the Business Growth Matrix.
- Order the questions by level of business owner.
   - Ask questions and discuss responses in the matrix and traffic light accordingly.
   - Dig deeper into the issues, including the key issue whilst going through this.
   - Prioritise the issues and ensure they put the agreed actions into their diary.

**Questions to Ask About Each of the Issues and the Key Specific Issue:**

   - What do you think has caused this particular challenge/issue?
   - Have you faced similar issues in the past? How were they resolved?
   - What impact is this issue having on you, your business and your team?
   - What resources or support do you think you need to address this?
   - Do you have these resources available to you?
   - Is there anything stopping you from resolving this?

Don’t worry if you don’t get through the whole thing and keep reassuring your client of the same.

## 4. Creating a Plan of Action (10m)

- Goal Setting: Establish short-term and long-term goals.
- This is a very fast high level overview to assure them that in an upcoming session you will dive in deeper, you just want them to start making progress before the next session.
   - Action Steps: Outline specific actions that need to be taken to achieve these goals.
   - Responsibilities and Timeline: Assign responsibilities and set a timeline for each action item.

**Questions to Ask:**

   - What are the top (max 3) goals you want to achieve in the next 3 months?
   - What specific steps do you need to take to achieve these goals?
   - Who on your team can help with these actions, and what are their roles?
   - What is a realistic timeline for each action item?
- What can be done before the next session (make a note and remember to keep them accountable to it next session, this will set a great impression.)
