### What Is This?

This session is about guiding your client to create a focused and actionable 90-day business plan. This plan turns their long-term vision into specific projects and goals that they can work on in the immediate future.

### Why Is It Important/Useful?

**Continuous Progress:** Regularly updating helps clients progress towards their business vision.

**Focused Execution:** Helps clients focus on key areas that need attention.

**Ownership and Clarity:** Clients create and own their plan, giving them clarity and commitment.

**Resource Management:** Helps prioritize tasks and manage time effectively.

**Motivation and Energy:** A clear plan energizes clients and keeps them motivated.

### Session structure

1. Explain the 90-Day Planning Concept (5m)
2. Define 3 Key Projects (10m)
3. Set Success Metrics (5m)
4. Develop Project Phases or Milestones: (15m)
5. List the Actions (30m)
6. Prioritise, Assign, Schedule and Success (10m)
7. Set Accountability Measures (5m)

## How to Deliver This Session

#### Pro Tip: Use Proper Project Management Software

This can be done in proper project management software like clickup, Asana, Monday if they have it. They do not need to use the excel sheet. Proper software would be better as it will have more functionality to store notes, date reminders, assign people, and have the team comment on tasks.

#### Pro Tip: 1st 90 Day Plan Timeframe

You will more often than not be starting a client when the 90 day plan doesn’t align to the business quarter or the client calendar quarter. Either extend or shorten to get on to a nice quarterly rhythm. Ideally don’t go below 60 days or above 120 days.

#### 1. Explain the 90-Day Planning Concept (5m)

Clarify that this is not about their ultimate long-term outcomes but specific, actionable projects for the next 90 days that you will help them stay accountable to. Also so they can keep their team accountable too.

Reinforce the importance of this plan as a tool for transformation. By consistently focusing on these 90-day cycles, your clients will see significant progress in their business. Encourage them to take ownership and stay committed. This approach not only drives results but also strengthens your coaching relationship through demonstrated value and accountability.

#### 2. Define 3 Key Projects (10m)

Work with your client to name the mini projects they want to focus on in the next 90 days. For instance, "Product Launch," "Marketing Revamp," or "Team Training Program." 3 is ideal, 4 is manageable, 5 is usually absolute maximum.

### For Each Project:

#### 3. Set Success Metrics (5m)

For each project, help your client define what success looks like in 90 days. For example, "Increase sales by 20%" or "Launch the new website."

#### 4. Develop Project Phases or Milestones: (15m)

Break down each project into either:

- **Sub-projects** - where multiple independent projects need to be done to achieve success
- **Phases** - where a sequential series of steps needs to be taken.
- **Milestones** - breaking down a much larger goal into mini achievements.

### Example Business Project Names and Goals:

- **Project Name:** "Customer Acquisition Drive"
  - **Success Metric:** Gain 50 new clients.
  - **Sub-Project:** Launch a targeted ad campaign by April 1st.
- **Project Name:** "Operational Efficiency Boost"
  - **Success Metric:** Reduce operational costs by 10%.
  - **Sub-Project:** Implement new CRM software by March 20th.
- **Project Name:** "Team Skill Enhancement"
  - **Success Metric:** Complete training for all team members.
  - **Sub-Project:** Conduct four training sessions by end of the quarter.

#### 5. List the Actions (30m)

For each sub-project, work with your client to identify what actions need to be completed for it to be achieved. Encourage them to think about resources needed and potential obstacles. Encourage them to eliminate non-essential activities to focus on these priorities.

#### 6. Prioritise, Assign, Schedule and Success (10m)

Help them PASS the tasks onto their team:

1. Set a date for each action
1. Assign who is responsible
1. Is any clarification of success required

#### 7. Set Accountability Measures (2m)

**Regular Check-Ins:** Establish regular sessions to review progress and adjust plans as necessary. Stress the importance of accountability. You want to review their progress every session.

**Avoid Distractions:** Encourage them to be ruthless about their time and energy, focusing solely on the plan and avoiding distractions or unnecessary new initiatives.

### Homework: Power Plan (5m)

Get your client to do a Power plan. You can introduce this as:

*Last session we got a long-term plan in place for your business, this session we got the immediate plan of what needs to happen this quarter, and what the plan of how that translates into your and the teams day to day actions. Now we need to make sure your time is available to actually execute on that so for that we use what’s called the Power Plan. And it’s something I’d like you to work on before the next session….(dive in).*
