## What Is This?

This session is designed to help your client make significant progress on a specific issue or seize an opportunity identified in the previous session using the dashboard assessment or another critical area they have highlighted. The focus is on empowering the client to find their own solutions, while you provide the tools and frameworks to facilitate this process.

## Why Is It Important/Useful?

- **Empowerment:** Encourages clients to develop their own solutions, enhancing commitment and ownership.
- **Flexibility:** Adapts to the client's most pressing needs, ensuring relevance and impact.
- Skill Development: Enhances your coaching abilities by using various methods to address different situations.
- **Immediate Progress:** Aims to create a sense of achievement and momentum by tackling the biggest constraint.

## Pro Tip for Coaches

The key to this session is flexibility and empowerment. Use your judgment to select the most appropriate method or tool to address the client's issue. Encourage self-reflection and responsibility, fostering a transformative coaching relationship. This approach not only resolves current issues but also empowers clients to tackle future challenges independently.

## Session Structure

1. Opening the Session (10m)
1. Deep Dive into Key Issue(s) or Leverage Points (10m)
1. Flexible Problem-Solving (45 minutes)
1. Creating a Plan of Action (15 minutes)
1. Personal Goals Homework + Finish  (10m)

### 1. Opening the Session (10m)

Begin with a check-in on any tracking or KPIs discussed previously. Use questions like, "How are you getting on with your tracking?" or "Is the 13-week cash flow up to date?" to gauge progress and set the tone.

### 2. Deep Dive into Key Issue(s) or Leverage points (10m)

**Identify Core Problems:** Focus on the primary issue or opportunity. Use questions to explore the client's understanding, like "What has improved so far?" and "What still needs fixing?"

**Review Past Insights:** Recap what was discussed previously about this issue and its impact on their business.

**Question to ask:**

- What’s stopping this business from doubling or being 10x?
- How long has it been like that?
- What have you tried to fix it?

### 3. Flexible Problem-Solving (45 minutes)

- **Choose a Method:** Depending on the issue, guide the client using one of the following frameworks:
  - **COACH Method:** For creating a structured plan of action.
  - **ISSUE Framework:** To identify and address specific obstacles.
  - **Profit System Tools:** Introduce a relevant tool that can help address their issue, such as financial dashboards or operational efficiency techniques.
  - **Four Quadrants:** Targeting Blindspots when struggling to understand why not succeeding
  - **More-Better-New** - framework to think about decision making of action
  - Any other framework and tools you have to help solve this!
- **Facilitate Progress:** Ensure the chosen method helps the client feel they've made substantial progress on their biggest constraint (or opportunity).
**Ask questions like:**

- What specific steps do you need to take to overcome this?
- Who can support you in this effort?
Do NOT get stuck into understanding the problem unless you have to. Focus on solution orientated “How” questions. ### 4. Creating a Plan of Action (15m)

- **Set Clear Goals:** Help the client establish clear, achievable goals related to the issue or opportunity.
- **Outline Action Steps:** Using the insights gained, guide them to outline specific actions needed to achieve these goals.
- **Assign Responsibilities and Timeline:** Work with the client to assign responsibilities and set realistic timelines for each action item. Encourage them to add these to their calendar.

### 5. Personal Goals Homework (10m)

- **Set Personal Goals Homework:** Ask the client to reflect on their personal goals and consider what achieving these will cost them in terms of time, effort, and resources.
- **Link to Future Sessions:** Explain how understanding personal goals will aid in aligning business objectives in the next session.
