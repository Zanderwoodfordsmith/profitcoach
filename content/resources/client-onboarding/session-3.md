### What Is This?

In this session you will guide your client in creating a three-year business plan, focusing on key business areas. This will help them align their business goals with their personal lifestyle aspirations.

### Why Is It Important/Useful?

**Alignment:** Ensures the business supports the client's desired personal lifestyle.

**Clarity and Focus:** Provides a clear roadmap for long-term business growth.

**Strategic Planning:** Helps identify necessary business structures and KPIs.

**Enhanced Decision-Making:** Gives clients the tools to make informed decisions.

**Foundation for Action:** Sets the stage for actionable short-term plans.

### Session Structure

1. Start with Personal Goals (10m)
1. Connect Personal and Business Goals (5m)
1. Start Working On The 3 Year Orbit (45m)
1. Establish Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) (15m)
1. Finalize the 3-Year Orbit Plan (homework) (5m)

## How to Deliver This Session:

#### 1. Start with Personal Goals (10m)

Begin by discussing where the client wants to be personally in 3-5 years. This ensures their business goals align with their lifestyle desires. Can look at 1 Page Growth Plan or Lifestyle calculator if they have done them.

#### 2. Connect Personal and Business Goals (5m)

Transition to identifying what the business needs to achieve to support these lifestyle goals. Discuss aspects like income, work hours, and flexibility.

#### 3. Start Working On The 3 Year Orbit (45m)

Describe how the orbit model works, focusing on six key segments: Profit & Cash, Revenue & Marketing, Ops & Product, Financials & KPIs, Innovation & Management, Team & Leadership. You can choose which version you use or even sticky notes on a wall.

**3.1 Complete 3 year segments for each area (30m)**

Work with the client to establish clear, strategic goals for each of the six segments. Encourage them to think about what success looks like in three years for each area.

**3.2 Detail the 2-Year and 1-Year Goals for One Segment: (10m)**

Choose one segment to dive deeper into, establishing 2-year and 1-year goals. This will help the client understand how to break down long-term goals into shorter, actionable steps. You don’t have to do all areas but if there is time you can do more, however they can work on this before the next session.

**3.3 Discuss Necessary Business Structures (5m)**

Explore what business structures and strategies are needed to achieve these goals. Discuss team size, operational changes, and leadership roles that might be required. You are picking out the key things that need to start changing in order to make this vision a reality.

#### 4. Establish Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) (15m)

Identify the KPIs that will be crucial for measuring progress. Explain the importance of tracking these metrics to manage and control business growth effectively.

This does not mean you will have them measure every KPI they think of immediately. However it’s good to have that list for the future.

**Select the 3-4 most critical ones to start tracking immediately and review these at the start of every future session.**

### 5.  Finalize the 3-Year Orbit Plan (homework) (5m)

Work with the client to ensure all segments are aligned and realistic. Validate that the growth in one area supports the growth in others, and adjust as necessary.

#### Next Session Focus

Explain that the next session will focus on turning this long-term plan into actionable steps for the next 90 days. Encourage the client to reflect on what was covered and start thinking about immediate actions/priorities.

By the end of this 90-minute session, your client should have:

1. A clear understanding of their three-year business goals
1. At least one segment fully detailed with 2-year and 1-year goals.
1. A list of possible KPIs to track
1. Identified 3-4 key KPIs and established ways and frequency to begin tracking
1. Clarity on how to complete the other sections ideally before the next session

This sets a strong foundation for creating a focused and actionable 90-day plan in the following session.
