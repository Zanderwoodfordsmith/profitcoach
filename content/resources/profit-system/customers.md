## Purpose of this Module

To help the client create a clear understanding of their customers, the customer segmentation and the power of a target market

## Expected Duration

90 minutes

## Format

Presentation and workshop

## What you should know before you start

The value of focusing on a target market.

Customer segmentation into multiple target markets

## Your Inventory (material included in the module folder)

- Article – customer lifetime value
- KB – Target Marketing
## Your Preparation

Make sure you are familiar with the concepts of segmentation and target markets

Have all the materials readily accessible and a method of delivering the appropriate materials to the client.

## Your Session Guide

Review the client’s coaching sheet from the previous session

Review any outstanding work from a previous session

Run the Customer PPT presentation with the presentation script. The topics covered are:

Customer ABC segmentation

Target market

Customer Journey

Customer service

At each topic change in the presentation there is a note to pause the presentation and discuss the topic with your client and a short Q&A session

Assign the tasks to be completed for the next session

Complete the next coaching sheet
