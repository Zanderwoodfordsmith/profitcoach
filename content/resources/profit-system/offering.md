## Purpose of this Module

To help the client find the best way to represent their products (offerings) and their offers and to understand the difference.

## Expected Duration

3 hours

## Format

Presentation of a slide deck and some workshops

## What you should know before you start

- The definitions of offering and offer.
- The difference between features and benefits
- Distribution of products across customer segmentation.

## Your Inventory (material included in the module folder)

- PowerPoint presentation to the client
- Script for the presentation
- Handouts for the client
- Workshop with the client
- Industry examples
- Client work assignment (after the session)
- Coaching Worksheet blank – not in this folder

## Your Preparation

- Review the pptx presentation.
- Make sure you are familiar with the handouts, decide which you want to use and when to use them.
- Plan the workshop activity
- Have all the materials readily accessible and a method of delivering the appropriate materials to the client.

## Your Session Guide

1. Review the client’s coaching sheet from the previous session.
1. Review any outstanding work from a previous session.
1. Show the PowerPoint presentation that explains the various elements included in the module. The script is provided to go with each slide. Some of the slides are animated.
1. Show some industry examples of the topic elements and have a short Q & A session with the client on the elements.
1. Assess the client’s current status for each of the elements.
1. Provide the worksheet and run a workshop with the client on the following:
   - Product list
   - Feature list for one or more selected products
   - Conversion of features to benefits for the selected products.
1. Stress that this is to get them started and you don’t expect them to produce the final result as it may take some time and possibly the participation of other team members.
1. Review the work done.
1. Assign the task to complete
1. Complete the next coaching sheet
