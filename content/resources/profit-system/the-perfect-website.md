Business owners often rely on designers to create their website and while it may look nice it doesn’t do what it needs to…sell.

### Mistakes:

- Too much insider language
- Too many words in the header
- The CTA buttons are passive
- The CTAs are not repeated down the page
- The images used do not relate to the product or support what you are saying.
- Language is cute and clever but not clear
- Site does not promote a lead generator
- You are using a slider so images change too fast and frustrated potential customers
- Site tells your story instead of inviting clients into a story
- Your site is too complicated. It needs a single purpose - to create sales

## 9 Elements of a perfect Website

![](/resources/img/perfect-website/the-perfect-website-builder-step-by-step-kix-t4367lv5sp04.png)

### Header

You only get one chance to make a first impression. You have only a few seconds to peak user curiosity (not to be cute).

Don’t use the header to differentiate, clarity will differentiate.

### Does it pass the grunt test?

- What do you offer
- What is the physical tangible thing you sell. Financial advisors offer a “path to a better future” but that could be a gym, church. What is it?
- How will it make his life better?
- What is the one main way in which it will make your target customers life better after they buy
- What does he need to do to buy it?

Show a button with a clear call to action.

- Buy now
- schedule a call
- shop now

Don't worry about feeling pushy. An unclear CTA shows you don’t believe in your product. Don’t use “learn more”, “find out about us”

![](/resources/img/perfect-website/the-perfect-website-builder-step-by-step-kix-1i1zaxqqyobm.png)

### Header Examples

Good examples:

- Injury Lawyers committed to helping you get your life back
- Great managers aren’t born, they are trained, see how we do it.
- Transform your health, regain your life. A proven drug free path to healing for all your health concerns.
- Surprise and delight your guests with handcrafted desserts

Notice they are all simple and say exactly what the business offers. It’s more important to be clear, than to be cute.

### Stakes

This is the failure section. What is the cost of not doing business with you?

By helping people realise this it increases the value of offer.

What pain are you helping people avoid:

- Wasting time
- missed opportunities
- lost business
- embarrassment
- loss of sleep, frustration
- losing to competition.
- weight gain
- confusion
- lack of access
- lack of status
- not reaching potential
- etc…

You want negative stakes but don’t over do it.

Prospects brains are only willing to go so far before it’s just depressing so contrast with happy ending

**Examples:**

- No more sleepless nights tossing and turning on a mattress that doesn’t work for you
- Most people don’t realise how much time they are wasting in their email inbox everyday, we have a solution
- We meet people all the time who are wasting their money because they don’t know how to invest it
- Are you tired of paying for marketing that doesn’t get results

### Value proposition

A good story loves contrast. After you have agitated the problem now build the perceived value of your solution.

Tell the customer everything they get, can they: save money, save time, reduce risk, are they getting quality

Be specific and where possible be visual.

Ideally include a headline which sign posts this section is where they can see the benefits of the product or service.

- Our customers no longer struggle with…
- You don’t have to be confused anymore
- The stakes are high, act now and avoid the hassles.
- Our heart breaks when we see people struggle with…

---

### Guide

Now that they know you have a solution it’s time to show them you are the best option.

There are two aspects to this:

1. Empathy- Show you understand their challenges/obstacles
1. Authority- showing your ability to solve their problems

To do this you can mention their primary pain point. “We understand how difficult it is to struggle with…” or “We know what it feels like to…”

Then put in the thing they want e.g. “be overlooked for a promotion”, “spend time away from your family”

![](/resources/img/perfect-website/the-perfect-website-builder-step-by-step-kix-ncqbfugpkbup.png)

Some people feel uneasy with this section but there is no point having a great looking website that doesn’t result in sales.

You can demonstrate authority by showing awards, a list of logos, Number of clients you have worked, years experience, testimonials.

You don’t want to overdo the authority. Need to find the right balance between just enough empathy and authority.

When using testimonials to build authority many businesses make the testimonials too long.

Highlight where a customer:

- Solved a big problem
- Achieved an amazing result
- Overcame an objection

### 5. Plan

You must make it easy & simple for customers to buy from you.

Pave a path for the customer and they will follow it. The plan shows customers what they need to do, to do business with you.

If a customer is confused about what to do they will simply leave  your website.

It may be obvious to you, but it might not be to your customer.

**Ideally you want 3 simple steps**, Don’t go past 4. Here’s an example:

1. Tell us about your event.
1. Let us create a custom menu.
1. Host the party of your dreams.

### 6. Explanatory paragraph

This is where SEO comes from. Your explanatory paragraph is going to do the following:

- Identify who your customer wants to become.
- Identify what they want.
- Define the problem setting them back.
- Position you as their guide.
- Share a plan they can use to solve their problem (which
- includes your product).
- Call them to action.

It is a Mental map for your prospect and casts a vision for their lives by using your product.

### 7. Video

The video is just a more dynamic, engaging way to share the other parts of the website previously mentioned.

Keep it short, usually below 3 minutes. If it’s interesting it could be 5 or more but don’t go too long. Trim it down.

If it’s a longer video (webinar, detailed product demo) then give that away in exchange for an email. They will watch more because they have “Invested" in the process.

Give the video a catchy title that makes people want to watch it. Put the title above the play button E.g. Here’s How We’re Different

Make sure to hook the viewer quickly with a problem and then go on to how you can solve it for them. Otherwise they will click away.

---

### 8. Price

This is the section where you spell out cost and price points.

Show the different products you have available and the benefits to why a prospect should choose one over another.

Ideally each of your avatars has a clear product they should choose.

You want to have a certain product or membership that is your target you are using psychological principles to direct people towards.

This is also where you want to contrast the price against the cost of not buying the product.

The pricing structure is covered in detail in another video.

---

### 9. Junk Drawer

Possibly the most important section of your website. Why?

- Put EVERYTHING else you previously thought was important in here, as links in the footer
- Many websites feature too many options at the top of the page.
- Put as many of these options as possible at the bottom of the website in the footer or “junk drawer”.
- You don’t want lots of links competing with your primary call to action that should be repeated down the page.
- It also causes decision fatigue for the prospects to decide what to click and AND what not to click on.
