## 6 easy steps to success with the Revenue Growth Accelerator

1. Input your monthly target order and sales (invoiced) values in cells F14:F25 and M14:M25 on the Monthly tab.
1. Input your prior year values for all months, and actual values for the preceding months, into the same two tables on the Monthly tab.
1. Input your annual revenue target into cell C3 on the Monthly tab.
1. Input order and invoiced values for the current month tab in rows 8 and 10. A value must be input into each line even if it is zero.
1. Assign ownership for the delivery of the order number and invoiced number to two separate members of your management team. This is critical!
1. Ensure that the daily order and invoiced values are updated daily and the variances in column AB reviewed, with action taken where necessary.

Please delete any bank holidays (or non-trading days) as necessary for your business.
