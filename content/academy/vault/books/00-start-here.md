This library gives you the core ideas from about 150 of the best books on mindset, focus, leadership, sales and running a business, plus a few talks worth your time. Each lesson takes about five minutes.

## How each lesson works

- **The video** gives you the book's core message in a few minutes.
- **The big idea** sums up the book in a sentence or two.
- **Key ideas** pull out the parts worth remembering.
- **Use it with clients** shows how the idea helps the owners you work with.
- **Try it yourself** gives you one thing to do this week.

## Where to start

Pick the section that matches what you, or a client, are stuck on right now.

- **Mindset & Purpose** for confidence, setbacks and direction.
- **Energy & Wellbeing** for sleep, stress, mornings and calm.
- **Habits** for building routines that stick.
- **Time & Priorities** for deciding what matters and dropping the rest.
- **Focus & Performance** for deep work and doing your best under pressure.
- **Thinking & Decisions** for making better calls with less stress.
- **Learning & Mastery** for getting good at new skills fast.
- **Coaching & Leadership** for leading people and running teams.
- **Sales & Influence** for conversations, persuasion and closing.
- **Business & Money** for strategy, growth and profit.

## Should you read the whole book?

Use these lessons to choose. When an idea lands, buy the book and read it properly. The best ideas get better the deeper you go.

The videos come from Nathan Lozeron's Productivity Game channel.
