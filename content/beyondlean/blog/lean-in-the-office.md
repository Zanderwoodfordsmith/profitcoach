Lean Office is the use of Lean or Six Sigma methods in an office or service environment.

Many people have said in the past, about an office or service environment, "This is not a car plant. You can't use Lean manufacturing tools here."

How wrong they were.

Lean in an office, or Lean Service, is spectacularly successful, often more so than in a manufacturing environment. The tools and method are the same, but how you apply them is tailored to the specific sector and business. It is best guided by experienced practitioners, not left to internal people who have read the latest "Lean" book.

Here are some results from recent projects in office and service environments:

- Sales conversion up 81% in a media telesales business.
- Right first time quality up 120% in media telesales.
- Productivity up 161% in UK bank divisions.
- Headcount reductions of 20 to 40% in all recent Lean Service projects.

## Tools to use in an office

Some of the tools to use:

- Standardised work
- Process mapping
- User guides
- Capacity planning
- 5S
- Line balancing
- Heijunka (levelling the workload)
- Visual management
- Work organisation

But most of all: basic management. Many companies aren't very good at the real basics of management, and the most worrying part is that they don't even know they aren't very good.

A solid training plan for the management team at all levels will bring startling results once they begin to apply what they have learned.
