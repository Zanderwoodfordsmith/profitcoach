## Earn what you deserve

A question I get asked again and again by the internal improvement consultants and client team members I have mentored is, "How can I earn more?" More often, it's "I wish I earned as much as you."

My answer doesn't always go down that well, because it is very direct, but it is very true: you will always earn what you deserve to earn, not what you want to earn.

So in answer to the question "How can I earn more?", the simple answer is: deserve more. By this I mean get better at what you do, become better educated in your subject, and learn the skills you need to get paid more. It is hard work and takes effort, but the rewards are a by-product of the work you put in.

## Cause and effect

Let me explain this in language improvement people will understand: cause and effect. For every effect there must be a cause. If your income is the effect, then the actions you take, the knowledge you have and how you use it are the cause.

In short, you are the cause. So to change the effect (your income), you need to change the cause (you). Get better at what you do, and the effects of that will be the benefits you reap.

## The farmer's law

The farmer understands the law of cause and effect better than most. He knows that the harder he works up front, ploughing and planting and keeping the pests away, the better the harvest he will get and the more he'll get paid. The work has to be done before the rewards can be reaped.

That is the law of cause and effect. Most people get it backwards and expect the benefits before they put in the work. Unfortunately for them, the universe doesn't work that way.

So if you want to earn more, learn from the farmer, who's been following this simple law for thousands of years. Get better at what you do. Deliver your projects on time, and deliver benefits from them over and above what was expected. Learn from those who've walked the road before you. A good mentor is worth their weight in gold.

Most importantly, take responsibility for your own life and career, because nobody else can put the work in for you, just as you wouldn't ask anyone else to do your breathing for you. Start now, put the work in now, and you'll reap the rewards.

It's your choice, but always remember: you'll always earn what you deserve.
