A couple of common myths I've read over the last 10 years or so happen to involve pieces of work we have delivered quite recently. Here they are:

1. In a contact centre, you can't improve productivity without hurting customer service.
2. Lean, or operational excellence, won't work for a field force, because the teams are spread out geographically (so it's hard to use visual management, daily performance meetings and so on).

We have developed proven methods for both of these challenging environments. They deliver the kind of benefits you would normally see in a manufacturing, back office or transactional environment, by tailoring the operational excellence (Lean Six Sigma) approach to each environment.

This lets the principles of Lean and Six Sigma be applied in a flexible, fluid way, based exactly on the needs of the business.

## In the contact centre

For example, the contact centre approach has now been used in over 12 different call centre environments, from inbound and outbound sales, to customer services, to blended front and back office departments. Each was slightly different, depending on the needs and focus of that business. All delivered more than a 20% uplift in productivity and a similar uplift in quality, while giving customers a much more consistent service and a better experience.

## In the field

Similarly, in our recent field operations engagement, the approach was heavily tailored to the environment while keeping the basic principles in place. Its first results would make even the biggest critic call for it to be rolled out straight away.

Of course, the first step in deciding exactly what the approach needs to be is always the operational diagnostic.
