SMED (single minute exchange of dies) and quick changeover are different terms for very similar things: reducing set-up times.

## Definitions

- **Set-up:** the work needed to convert a process from running one part or operation to running another.
- **External set-up:** work done while the machine is running good parts.
- **Internal set-up:** work done while the machine is not running good parts.
- **Set-up time:** the time from the last good unit of one production run to the first good unit of the next production run.

## Where is most of the time spent?

Standard set-up activities:

- exchanging dies or tools: 10%
- positioning dies or tools: 20%
- making adjustments: 45%
- gathering the items needed: 25%.

## Five steps to quick changeover

1. Observe the current process.
2. Separate internal and external set-up.
3. Convert internal set-up into external.
4. Streamline internal operations.
5. Streamline external operations.

### Step 1: Observe the current process

List the process elements. For example:

- start the machine and wait on the machine cycle
- remove and gauge the part, and place it in a bin
- get new parts, place them in the fixture and start the machine.

### Step 2: Separate internal and external set-up

- Stage all items before shutting down the machine.
- Develop a checklist of the items needed for the changeover.
- Store high-use items at the machine.
- Use die charts and tool boards.
- Use visual management, such as an "on deck circle" or a scheduling board.
- Place tools and parts in order of use.
- Check all items to make sure they fit and work properly.
- Sharpen and preset tools.
- Clean fixtures and check they are free of defects.
- Clean up and return removed tools after the first good part.

### Step 3: Convert internal set-up to external

Standardise operations to keep internal adjustments to a minimum, for example:

- die pressures
- the size and shape of dies, tools and jigs
- bolts, hoses and handles
- height and stroke: die height, machine level, and handling and storage level.

Prepare the operating conditions before the changeover:

- preheat, preset, pre-cut or pre-adjust
- dress grinding wheels offline
- use intermediary fixtures or jigs
- preset tools
- position dies
- replace as few parts as possible
- position dies or tooling for quick insertion into the machine.

### Step 4: Improve internal operations

Eliminate the time lost removing and installing bolts:

- replace bolts with functional clamps.

Eliminate all adjustments:

- graduated scales: standardised settings
- calibrated values: setting gauges or blocks
- measurement devices: dead stop locations.

Analyse the motion elements:

- move both hands at the same time
- keep two-handed motions brief and small
- make the most of hands and forearms for light work
- avoid swift changes in direction
- keep an appropriate centre of gravity.

### Step 5: Improve external operations

Avoid time lost looking for, or checking, the right items:

- colour coding
- number coding
- proper arrangement and orderliness
- carts kept just for changeovers
- material flow racks
- go/no-go gauges.

Simplify adjustments:

- dedicated set-up equipment and carts.

Keep collecting ideas to improve set-up. And remember that all improvements begin and end with 5S.
