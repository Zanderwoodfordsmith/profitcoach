## What is total productive maintenance?

A system of maintenance covering the entire life of the equipment and all of the people involved.

- It is a tried and tested way of eliminating waste, saving money and making factories better places to work.
- It gives operators the knowledge and confidence to manage their own machines.
- It is a long-term process to increase skills, raise efficiency and achieve zero losses.

![The pillars of TPM](/beyondlean/blog/total-productive-maintenance/tpm-pillars.jpg)

## What are the benefits of TPM?

TPM refuses to accept that machines inevitably fail.

- It reduces the total life-cycle cost of equipment.
- It reminds us that every time a machine fails, something could have been done to prevent it.
- Gradually, the operators become maintainers.
- Gradually, the maintainers become improvers.
- It develops the five senses, as well as technical skills.

## What is effective maintenance?

- **Planned maintenance:** "The scheduling of maintenance activities on a time or usage basis, regardless of the current performance levels of the equipment."
- **Preventive maintenance:** "A systematic approach to eliminate catastrophic failures and breakdowns through regular care and attention, early diagnosis and rectification."
- **Predictive maintenance:** "A systematic approach using history and equipment condition monitoring to predict and prevent catastrophic failures and breakdowns."
- **Breakdown maintenance:** "When all else fails, making sure equipment is available again in the shortest possible time, while eliminating root cause faults with sustainable repairs."
- **Effective equipment design:** "A systematic approach to equipment design which maximises equipment availability, minimises life-cycle costs and eliminates waste."

## What is autonomous maintenance?

An operator skills development programme. It:

- allows problems to be identified and solved quickly
- stops the accelerated deterioration of plant and equipment
- stops deterioration-related failures
- stabilises equipment conditions (standards)
- develops training materials on how to run, operate and maintain equipment.

## What is overall equipment effectiveness (OEE)?

OEE is a total measure of performance that relates the availability of a process to its productivity and quality.

- OEE is the key performance indicator (KPI) for TPM.
- OEE measures the impact of a change on a process, caused by removing process or equipment losses.
- A world-class company should achieve 85% OEE on key equipment.

## What is quick changeover, or SMED?

Quick changeover is a method of analysing and reducing the time needed to change a process from producing one good part to producing the next good part.

- It drives standardisation.
- Set-up is one of the six big losses.
- Quick changeovers make flow possible.
- It is often called "single minute exchange of die" (SMED).

## What will TPM do for my business?

TPM works in three steps, each building on the one before:

1. It improves your people.
2. It improves your plant and equipment.
3. It improves your company culture.
