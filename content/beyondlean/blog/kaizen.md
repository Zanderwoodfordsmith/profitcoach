Kaizen is the action phase of the [Standard Business Roadmap](/beyondlean/blog/standard-business-roadmap): the phase where the continuous improvement activities take place.

When we say kaizen, we don't just mean improvement workshops or quick-hit improvement teams. The meaning comes from the Japanese words kai and zen, where kai means change and zen means good. The popular meaning is the continual improvement of all areas of a company, not just quality or performance.

Kaizen: the continuous and incremental improvement of an activity, process, area or company to create more value with less waste, giving quantifiable and sustainable benefits.

## The aim of kaizen

To create an environment of continual improvement, using the skills and abilities of all our people to make quantifiable and sustainable change, while focusing on adding value and removing waste to increase customer satisfaction and profitability.

## Where the real changes happen

This is the phase where real changes are made: from cultural changes and partnership processes, through putting in place or upgrading key HR policies, right up to real visual and physical initiatives such as these.

### Performance management system (PMS)

Performance management is a structured, visual approach to monitoring how the business is performing. It makes your performance visible, drives you to take action, and gives you timely feedback on the effects of your actions.

### Standardised work

Standardised work delivers customer satisfaction through a stable, repeatable process. It ensures consistent safety, quality, cost and delivery in a standard working environment.

### TPM (total productive maintenance)

A system of maintenance covering the entire life of the equipment and all of the people involved. TPM is a tried and tested way of eliminating waste. It is a long-term process to increase skills, raise efficiency and work towards zero losses.

### SMED and quick changeovers

Quick changeover is a method of analysing and reducing the time needed to change a process from producing one good part to producing the next good part.

### 5S

5S is a systematic approach to workplace organisation. It encourages ownership and self-discipline, so the working environment is kept up and keeps getting better. It aims to remove waste from the workplace, provide an environment where continuous improvement is embraced, improve safety and increase quality.

### Line balancing

The even distribution of human work across a production area. The work is balanced so that overburden and waste are avoided.

## Choosing the right tool

Which of these, or which other lean or kaizen tool, to use will become clear from the diagnostic carried out in the earlier phase.

Use the right tool for your problem priority. Deploy the tool that cures what's hurting your business right now.

Ultimately, eliminating all of the seven wastes is the end goal of kaizen. Remember: change for the good is kai zen.
