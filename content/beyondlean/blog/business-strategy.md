## A clear business purpose

The first step in producing your business strategy is to set down a clear business purpose that can be shared with the whole workforce.

A series of visionary, yet specific, purpose statements can guide all employees through any kind of change. Developing a business purpose statement is critical to giving the whole change programme human energy and life.

**Everyone needs a purpose to believe in.**

The same purpose statement, or statements, can also be used to define clear operating principles, should you enter into a Gateway or partnership process with the workforce and the unions.

## Route map and tactical plans

These are your working tools: the detailed plans that will turn the future state map into reality.

The team can then begin to detail the tactical implementation plan to achieve the future state. Using the sizeable portions of the material and information flow diagram, the project team should focus on each portion in turn, starting at the point closest to the customer and working upstream. Each portion of the plan needs specific objectives and goals, with timings and the resources it needs. Highlight the key milestones that fall on the critical path of the plan.

When creating the improvement plan, the project team should not be distracted by chances for quick or ad hoc savings. The plan should follow the structured approach of working backwards from the customer. The team must only use Lean Six Sigma tools or techniques in pursuit of better flow.

Consider budget needs project by project. Most Lean Six Sigma improvements should take a low cost or no cost approach, for the biggest financial impact. Only spend what you can justify.
