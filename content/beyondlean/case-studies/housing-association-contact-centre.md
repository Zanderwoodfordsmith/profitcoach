## At a glance

- **2 to 12** of 16 areas at competitive level, from March to August
- **63 of 136** advisers over the call-time target before the work
- **21%** of staff had left in the previous 12 months
- **About 7%** of email briefings judged effective

## The problem

The centre handled customers' calls and emails. We scored it on 16 areas of good operations, from basic through competitive to leading. In March, 14 of the 16 scored basic.

Advisers were judged mainly on how short their calls were. That pushed people to end calls even when the problem was still there, and nobody measured whether it was fixed first time. Of 136 advisers, 63 were over the call-time target, and the wide spread showed people doing the same job in very different ways.

Team leaders spent most of their day on admin and rotas. Many sat away from their teams, on different shifts, and did no call listening or coaching. Briefings went out by email with no check that anyone understood them. The only face-to-face team meeting was monthly, and a fifth of staff had left in a year.

## What we did

- One standard way of handling calls, built around fixing the problem first time.
- New measures for teams and individuals, covering quality, output and speed as the customer sees them.
- A "structured day" for team leaders, with coaching to run it. Leaders moved to sit with their teams and took over call listening and coaching.
- Rotas and planning went back to the resourcing team, which gave leaders time to lead.
- Boards and short team briefings in every team, at every site.

## What changed

We ran the same assessment again in August. Twelve of the 16 areas now scored competitive, against 2 in March. The biggest gains came in managing people's performance, the team leader role, quality, output and team communication.

## What an owner can take from this

People chase what you measure. Measure only speed and you get quick calls and repeat calls, so measure whether the customer's problem got fixed. Your team leaders, or you, need time with the team every day to listen and coach. If rotas and admin eat that time, hand them to someone else. And brief people face to face, then check they understood. Email alone reached very few people here.
