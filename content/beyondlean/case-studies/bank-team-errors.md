## At a glance

- **56 to 11** errors per team each month
- **80%** fewer errors
- **3 months** from start to result
- **3 simple tools**: one written way of working, boards on the wall, coaching for leaders

## The problem

Each team in this part of the bank made 56 errors a month. In banking, every error means work done twice, and often a customer left waiting for it to be put right.

## What we did

We kept it simple and used three tools.

- **One written way of working.** We wrote down the best way to do each task, so the whole team worked the same way.
- **Boards on the wall.** Each team tracked its quality, cost and delivery on a board everyone could see.
- **Coaching for leaders.** We coached the team leaders to run their teams with these tools.

## What changed

Within three months, errors fell from 56 to 11 per team per month. That is 80% fewer.

## What an owner can take from this

Errors often come from people doing the same job in different ways. Write down the best way and teach it to everyone. Put the numbers where the team can see them, so a bad day shows up the same day. Then make sure your leaders, or you, use both every day. None of this needs new software, and three months is long enough to see the difference.
