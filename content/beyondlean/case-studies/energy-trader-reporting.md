## At a glance

- **26 hours** a week of reporting in a team that earned about half the trading profit
- **3 teams** building the same daily reports from the same data
- **74%** of the steps in one report added nothing
- **£1,022,022** a year found across three changes
- **1 week** for the first change to start paying back

## The problem

The trading team's job was to make money in the markets, and it earned about half the profit of its whole unit. Yet it spent about 26 hours a week, almost one full-time person, building reports.

Three teams built the same daily position and profit reports from the same data, each with its own home-made tools. For the profit report, one team worked from the previous day's close and the other two from the opening position, so they met every day to make the numbers agree. A weekly market report had also followed its original author into the trading team. Of the steps needed to produce it, 74% added nothing.

## What we did

We mapped how each report was made, step by step, and marked which steps added value. Then we recommended three moves:

- Put each report back with the team whose job it is. The market report would return to market analysis, and the position and profit reports would go to the one team set up to produce them.
- Agree one version of the truth, so nobody needs a daily meeting to make the numbers match.
- Simplify and automate what is left. The market report could drop from 6 hours a week to 1.5.

## What changed

These are the savings the review found. Moving the market report was worth £403,768 a year, starting within a week, plus £12,011 once it was automated. One team owning the position report was worth £121,448, and the profit report £484,795. That is £1,022,022 a year, mostly extra trading profit from traders back at their desks. The team's senior manager put the wider prize, if all its reporting moved out, at £2m more profit, as a cautious estimate.

## What an owner can take from this

Look at who in your business earns the money: the salespeople, the engineers, you. Count the hours they spend on reports, admin and checking numbers. Every hour moved off their desk can go back into earning. And if two people keep the same spreadsheet, pick one and trust it.
