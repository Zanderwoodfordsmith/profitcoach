## At a glance

- **50%+** more productive
- **42%** better quality
- **70%** fewer queries passed on to others
- **6 months** from pilot to roll-out

## The problem

The agency was bringing in New Ways of Working, starting with a pilot and then rolling it out across the agency.

## What we did

Paul led the work on the pilot and the roll-out. He also coached and mentored the agency's own internal consultants up to an accredited level from Cardiff Business School.

## What changed

Productivity rose by more than 50% and quality by 42%. Queries passed on to others fell by 70%.

## What an owner can take from this

Every query, every "can you check this?", is work done twice. Find out why the questions come up and fix that at the start. Try changes on one team before you change everyone. A pilot finds the problems while they are small, and gives you proof before you ask the rest of the business to follow.
