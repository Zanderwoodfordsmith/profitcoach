## At a glance

- **£10.5m to £7.5m** a year to run the centre
- **£3m** a year in signed-off benefits
- **About 200 seconds** off the average call
- **800 people** in the centre, and a team of 6 on the work
- **12 months** to cut the running costs

## The problem

The supplier's customer contact centre had 800 people, and it cost £10.5m a year to run.

## What we did

A team of 6, Paul among them, ran the improvement work over 12 months.

## What changed

In 12 months, the cost of running the centre fell from £10.5m to £7.5m a year. Benefits of £3m a year were signed off. The average call became about 200 seconds shorter.

## What an owner can take from this

Small savings add up fast on work that repeats all day. Take three minutes off a job your team does 50 times a day and you win back about 12 hours a week. Find the jobs that repeat most, and start there. Then coach your leaders to run the new way, so the savings last when you step back. A saving only counts once someone has checked it. Here, the £3m was signed off.
