## At a glance

- **£10.5m to £7.5m** a year to run the centre
- **£3m** a year in savings, signed off by the client
- **About 200 seconds** off the average call
- **800 people** in the centre, with a team of 6 leading the change
- **12 months** from start to finish

## The problem

The supplier's customer contact centre had about 800 people taking calls, and it cost £10.5m a year to run. At that size, every second on a call costs real money. A few seconds saved on each call, across 800 people, adds up to whole teams' worth of time.

## What we did

Paul led a team of six through the centre. We brought in the same ways of running the work that Paul has used in more than 12 call centres, and coached the centre's leaders to run them day to day, so the change would hold after we left.

## What changed

In 12 months, the cost of running the centre fell from £10.5m to £7.5m a year. The client signed off savings of £3m a year. The average call became about 200 seconds shorter.

## What an owner can take from this

Small savings add up fast on work that repeats all day. Take three minutes off a job your team does 50 times a day and you win back about 12 hours a week. Find the jobs that repeat most, and start there. Then coach your leaders to run the new way, so the savings last when you step back. A saving only counts once someone has checked it. Here, the client signed them off.
