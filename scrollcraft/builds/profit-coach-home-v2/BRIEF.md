# BRIEF: The Profit Coach homepage

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Zander asked for a new
Profit Coach homepage and said "feel free to choose" on palette and angle. The
evidence below is quoted from his request, Mariska's strategy notes, the Pam and
Zander transcript, the Drive brand kit (positioning input, Brand Guide rev4) and
`PRODUCT.md`. Everything marked **Authored** is my decision, not theirs.

## What this is

A homepage for The Profit Coach, aimed at UK owners of established businesses
(around £1M+, 5 to 50 staff, trades, engineering, manufacturing, construction,
services). The page gets owners to want a better business and take one step:
the free BOSS Score. It is **not** about becoming a coach. That is the BCA
brand.

> "Important this is not getting them to become business coaches (thats the bCA
> brand) this is about getting business owners to want to improve their
> business." (Zander)

**The one belief the page installs:** my business has a score, and I want to
know mine before anyone gives me more advice.

**The one action:** "Get my free BOSS Score". The same label is used everywhere.
The secondary link is "See how the Profit System works". The strategy call is
never the homepage ask, because Mariska reserves it for after the score.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Zander: "modern and premium." Authored: *a precise instrument in
   calm hands*. Three to five words: clear, exact, quietly confident, humane.
   References: a clinic's diagnostic report, a ship's radar console, an
   Ordnance Survey sheet. The positioning doc's register is "Linear rather
   than coaching-pastel".
2. **Journey (Mariska's order, verbatim beats):** Successful but trapped →
   problem → consequences → BOSS diagnostic → five levels → owner bottleneck →
   methodology → the maths → proof (Pam and Zander, after the diagnostic) →
   objections → CTA.
3. **Energy curve.** Authored: calm open → quiet personal → the loudest, darkest
   act at the diagnostic → a steady climb through the levels → analytical →
   warm and grave in the story → calm, resolved close.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment.** Mariska: visitors should
   think "How the hell do they know exactly what's happening in my business?"
   The one moment is when the screen goes dark and your answers draw onto the
   wheel. See the feeling curve below.
5. **One thing no other site does.** Authored: the page is operated, not read.
   You tick what's true about your week, and the page keeps your answers: they
   draw the diagnostic, sit in the dock, and come back at the close.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** Zander: "modern and premium." Authored:
   premium-minimal pushed toward **dense** in the data acts (the wheel, the
   50-playbook grid). Numbers and diagrams replace photography, per Mariska:
   "BOSS wheel, scorecards, dashboards, numbers, diagrams ... avoid smiling
   corporate teams, beach laptops, coaching photographs."
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Authored: **distinct scenes**, hard cuts
   between light and navy grounds. The page is a sequence of readings, and a
   continuous world would bury the diagnostic in atmosphere.
8. **Assets.** The logo in colour and white SVG, the Brand Guide rev4 palette
   (USAFA Blue #0B5191, Tufts Blue #2291EB, Blue Green #1CA0C2, Sky Blue
   #74D0ED, Cultured #F8F8F8), and one real photo: Pam and Zander on stage with
   a flipchart of numbers. That photo gets the Brand Guide treatment
   (desaturated, high contrast, corporate colour). No footage, no generated
   imagery, no stock. There is no ffmpeg full build and no KIE key on this
   machine, so this is a bring-your-own-assets build and the scene is drawn in
   HTML and SVG.

## Palette and type (authored)

- Light ground #FAFBFD, surface #FFFFFF, ink #0A1B2B, soft ink #4A5F73, accent
  USAFA #0B5191.
- Navy ground #051E36 with the brand gradient (#051E36 → #0B5191 → #2291EB) as
  light, never as a text background. Sky Blue #74D0ED is the accent on navy.
  Blue Green #1CA0C2 appears only inside the data (wheel, flags).
- Type is Figtree for display and text, the closest free match to the brand's
  Proxima Nova, which has no web licence here. IBM Plex Mono is used for the
  instrument readouts (codes, scores, refs). It has an engineering feel that
  suits the trades and manufacturing ICP. Geist was dropped: the design hook
  flagged it as an overused default, and it isn't the brand face anyway.

## Voice rules that bind this page

- No em dashes. Present tense, active voice, short sentences, UK spelling.
- Mariska's copy pillars:
  - Promise: "Less chaos, more profit, real freedom".
  - Mechanism: BOSS diagnostic.
  - Philosophy: data before advice.
  - Framework: Profit System.
  - Transformation: Overwhelmed to Owner.
  - Enemy: "a successful business that owns its owner".
- Positioning doc: "Everyone else sells a framework. We sell a score." Do not
  lead with aggregation ("25+ thinkers").
- Cleared numbers only: 50 questions, 10 areas, 5 levels, a score out of 100,
  about 10 minutes, free, 50 playbooks, 250+ coaches trained worldwide, and
  1.1⁵ = 1.61. No prices, no benchmarks, no invented testimonials. The only
  testimonial is John Davy's.

## The coach collective (authored recommendation)

Zander floated "thousands of years of business experience" and was unsure.
**Decision: not used.** It is an unverifiable sum, and the positioning doc asks
us to kill aggregation claims. The collective shows up as **one system, one
score**: "Business coaching starts with a coach. Profit coaching starts with a
business." (Mariska). Every Profit Coach works from the same BOSS Score, and
250+ coaches have been trained worldwide. It sits after the founder story, as
proof, and never as the hero.

## Build record

**Grammar: Self-diagnosis (new).** The page asks, the visitor answers, and the
page shows the answers back. Every act either asks something or shows what the
visitor said.
- **Bans:** scrub video, full-bleed or stock photography behind type,
  spotlight, magnet, drift interpolation, section counters, invented numbers,
  and painted dashboards. Every wheel is real markup computed from the
  visitor's ticks.
- **Nav:** a static masthead inside the hero, then a docked readout that
  carries your flag count and the one CTA.
- **Hero:** your week as chips tethered to "You".
- **Close:** your own wheel returned, plus one action.

**Signature move:** the recognition ledger. Ticking "that's me" lights that
area on a live BOSS Wheel. The flags persist in this browser, light up the
peak's wheel before the sweep reads all ten areas, sit in the dock, and come
back in the close.

**Journey (six beats):**
1. Recognition: hero and ledger.
2. Turn: the BOSS Score.
3. Placement: levels and bottleneck.
4. Substance: Profit System and maths.
5. Proof: story and collective.
6. Commitment: questions and close.
