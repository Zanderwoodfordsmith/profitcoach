It starts with where you are.

Every owner sits on one of five levels. The work only makes sense once you know which one, and what it takes to reach the next.

  1. Level 1

    Overwhelm

    Fires every day. It's hard to think past this week.

  2. Level 2

    Overworked

    It works because you work. The team waits on you.

  3. Level 3

    Organised

    Processes are in place. It runs without you, for a while.

  4. Level 4

    Overseer

    Leaders own their areas. You steer the business.

  5. Level 5

    Owner

    The business runs, grows and pays you. You choose your role.

Three pillars hold the whole business.

Vision is clarity about the future. Velocity is the speed of profit and growth. Value is what the business is worth over time.

Miss one and the other two can't carry it. Here, Velocity is missing. You can see the future, but profit doesn't move.

Each pillar opens into three steps. The gap is rarely a whole pillar. It's one step inside it. Add the owner, and that's ten.
