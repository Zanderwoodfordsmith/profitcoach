Pam learned this the hard way.

She built the business on being good at the work. The collapse taught her that isn't the same as being good at business.

Pam started her first business at 17. Twenty years later she had five businesses, a great team and money in the bank. It all ran on her hard work.

On New Year's Day 2000 she caught flu and went into work anyway. Within days she was paralysed from the neck down. On day ten her family was called in to say goodbye. Her son, Zander, was ten.

She spent three years learning to walk again, reading business books for up to eight hours a day. She went back £70,000 in debt, and within five years she had rebuilt everything. She sold at 46.

Zander had grown up inside that business. At eight he told her he had already spent too many years of his life in it. That line stayed with both of them.

Pam wanted to be the coach she wished she'd had when she started at 17. With Zander, she turned what she had learned into the Profit System: a score of the whole business, including the owner, and a set of playbooks in the order the score says to use them.

The same system is now how every Profit Coach works. 250+ coaches have been trained worldwide. Whoever you sit with, the work starts with your numbers.

Don't wait for a life-changing moment to make a better decision.

Pam Woodford, co-founder
