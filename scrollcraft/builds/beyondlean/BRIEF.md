# Beyondlean (Paul Swift) — scroll-craft brief

Interviewed in chat with Zander, 29 Oct 2026. Zander approved the proposed answers ("that direction sounds right") and asked for premium, not overdone.

1. Vibe: calm, exact, earned, British. Clean, modern, premium; "these people are trying to get clients, they've got to be trusted" (Zander). References he picked on Dribbble: Peterdraw luxury law, Eva Reed legal consultation, Consultia (layout, but more colour), Consulto (colour).
2. Journey: who Paul is and what he's done; the owner's mess; the turn (measure first); proof with real numbers; the man; book a call or take the BOSS Score.
3. Energy: quiet open, tension on the mess, strongest on "measure first", then calm and warm to the end.
4. Feeling curve:
   - Hero: respect. A serious man, big, with the record floating beside him.
   - Record: reassurance. Real numbers, sectors he has worked in.
   - Problems: recognition, a little discomfort. Their week in plain words.
   - Untangle (PEAK): relief. The knot of work running through the owner straightens into clean lanes.
   - Case files: confidence. Each story stacks on the last.
   - Paul: warmth. Farm, karate, his own small business.
   - BOSS Score: curiosity. A clear next step.
   - Close: ease. Ready when you are.
   Peak sentence: "There's a bit where all the lines tangled through 'you' straighten out as you scroll, and you get it." Lives in the Untangle act.
   Tell-someone: "It's the site where you watch your business stop running through you."
5. Unique: the untangle, drawn from Paul's Lean method, not stock icons.
6. Range: premium-minimal, dark editorial (midnight + one electric blue).
7. Structure: distinct scenes, not one unbroken world.
8. Assets: one headshot (cutout made with macOS Vision), his decks and old site. No generation (no Kie key).

Grammar: editorial dossier (natural flow, one pinned peak, sticky stacked case files).
Silence: the line under the record before the problems section is intentional.
