Profit Coach for LinkedIn — privacy policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

What this extension does

Helps coaches save LinkedIn profiles they are viewing into their Profit Coach pipeline, draft personalized messages they send themselves, and optionally save a Sales Navigator browser session for lead imports.

Data we access

  • Visible LinkedIn profile fields on pages you open (name, headline, company, about, profile URL) when you use Save or Draft.
  • LinkedIn cookies only when you click Save Sales Nav session or Copy cookies.
  • Profit Coach login from an open Profit Coach tab so actions attach to your account.

How we use the data

Profile data is sent to Profit Coach APIs you trigger, to create/update prospects and generate draft copy. Session cookies are stored for Sales Navigator imports you run in the app. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. We do not auto-send LinkedIn messages.

Storage & retention

Prospects and sessions are stored in your Profit Coach account. Contact us to request deletion. Extension local storage only remembers your preferred site and recent profile context on your device.
