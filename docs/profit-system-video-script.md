## 1. Why this matters
**Slide:** cover. The Profit System: 5 levels, 3 pillars, 9 steps.

In Session 1 you show a new client the map we use to grow their business. We
call it the Profit System, or the 5, 3, 9. Five levels, three pillars, nine
steps.

## 2. The five levels
**Slide:** the five levels on one page, 5 at the top, 1 at the bottom.

The five levels show how much a business still needs its owner. Level one is
Overwhelm. Too many fires, and they don't know what to do next. Level two is
Overworked. They do and check everything, because there are no systems and the
team isn't trained. Level three is Organised. Processes are in place, so things
run without them for a while. Level four is Overseer. They work on the business,
not only in it. And level five is Owner. Leaders run the business, and the owner
steers. Most owners you meet are at level one or two. Your job is to move them
up.

## 3. The three pillars
**Slide:** the three pillars hexagons.

Every business has three big jobs. We call them the three pillars: clarify
vision, control velocity and create value. You can say clarify, control, create.
Or vision, velocity, value. Clarify vision is about the future: where the
business is going and the plan to get there. Control velocity is about speed:
how fast profit and growth come. Create value is about the long run: the
numbers, the systems and the team, so the business can run, and sell, without
the owner.

## 4. What if a pillar is missing?
**Slide:** the hexagons with the Firefighter, the Operator and cash flow chaos.

So what happens if a pillar is missing? With no clear vision, you get the
Firefighter. The business is growing, but there are always fires, because
nobody agrees where it's going. With no value, you get the Operator. They know
what they want, but they're always busy and everything relies on them. And with
no velocity, you get cash flow chaos. They're clear on where they're going, but
always short of cash.

## 5. The nine steps
**Slide:** the 9 Step Roadmap from the app.

Each pillar holds three steps. That gives you nine, from where the business is
today to the business they want.

## 6. Clarify vision: steps 1 to 3
Under clarify vision: one, Aligned Vision, from a foggy future to clear
direction. Two, Defined Strategy, from busy tactics to a deliberate strategy.
Three, Disciplined Planning, from endless to-dos to a 90-day rhythm.

## 7. Control velocity: steps 4 to 6
Under control velocity: four, Profit and Cash Flow, from tight cash to reliable
margin. Five, Revenue and Marketing, from feast or famine to predictable demand.
Six, Operations and Delivery, from a business that needs the owner to one that
runs without them.

## 8. Create value: steps 7 to 9
Under create value: seven, Financials and Metrics, from gut feel to numbers they
trust. Eight, Infrastructure and Systems, from daily heroics to repeatable
systems. Nine, Team and Leadership, from doing it all to a team that runs the
machine.

## 9. PROFIT
**Slide:** P, R, O, F, I, T.

Here's a handy one. The first letters of steps four to nine spell PROFIT.

## 10. How it all fits together
**Slide:** the five levels beside the pyramid, Owner Performance at its base.

Here's how it all fits together. Owner Performance is the foundation: the
owner's own time, energy and habits. On top of it you build clarify vision, then
control velocity, then create value. As the owner works up the pyramid, they
move up the five levels.

## 11. The grid in BOSS Pro
**Slide:** the BOSS Pro grid from the app.

Put the five levels against the ten areas and you get fifty boxes. BOSS Pro asks
one question per box, so you see which boxes are red, amber or green for your
client. Fix the lowest level first.

## 12. Playbooks
**Slide:** the Playbooks library and one playbook from the app.

And every box has a playbook. A short guide that says what the problem is, why
it matters, and the actions to take. You find them all in Keep Clients, under
Playbooks.

## 13. How to use it in Session 1
**Slide:** show the map, ask their level, run BOSS Pro together.

In Session 1, show your client the five levels, the three pillars and the nine
steps. Keep it short and use your own style. Ask them which level they think
they're at. Then run BOSS Pro together, and the grid shows you both where to
start.
