# Lead Gen Jay, Cold Email Course 2026, part 3 of 3 (04:55 to 07:15)

Covers analytics, running the system, replies, scaling, Clay, signals, reply automation, omnichannel. Caption errors corrected (GlockApps, Unibox, Apify, n8n, Supabase, Trigify, HeyReach, FindyMail).

## 1. Core principles
- The biggest factor is **who you email**. Heavily emailed people, big companies, a weak offer or a CTA that asks too much all lower results [05:00:00].
- Judge on **opportunities (positive replies)**, not raw replies [04:59:00]. Rates of 10 to 20% come only from narrow signal lists that can't be scaled [04:56:00].
- Getting replies is half the job. Turning them into calls is the other half. **Speed beats personalisation** [05:17:01].
- Get a plain control campaign working before trying AI personalisation or signals [06:07:02, 06:24:00].
- After someone replies it's a warm email: links, video and sales words are fine [05:17:01].

## 2. Campaign structure
- No links in email 1. Links only in emails 2 and 3, with tracking off [05:08:00].
- Test email 3 as a new thread with a new subject ("Yes or no") against sending it in the same thread [05:12:00].
- If they don't answer your reply, set a "remind me if no reply in 1 week" reminder. Getting to a call often takes 2 to 4 replies. Jay says this gives a 50% lift from interested reply to booked call [05:24:01, 05:28:01].
- "Try me in 30 days" replies go into a 30-day-wait subsequence [05:22:00].
- Keep the conversation in the Unibox until they **book or opt in**, and only then move them to the main CRM and nurture emails. Never drip every interested reply from another domain [05:18:00, 05:35:01].
- Recycle people who didn't reply after 3 to 6 months (never sooner). Use new pains and case studies. Include only "completed, no reply" [05:42:00 to 05:44:01].

## 3. Copy
- AI writes **only 2 to 8 words or one sentence**, from research that isn't on LinkedIn (clients, partners, news, ads). Jay says this can lift replies by up to 200% [06:04:00]. Good: "Saw you just partnered with Olay on a recent campaign", "saw your Facebook ad offering X". Bad: "Go Gators" (from LinkedIn), "I admire your company's ability to…" [06:05:00 to 06:07:02].
- An AI picks the right **case study for each prospect's segment**, so one campaign serves many sectors [06:08:00 to 06:10:01].
- Answer the objection they haven't voiced in the copy itself [07:04:00].
- Reply rules: answer their question first ("Thanks for asking about pricing. It ranges from X to Y. I'd love to hear your needs so I can give a more accurate quote"). Don't dump everything; leave a reason for the call. Always end with a clear next step: "Do you have 15 minutes next Tuesday? Here's the link." [05:26:00 to 05:28:01]
- Soft-no reply: "Thanks for responding. I understand you're not interested in X. If you ever are, here are some of our case studies [link]. I really appreciate you taking the time." [05:25:01] Some "not interested" replies are openings: "That's not what we focus on, we do Y. Does that change anything?" [05:24:01]

## 4. Targeting and lists
- When a campaign is weak, check the list first: right companies, bounces, which email providers [05:05:00].
- Clay flow: Apollo → AI web-research qualification (**about 50% failed** even in a good list) → MillionVerifier → FindyMail for catch-all addresses → personalisation → Instantly [06:12:02 to 06:18:01].
- Signals give 5 to 20% reply rates, but there's roughly a 50% chance they do no better than a plain control [06:24:00]:
  - Job listings for the role you'd replace (leave out competitors). Freshness: Apify daily > Clay > Apollo [06:25:01].
  - People who engage with chosen LinkedIn creators' posts (Trigify). Only 20 to 30% pass qualification, and the shared creator gives you rapport [06:28:01].
  - Funding or leadership hires: scrape Crunchbase daily, and keep a database so no company is emailed twice [06:32:01].
  - Business award winners from Google News [06:35:00].
- Block list: past or current clients and competitors in a Google Sheet that Instantly reads [05:37:02].
- Keep a record (sheet) of every lead contacted, with status, before deleting leads from Instantly [05:39:01].

## 5. Deliverability and infrastructure
- **Open and click tracking off** [05:03:01].
- Launch: domains at least 30 days old, 100% health, max **20 a day per mailbox with slow ramp on** [05:14:01].
- Instantly settings: skip hostile prospects; "allow risky emails" off; deliverability optimisation on; version 2 analytics on [05:15:00, 05:12:00].
- The "this message seems dangerous" banner is as bad as spam, and normal tests miss it. Test the **real copy from a real mailbox in GlockApps** and send to your own addresses [05:06:00].
- Run 25 a day per mailbox (never above 50). Scale by **adding mailboxes**: 10,000 a day = 400 mailboxes = 100 domains × 4 [05:49:02, 05:54:00]. Spread across Google, Microsoft and SMTP providers [05:55:00].
- If inbox placement drops: pause, warm only, **change the copy first**, then warm longer [05:45:00]. Use spintax and rotate whole offers, because copy "burns out" [05:59:01].

## 6. Numbers and benchmarks
- High: **above 5% replies** (spam-box signal campaign about 22%). Good and scalable: **1 to 2% replies, about 1% opportunities**. Low: under 1% replies, under 0.5% opportunities [04:58:01 to 05:00:00].
- Replying within 30 minutes gives a 60% higher chance of a call. Aim for 5 to 10 minutes [06:40:00, 05:16:01].
- "J" = emails per booked call, the number to know before scaling. At 200 emails per call, 10,000 emails = 50 calls, which needs about 10 closers [05:48:00].
- About $3 per mailbox per month [05:54:00]. LinkedIn automation about 30 connections a day [07:01:00].

## 7. Mistakes
- Slow replies, canned replies that ignore the question, too much information, no CTA, no follow-up [05:26:00].
- Replying from a different mailbox makes it cold again [05:18:00]. Never checking the Unibox "Others" tab (late replies, different addresses) [05:20:00].
- Sharing the inbox between three people. Give it to one owner [05:57:00].
- Scaling before the team can keep up: replies pile up, lists run out [05:59:01].
- Wrong AI facts get you marked as spam. Read 100 outputs and you need 9 out of 10 good [06:06:01].
- Picking A/B winners without enough data [05:10:00].

## 8. Tricks
- Always keep a control. Pick winners on opportunities. Keep an AI chat log of past test results [05:09:00, 05:45:00].
- Saved Unibox reply snippets, also in the mobile app [05:23:02].
- Webhook on "interested" → Perplexity research → AI draft reply → sheet → alert to the salesperson [05:28:01]. At 10+ replies a day: an agent that sorts replies (interested / soft no / hard no) with human approval and retrains on corrections [06:45:01 to 06:52:00].
- `?utm_source=inst` on the calendar link, so the CRM can report calls and sales that came from cold email [05:52:00].
- Retargeting pixel on the calendar or landing page: case-study and objection ads for 30 to 90 days [06:56:00, 07:03:00].
- Voicemail drop when they open the email, LinkedIn connect, handwritten letter after a positive reply [06:58:00 to 07:01:00].
