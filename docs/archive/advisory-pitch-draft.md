# Advisory pitch (draft, 20 Oct 2026)

The coach's version of Alex's done-for-you pitch. Same shape: bridge, three phases with check-ins, recap, temperature check, price. The difference is the promise. BCA builds it **for** the coach; the coach does it **with** the owner. The three phases are the three BOSS pillars, so the call, the explainer brochure (`/site/[slug]/explainer`) and the BOSS Wheel use one map.

Words in [brackets] come from the call or the coach's Blueprint. Nothing here is final.

---

## Bridge

So you mentioned you're struggling with... [their main pain]

- every decision still coming back to you
- turnover growing, but profit and cash not keeping up
- not being able to step away without things slipping

And that's costing you... [the consequence]

- long days, and a team that waits on you
- worry about cash at the end of every month

So here's what I'd suggest.

I work with you as your advisor. Someone you meet every month, who helps you see the whole business clearly, decide what matters most, and follow through.

And the part that matters most: we do it together. I bring the questions, the tools and the plan. You and your team make the changes. That's what makes them stick, and it's why it takes a few hours a month of your time, not days.

That's what takes you from [where they are today] to [their goal].

There are three phases to it. We set the direction, we get it moving, then we make it last. Let me take you through each one.

## Phase 1. Vision: set the direction

Most owners have never seen their whole business on one page. Every decision gets made in the moment, with no full picture behind it.

So phase 1 is where we fix that. It takes the first month, four sessions.

1. **Your BOSS review.** You take the full BOSS Diagnostic, 50 questions across 10 areas, including you as the owner. We go through it area by area, so you see where the business stands and where it leaks time and money.
2. **Your biggest lever.** We find the one or two issues costing you the most, and agree what to tackle first.
3. **Your three-year direction.** Where you want the business, and your life, to be in three years.
4. **Your 90-day plan.** Three priorities, who owns each one, and the weekly numbers that show they're working.

What you need to bring is honest answers and your numbers.

So by the end of month one you know where you stand, what matters most, and exactly what the next 90 days hold.

**Check in:** Does that make sense so far? Happy for me to show you what happens once the plan's set?

## Phase 2. Velocity: get it moving

So now you've got the plan. Phase 2 is where we go after the areas that move money and time fastest. Your score decides the order.

You know how you said [their money or time worry]? This is where we tackle it.

- **Profit and cash.** Pricing that holds its margin, costs under control, a cash forecast you trust.
- **Sales and marketing.** A clear offer, sales calls that convert, follow-up that brings customers back.
- **Operations.** Systems for the work you repeat, so jobs run without you in the middle.

Every fix has a playbook: the problem, the steps, the template and the result to expect. So nothing rests on guesswork.

And small gains add up. Improve five numbers by 10% each, leads, calls booked, sales closed, the average sale and how often customers buy again, and that's 61% more revenue. That's the maths, not a promise. We put your real numbers in during month one.

Your part in this phase is making the changes between sessions. My part is keeping you to them.

**Check in:** Does that make sense? Can you see how that takes [their worry] off you? Want me to show you how we make it last?

## Phase 3. Value: make it last

The business is making more and keeping more cash. Phase 3 is where it grows in value, because it stops depending on you for everything.

- **Your numbers.** Dashboards your team keeps up to date, so you read the business in minutes.
- **Your systems.** Tools and processes that let you grow, with automation where it saves time.
- **Your team.** The right people in the right seats, and leaders who decide without you.

There are five levels every owner moves through: Overwhelm, Overworked, Organised, Overseer, Owner. Your BOSS Score tells us where you are today. The work is moving you up, one level at a time.

And it's built in. Every 90 days we re-score the business so you see the change, and we set the next 90-day plan.

Pick the line that matches how the coach sells:

- **Ongoing:** This is where many owners keep working with me for years, one 90-day plan at a time.
- **90 days:** At day 90 we re-score. You can keep going with me, or run the plan yourself.
- **90 days, then ongoing:** After the first 90 days we go month to month, one 90-day plan at a time.

**Check in:** Does all of that make sense? Any questions on the three phases?

## Temperature check

From everything today, I'm confident I can help. But the most important thing is that you feel solid on it.

On a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is this doesn't make sense and 10 is I can see exactly how this works for my business, where are you?

9 or above with conviction: move to price. 8 or below: "What's keeping you from a 9 or a 10?" Handle it, then check again. Never move to price over a doubt.
