**📚 Summary:**

This lesson explores the importance of mapping the customer journey to improve the overall customer experience. By tracking every interaction between the company and the customer, businesses can gain valuable insights into customer actions, feelings, and thoughts, leading to long-term loyalty. Understanding the customer journey is a crucial aspect of providing excellent customer service.

#### **🤓 Learning Points:**

The customer journey consists of five stages: awareness, interest, consideration, purchase, and post-purchase.

Mapping the customer journey helps companies understand how customers learn about their products, make purchasing decisions, and ultimately engage with the brand.

By identifying every touch point and understanding customer emotions and thoughts throughout the journey, companies can identify areas for improvement and provide a better overall customer experience.

#### **✅ Actions & Application:**

1. Identify the touch points in your own customer journey and map them out to gain a better understanding of the customer experience.  
2. Analyze the emotions and thoughts that customers may have at each touch point. Use this knowledge to address pain points and create positive interactions.  
3. Regularly review and update your customer journey map to ensure it reflects the evolving needs and expectations of your customers.
