# Speed Up Your Learning

## Body
This lesson teaches you how to speed up video playback to enhance your learning efficiency and complete the course faster (& how to do it with videos outside of The Academy).

**In this guide**

- How To Do It
- Method 1: Using Built-in Speed Controls
- Method 2: Using a Browser Plugin

## Guide
### What Is This

This lesson teaches you how to speed up video playback to enhance your learning efficiency and complete the course faster (& how to do it with videos outside of The Academy).

### Why Is It Important/Useful

-   **Time-Saving**: Finish the course in half the time.
-   **Improved Focus**: Faster playback can help you stay more engaged.
-   **Enhanced Learning**: Speeds up the intake of information, making you a quicker and more efficient learner.
-   **Versatile Application**: Useful not just for this course, but for any video learning you do in the future.

## How To Do It

### Method 1: Using Built-in Speed Controls

**Step 1: Access the Video**

Open the video you want to watch in The Academy's platform.

**Step 2: Find the Speed Control**

Look at the lower right corner of the video player.

**Step 3: Adjust the Speed**  

Click the speed control button. Choose your desired speed (0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, or 2x).

The pro of this is you can start using it immediately just click the speed gauge on each video.  
The downside is you can't go higher than 2x and you have to click the gauge every time you click a new video.

* * *

### Method 2: Using a Browser Plugin

If you want to go faster than 2x or watch videos outside the program, follow these steps.

**Step 1: Install the Plugin**

Download the Video Speed Controller plugin from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/video-speed-controller/nffaoalbilbmmfgbnbgppjihopabppdk?hl=en) (search on other browsers)

**Step 2: Add to Chrome**

Click "Add to Chrome" in the upper right corner of the plugin page. Confirm by clicking "Add Extension" in the popup.

**Step 3: Use the Plugin**  

Play your video. A speed control panel will appear in the top right. Use it to adjust the playback speed to your liking.
