# Choosing between Ai Co-Pilot & Auto-Pilot

## Body
### **Objective**

This SOP outlines the exact steps to choose the appropriate AI mode — **Co-Pilot** or **Auto-Pilot** — for managing campaign conversations effectively inside Connector Ai.  
By following this process, you’ll ensure your AI operates in alignment with your campaign strategy, message tone, and desired level of automation.

---

### **Key Steps**

1. Access Your Campaign  
* Open the **Campaign Management** interface.  
* Locate the test or active campaign you wish to manage (e.g., *Dummy Campaign*).

---

2. Open AI Inbox Manager  
* Click the **three dots (⋮)** next to the selected campaign.  
* Choose **AI Inbox Manager** from the dropdown options.

---

3. Select the Relevant SOP  
* From the SOP list, select the **Standard Operating Procedure** you created for this campaign.  
* This ensures your AI uses the correct communication framework and objectives.

---

4. Confirm AI Selection  
* Navigate to the **AI Settings** tab.  
* Double-check that the correct SOP is selected and applied to the campaign.

---

5. Choose Your AI Mode

Select between two operating modes:

* **Co-Pilot Mode** → The AI drafts replies for your approval before sending.  

  Ideal when you want to maintain full control and review tone, accuracy, and brand alignment.  

* **Auto-Pilot Mode** → The AI generates and automatically sends replies.  

  Perfect for tested campaigns that require speed and consistency without manual review.

---

6. Finalize and Activate  
* Click **Send** if you’re operating in Co-Pilot mode.  
* For Auto-Pilot, ensure all AI settings are correctly configured and saved.  
* Run a short test conversation to verify behavior before deploying to live leads.

---

### **Cautionary Notes**

⚠️ **Double-check your SOP selection** — using the wrong one can cause off-brand or inaccurate responses.  
⚠️ **If in Co-Pilot mode**, always review draft replies for quality, tone, and accuracy before sending.

---

### **Review Checklist**

Before finalizing your AI setup:

* Correct SOP is selected.  
* AI mode (Co-Pilot or Auto-Pilot) is properly configured.  
* Test messages reviewed for accuracy and tone.  
* Campaign responses function as intended.
