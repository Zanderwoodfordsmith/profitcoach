## Guide
## **What Is This:**

This training module outlines a step-by-step process for ending a coaching session, ensuring that the session concludes on a positive, clear, and actionable note.

## **Why Is It Important/Useful:**

* **Defines Clear Actions**: Ensures the client knows what actions to take next.  
* **Reinforces Key Learnings**: Highlights the most important insights and takeaways.  
* **Builds Motivation**: Ends on a positive note, motivating the client to take action.  
* **Schedules Future Engagement**: Sets the stage for the next session, maintaining continuity.  
* **Provides Support**: Ensures the client knows how to reach you between sessions for any questions or support.

## **How To Do It:**

### **Ending the Coaching Session with the F.I.N.I.S.H FrameworkPro Tip** - You want to use this along with the Coaching Sheet.

**Feedback & Focus -** Recap the main points discussed, including tools and strategies like the Growth Accelerator. Encourage feedback to ensure clarity. Provide relevant tools, templates, or resources related to the Growth Accelerator tool, positional contracts, and cash flow planning.

**Insights -** Highlight the most important insights and confirm agreed-upon actions with the client. Share success stories of other businesses that have successfully implemented similar strategies to reinforce insights.

**Next Steps -** Confirm specific actions the client will complete before the next session and discuss how progress will be measured. Encourage the client to choose an accountability partner within their team to ensure progress between sessions.

**Inspire -** End on a positive note with encouragement and support. Share relevant tools, resources, or success stories for inspiration. Suggest relevant books, podcasts, or workshops that can aid in the client's continuous learning and development.

**Schedule -** Set a date for the next session and discuss progress review. Remind the client to reach out for support or to share wins. Ensure they are aware of any additional resources or learning opportunities before the next meeting.

**Help & Support -** Ensure the client knows how to reach you for follow-up questions. Provide relevant tools, templates, or resources they need. Reiterate the importance of having an accountability partner and remind them how they can contact you for support.

**Do not recommend anything that is not totally relevant to what they are currently focussed on improving.Questions to Ask:**

* "What is your understanding of the main points discussed today?"  
* "What are the key insights you are taking away from this session?"  
* "What immediate actions will you take following this session?"  
* "How will you measure the success of these actions?"  
* "When shall we schedule our next session to review progress?"  
* "Do you have any final thoughts or questions before we wrap up?"

### **Objective:**

By following this structured approach, members will effectively end coaching sessions in a manner that is clear, motivating, and conducive to continued progress. This will help ensure that both the coach and client leave the session with a strong understanding of what has been achieved and what needs to be done next.
