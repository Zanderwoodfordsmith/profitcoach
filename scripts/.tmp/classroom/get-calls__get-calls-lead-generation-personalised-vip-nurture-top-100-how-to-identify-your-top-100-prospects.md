# get-calls-lead-generation-personalised-vip-nurture-top-100-how-to-identify-your-top-100-prospects

## Body
### **What Is This**

Your Top 100 list is a targeted group of high-potential prospects you want to focus your personalized efforts on. Managing this list effectively is crucial for successful nurturing and conversion.

### **Why Is It Important/Useful:**

**Streamlines Efforts:** Organizing your list helps you efficiently manage and track your outreach.

**Enhances Personalization:** Allows you to tailor communications based on past interactions and preferences.

**Supports Multi-Channel Communication:** Enables you to engage prospects across different platforms, boosting your chances of success.

## **How To Do It**

### **Step 1: Select the Very Best Prospects**

**Choose Prospects Who Fit Your Business Goals:** Focus on individuals or companies that align closely with your ideal client profile and can significantly impact your business. I.e. you want the very best. So if it is law firms you target, you would now choose the law firms with the perfect size or some other criteria that makes them even more ideal.

**Include People with Existing Relationships:** Add those who already know you and could benefit from your services, even if they are slightly outside your core area because you have an increased probability of signing them.

### **Step 2: Ensure Contactability**

LinkedIn Connections: Ensure these prospects are connected with you on LinkedIn. If not, send a personalized connection request.

Email or Phone: Have alternative contact methods like email or phone numbers to ensure you can reach out through multiple channels.

### **Step 3: Choose a System to Store Your Top 100 List**

You want to now go and create, save or import your list into one of these places:

**Saved Leads List in Sales Navigator:** Ideal for tracking LinkedIn interactions, allowing you to save leads and monitor their activity.

**Tags Inside Connector:** Use tags to categorize prospects within your LinkedIn network, helping you filter and manage them effectively.

**Campaign Inside Connector:** Create a non-messaging campaign to track profile views and activity, keeping you informed of their updates.

**SmartList in CRM:** Best for sending emails and making calls, a CRM consolidates all client interactions in one place for easy access.

**Excel Spreadsheet:** A simple starting point, though it lacks the automation and integration features of other tools.

Or a combo of them if you have different contact info for people. As long as you stay on top of all places.

### **Step 4: Track Your Messaging**

**Keep Detailed Records:** Use your chosen system to log each interaction, noting the date, method of contact, and any follow-up required (this is the benefit to using Connector or CRM as it does it automatically)

**Regularly Update Your List:** Continuously refine your list based on interactions and adjust your focus as needed.

Objective:

By the end of this lesson, you’ll have a practical system for defining and managing your Top 100 list, ensuring you can efficiently nurture these prospects and grow your coaching business.
