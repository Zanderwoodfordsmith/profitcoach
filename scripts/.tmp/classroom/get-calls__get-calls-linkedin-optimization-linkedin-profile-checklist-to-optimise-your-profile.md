# get-calls-linkedin-optimization-linkedin-profile-checklist-to-optimise-your-profile

## Body
### **What Is This?**

We have an updated checklist for optimizing your LinkedIn profile. While we are creating individual videos for each section, you can use this checklist to ensure your profile is optimized right now.

### **Why Is This Important?**

You want to have an optimized profile because when you start marketing on LinkedIn, it is what people will check to see who you are. An optimized profile can significantly improve your credibility and engagement, making it easier for potential clients to connect with you.

### **Resources**

📄 [LinkedIn Profile Optimisation Checklist](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uNFEygVhF4QxlCEYXFBWr1SnDD4mF8V5LKbQ67GKBRE/copy)

### **How It Works**

### **First Impression**

* **Background Photo:** Show yourself in action to build trust; avoid boring corporate pictures.  
* **Headline:** Make it specific and informative to your audience, including your added value. Optionally, include job title, keywords, and a call-to-action (CTA).  
* **Professional Headshot:** Head and shoulders facing into the screen, highly recommend a colour-popped background & be smiling. Use a recent (not older than 3 years) and professional photo shoot. Ensure authenticity.  
* **Custom URL:** Personalise your LinkedIn URL.

Follow The Steps Here to [Personalise Yoru LinkedIn URL](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a542685/manage-your-public-profile-url?lang=en#:~:text=On%20your%20profile%20page%2C%20click,URL%20in%20the%20text%20box.)

* **Create a Company Page:** Sync your profile with a company page. This enables your company logo to show at the top of your profile and gives your company a dedicated page to use.

You can either use your own busines name or We Recommended linking with the Profit Coach page. You can simply start typing it in and then select it.

### **About Section**

**We will write the 1st draft for you, you just fill out the form.**

* **Personal Introduction:** Write in the first person, focusing on your prospects and clients. Trigger your audience to click "see more" or contact you. Include a Professional Personal Introduction (P.P.I.).  
* **Expertise & Added Value:** Summarise your expertise and knowledge. Position your added value for your target audience.  
* **CTA - Skills:** Make it easy for people to contact you by providing options. Add top skills to improve internal SEO and visibility.

### **Settings Behind the Scenes**

* **Visibility:** Set visibility to "Anyone" or "All LinkedIn Members".  
* **Contact Details:** Set email "open" to 2nd-degree connections. Add a phone number for connections. Add websites (SEO). Optionally, include Twitter, birthday, and instant messaging.  
* **Two-Factor Authentication:** Turn on two-factor authentication for added security.

### **LinkedIn Services Page**

* **Highlight Services:** Create a dedicated landing page for your services.  
* **Visual Branding:** Use 3 images and keywords to enhance profile branding.  
* **Client Attraction:** Attract clients directly through LinkedIn feeds and searches.

### **Featured Section**

* **Content:** Upload at least 3 items (images, links, articles). Ensure they have a converting element or offer included.

### **Articles & Activity**

* **Activity Section:** Set your profile to show the type of content you wish your audience to see first, for example - posts, videos, newsletters.  
* **Analytics Section:** Monitor your profile growth and prospect interactions (visible only to you).

### **Contributions**

* **Content Sharing:** Share relevant content regularly. Aim to publish at least 4 times per month. Engage with comments and discussions to increase visibility.

### **Other**

* **10 Second Name Pronunciation:** Record via the mobile app.

### **Work Experience**

* **Current Experience:**  
  * Connect company page (logo).  
  * Provide a customer-centric description (minimum 3 lines). Use up to 2000 characters in every role to boost search and visibility rankings.  
  * Add media (SEO).  
  * Include testimonials and results.  
  * Embed 50 skills words throughout the experience section.  
  * **SEO Job Titles:** Use the drop-down menu for job titles; avoid custom titles to ensure visibility and search ranking.  
* **Past Experience:**  
  * List relevant past positions.  
  * Use drop-down suggestions for job titles and company logos.  
  * Enhance descriptions with keywords and multimedia.  
  * Provide a customer-centric description (minimum 3 lines). Use up to 2000 characters in every role to boost search and visibility rankings.  
  * **SEO Job Titles:** Use the drop-down menu for job titles; avoid custom titles to ensure visibility and search ranking.

### **Education**

* **University Information:** Use the drop-down menu to embed logos. Add links related to your current position. Use the education section to show contact details on top of your profile.

### **Volunteer Experiences**

* **Details:** List relevant volunteer roles. Highlight any leadership roles or significant contributions.

### **Certifications**

* **Details:** Highlight relevant certifications. Link to the provider's company page if possible.

### **Publications**

* **Details:** Link to any publications, LinkedIn Newsletters, or interviews.

### **Honours and Awards**

* **Details:** List all relevant honours and awards.

### **Languages**

* **Details:** Include all languages you are proficient in.

### **Recommendations**

* **Received Recommendations:** Ensure a minimum of 3 received recommendations. Ensure they are visible and client-related. Ensure the last recommendation is more recent than 1 year ago.  
* **Given Recommendations:** Give a minimum of 3 recommendations to others. Giving recommendations proactively boosts relationships.

### **Companies & LinkedIn Groups**

* **Following Companies:** Follow at least 20 companies.  
* **LinkedIn Groups:** Be a member of at least 5 LinkedIn Groups.
