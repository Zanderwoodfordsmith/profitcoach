# profit-brand-framework-revenue-marketing-platforms

## Body
### **Platforms**

Platforms to showcase your skills and the efficacy of your business can be divided into 3 categories:

1. Owned  
2. Earned  
3. Paid

### **Owned:**

### **Website**

A great way to ensure your leads are bringing people to a centralized point, is to have a website and a landing page.

**A good website should follow the principles of AIDA.**

1. Attention  
2. Interest  
3. Desire  
4. Action

These are the four steps you need to take your audience through if you want them to buy your product/service

### **Email Marketing**

Email marketing is a highly effective platform, as your email list should already consist of potential leads.

By Keeping in contact with these leads you will be able to start forming a relationship, and eventually position yourself to make a sale.

### **Speaking on stage**

Hosting talks or workshops is a great way to boost your perceived authority in your niche.

Here is a great resource on how to plan and execute your public speaking event:

[How to Use Public Speaking to Grow Your Business](https://www.score.org/blog/how-use-public-speaking-grow-your-business)

### **Earned:**

### **Make sure your website is utilising SEO**

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is the process of optimising your website so that it is easily found by search engines. The better your SEO, the more likely your website will be found, and the better your chances of getting leads. 

How to improve your SEO

* Optimize website speed  
* Include internal links in website pages  
* Use keywords relevant to your niche   
* Update old content 

### **PR**

Using positive PR is a great tactic to improve the public perception of your brand.

[How to Increase Your Brand Exposure in 2018 with Public Relations](https://www.quicksprout.com/how-to-increase-your-brand-exposure-with-public-relations/)

### **Implement Referral systems**

Word of mouth is one of the most powerful ways to get gain trust among consumers, and to advertise your brand. People will always trust something a friend or family member told them, over any marketing campaign.

A great way to increase reach by word of mouth is to set up a referral system. This is a way to give people an incentive to advertise your business to their friends.

An example of this is a referral programme where if you subscribe to an online course, you will get 10% off if someone else signs up with your referral code.

### **Social Media**

* Set up a Facebook page  
* Start a youtube channel  
* Create a business page for LinkedIn  
* Get interviews on relevant Podcasts in your Niche

### **Paid:**

### **Set up a PPC ad**

PPC (Pay Per Click) allows people to bid a certain amount of money on specific keywords.It is essentially a way of buying visits to your site, rather than attempting to “earn” those visits organically.

Each time someone types in that keyword in google search, their ad will appear. You then pay each time a person clicks on your ad. If you are using PPC correctly, your ROI will soon outweigh the cost.

*Example of a PPC ad*

### **Send Direct Mail to potential consumers**

Direct mail is a great way to keep in contact with potential clients who are not yet ready to buy.

By sending weekly on monthly mails you remain on their mind, and when the time comes that they are ready to make a sale, you will be the first business on their list. 

Direct mail is also usually sent to people who have subscribed to or mailing list. Your advertising can be much more direct as they have already notified you that they are interested in your business by signing up.
