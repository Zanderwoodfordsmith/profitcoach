## Body
### **3 Greatest offers of all time**

If your business has a good enough offer you don’t need amazing copy or creatives because the value you provide will be enough to sell itself.

Companies have been using special offers as a marketing tactic for years, and if done right it can earn you a lot of money and customers;

### **3 Greatest offers of all time**

1. **Dominoes:** delivered pizza to your door in 30 minutes or it’s free  
2. **Sony:** 10 CDs for a penny   
3. **FedEx:** Your order will be delivered by tomorrow

What made these offers so successful was that each offer has an element of believability as well as a high ROI. 

### **When creating an offer keep the following in mind:** 

* Your offer needs to be attainable if it sounds too good without a justification than the offer won’t be a success. This is why closing down sales work because the prospect understands why there is such a discount.  
* You also need to provide something that your customers actually want or will get excited about.
