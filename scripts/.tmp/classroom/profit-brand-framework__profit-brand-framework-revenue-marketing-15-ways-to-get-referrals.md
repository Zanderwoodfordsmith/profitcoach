### **15 Ways to get referrals**

Referrals are a great way to get new leads and more business. Trying to get referrals consistently is however not that easy. 

Here are 15 things you can do today that will increase your chances of getting referrals:

1. Create a referral program  
2. Thank your referral sources  
3. Offer alternate ways to recommend you  
4. Educate current customers across all products and services  
5. Add a link for referral submissions  
6. Be remarkable  
7. Inspire confidence   
8. Offer a referral commission  
9. Provide content referral sources can share  
10. Treat vendors and suppliers as partners  
11. Update your LinkedIn profile  
12. Create a list of buyers you want referrals to  
13. Treat your clients as partners  
14. Give a referral  
15. Ask for referrals
