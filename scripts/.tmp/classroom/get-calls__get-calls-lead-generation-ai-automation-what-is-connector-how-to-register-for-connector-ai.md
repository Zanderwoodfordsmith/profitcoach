# What is Connector & How To Register for Connector Ai

## Body
### **What Is Connector?**

Connector is a cloud-based LinkedIn automation tool designed to streamline and optimize your LinkedIn outreach campaigns. With Connector, you can automate various actions on LinkedIn, allowing you to focus on high-value tasks.

### **Why Is Connector Important/Useful?**

* **Efficient Outreach:** Automate connection requests, follow-ups, and messages, dramatically increasing your outreach efficiency.  
* **Personalization at Scale:** Customize messages for different audience segments, ensuring each interaction feels personal and relevant.  
* **Increased Productivity:** Free up your time by automating repetitive tasks, allowing you to concentrate on strategic initiatives.  
* **Data-Driven Decisions:** Gain insights from detailed analytics and reports to fine-tune your campaigns and improve outcomes.  
* **Safe and Secure:** Operates within LinkedIn’s limits to ensure your account remains safe and compliant.

## **How To Do II**

### **Objective**

This SOP outlines the steps to register for Client Connect AI and connect your LinkedIn account for seamless integration.

### **Key Steps**

**Step 1: Access the Registration Website**

**generated-image-at-00:00:00**

* Go to the registration website: [connect.businesscoachacademy.com/register](https://connect.businesscoachacademy.com/register)  
* Alternatively, use the link provided in the video description.

**Step 2: Fill in Your Details**

* Enter your first name, last name, and email address.  
* Create a password.  
* Click on the 'Sign Up' button.

**Step 3: Automatic Login**

* After signing up, you will be automatically logged into your new account.

**Step 4: Connect Your LinkedIn Account**

* Click on 'Add Account'.  
* Select 'Add LinkedIn Account'.  
* Enter your LinkedIn login email address and click 'Add Account'.

**Step 5: Sign in to LinkedIn**

**generated-image-at-00:01:02**

* You will be prompted to sign in using your LinkedIn credentials.  
* Click 'Login'.

**Step 6: Verify LinkedIn Credentials**

* You will need to verify your LinkedIn credentials.  
* This may require using the LinkedIn mobile app.

**Step 7: Use the Mobile App for Verification**

* Log into your LinkedIn mobile app.  
* Look for a verification notification and click 'Verify'.

**Step 8: Resend Notification if Needed**

* If you do not see a notification, click 'Resend Notifications'.  
* If still no notification, click 'Try Another Way'.

**Step 9: Enter Authentication Code**

* If you receive a text message from LinkedIn, enter the authentication code provided.  
* Click 'Submit'.

**Step 10: Confirmation of LinkedIn Account Addition**

**generated-image-at-00:02:20**

* You will see a message confirming that your LinkedIn account has been successfully added.  
* Click 'Close'.

### **Cautionary Notes**

* Ensure that you have access to the email and mobile number associated with your LinkedIn account for verification.  
* If you encounter issues with verification, check your spam folder for any missed notifications.

### **Tips for Efficiency**

* Use a password manager to securely store your passwords.  
* Keep your LinkedIn app updated to ensure you receive notifications promptly.
