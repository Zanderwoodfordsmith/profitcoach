**📚 Summary:**

In this lesson, we will explore the long-term profit engine and its importance in business strategy. We will discuss the four pillars of the BOSS Programme, including financial control, sales and marketing, delivery, and resources. The episode emphasizes the need to set clear goals, identify core challenges, and address them to achieve long-term success.

#### **🤓 Learning Points:**

1. Understand the four pillars of the BOSS Programme: financial control, sales and marketing, delivery, and resources.  
2. Learn how to identify core challenges that hinder long-term growth.  
3. Gain insights into developing a business strategy and setting specific goals.  
4. Explore methods for evaluating current actions and implementing new strategies.  
5. Discover the importance of revisiting and refining your vision and mission statements.

#### **✅ Actions & Application:**

1. Reflect on your current business direction and assess the implemented actions.  
2. Identify and address any new challenges that have emerged.  
3. Develop possible solutions for future strategy sessions.  
4. Assess actions to continue, stop, or plan for long-term goals.  
5. Engage in constructive dialogue with colleagues or clients by asking strategic questions.
