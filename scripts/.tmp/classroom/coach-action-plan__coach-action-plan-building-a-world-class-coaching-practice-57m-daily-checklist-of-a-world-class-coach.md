## Guide
### What Is This

The D.A.I.L.Y Success Framework is a simple yet powerful system to help you focus on essential activities every day to grow your coaching business. D.A.I.L.Y stands for: Delivery, Acquisition, Interest Follow-up, Lead Generation, and You.

### Why Is It Important/Useful:

1. **Streamlines Your Day:** Keeps you focused on activities that directly impact your business.
2. **Boosts Productivity:** Helps prioritize tasks that drive results.
3. **Ensures Balance:** Encourages personal well-being alongside business tasks.
4. **Enhances Client Engagement:** Regular follow-ups and personalized outreach improve client relationships.
5. **Increases Lead Flow:** Consistent lead generation activities ensure a steady flow of potential clients.

## How To Do It

You want to focus on the top down but if you don't have anything to do in the first step you go to the next one.

### **D**elivery:

1. **Session Preparation:** Allocate time to prepare materials and tailor sessions to individual clients.
2. **Learning Coaching Content:** Dedicate time to reading the latest coaching methodologies or attending relevant webinars.
3. **Session Execution:** Deliver high-quality coaching sessions by being present, actively listening, and providing actionable insights.
4. **Session Follow-up:** Send follow-up emails summarizing key points and next steps.
5. **Client Feedback:** Gather feedback after each session and review it to identify improvement areas.
6. **Mastering Coaching Skills:** Learning Academy content, reading books, practising.

### **A**cquisition:

1. **Sales Calls:** Engage with potential clients through regular sales calls.
2. **Improving Sales Skills:** Review sales training materials or join group sales calls to enhance your techniques.
3. **Pitch Rehearsal:** Practice your pitch with a peer or record yourself to identify areas for improvement.
4. **Role-Playing:** Partner with a colleague to role-play sales scenarios and get constructive feedback.
5. **Sending/Following up invoices**

### **I**nterest Follow-up

1. **Lead Follow-up:** Daily check your list of leads who have shown interest but haven't committed and follow up with them.
2. **Inbox Filtering:** Filter your email inbox for any replies from interested leads and respond promptly using templates
3. **Scheduling Calls:** Send reminders to potential clients to schedule or reschedule calls.
4. **Personalized Messages:** Craft personalized messages for each interested lead to maintain engagement.
5. **Tracking Leads:** Use the CRM system to keep track of all follow-up activities and next steps for each lead.

### **L**ead Generation

**Volume (Do More)**

1. **Connector Campaigns:** Ensure your email automation campaigns are active, properly monitored, and analyzed for effectiveness.
2. **Open InMail Campaigns:** Launch and manage open enrolment campaigns to attract new clients.
3. **Personalized Outreach:** Send personalized emails or messages to potential clients to initiate contact.
4. **Content Posting:** Post valuable content on your blog or social media channels regularly.

**Refinement (Do it better)**

1. **Messaging Strategy:** Continuously refine your messaging to ensure it resonates with your target audience.
2. **Lead List Refinement:** Regularly update and refine lead lists to keep them accurate and targeted.
3. **Data Analytics:** Monitor key metrics such as open rates, click-through rates, and conversion rates to evaluate your lead generation efforts.

**Outbound Efforts:**

Ensure you are doing at least 200 outbound contacts per week to maintain a robust lead pipeline.

### **Y**ou:

1. **Skills -** improve your skillset by going through the programme, showing up to calls
2. **Sleep:** Aim for 7-8 hours of quality sleep each night to ensure you are well-rested.
3. **Exercise:** Incorporate regular physical activity into your daily routine.
4. **Nutrition:** Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains.
5. **Personal Development:** Invest time in learning more about the coaching system and other relevant topics to enhance your skills.

### Summary

By following the D.A.I.L.Y Success Framework, you can ensure that every day is filled with meaningful and impactful activities that drive your coaching business forward. Stay focused, take consistent action, and watch your success unfold!
