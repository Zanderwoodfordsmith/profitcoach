### **The Lead Generation Six**

We have 6 outreach campaigns you can use to build your business. In order or important from top to bottom:

1. **Connection Campaign**  - Automatically send out connection requests using connector software  
2. **Permission Pitch Campaign**- Craft personalised permission message to connection generated from connection campaign to get higher conversion and send Mini VSL explaining offer.  
3. **VIP Nurture (Top 100)**  - Choose the connections you consider to be ideal prospects to nurture with ongoing value based content better conversions.  
4. **Newsletter & Posting content**  - Build authority in your industry by posting a news letter and sharing content to increase conversion of all of the other campaigns.  
5. **Targeted Open InMail -** Double you lead generation efforts by sending up to 800 Open InMails per month  
6. **AnyBiz -** Only as a last option have a specific way to target any businesses in the world that are not specific to your target industry.

### **Optimising Outreach: Lead Generation Testing**

We follow a very specific process for running lead generation but first let me explain the reason why.

**Volume x Skill = Results -** You get the same results whether you send 10 messages at 100% vs 100 messages at 10%  
**Volume x Feedback = Skill -** The only way to develop skill is to actual do something and know whether it works.

Therefore:

**Volume x (Volume x Feedback) = ResultsVolume counts twice & feedback is important**  

It is essential to your lead generation efforts that sufficient volume is sent (another reason for using automation) along with the right process for gathering feedback for that is working. This is why we use a very specific testing process to improve.
