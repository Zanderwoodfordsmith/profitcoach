# LinkedIn Sales Navigator: Sign Up

## Body
### **What Is This:**

This lesson will guide you through the process of signing up for LinkedIn Sales Navigator and how you can get two months free if you have not used it before (and sometimes even if you have).

### **Why Is It Important/Useful:**

* **Access to Advanced Tools:** Unlock features like advanced search filters, lead recommendations, and InMail messaging.  
* **Enhanced Prospecting:** Improve your ability to find and connect with high-quality prospects.  
* **Insights and Analytics:** Gain valuable data to inform your prospecting strategy.

**Quick Caveat:**  

While LinkedIn Sales Navigator is an excellent tool for most industries and ideal clients, it might not be necessary for everyone. In some industries, leads may be easier to find or more active on other platforms. For example:

 **- Hair Stylists:** Often find leads through Instagram or Facebook where clients share and tag photos of their work.  
 **- Local Restaurants:** May benefit more from platforms like Yelp or Instagram.  
 **- Fitness Trainers:** Often use Instagram and YouTube to showcase workout routines and engage with potential clients.

## **How To Do It**

### **Step 1: Check for Free Options**

Before you sign up, you can either:

1. Follow [LinkedIn's Instructions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1355837) on how you can get access for free (Usually 1 month). I'm including the link because they may change things. If you have had it in the past you may need to contact them to get a free month.  
2. Alternatively, reach out to [LinkedIn Support](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/ask/sdsupport), as they sometimes offer free trials if you ask.  
3. Ask someone to refer you who already has a license as that will give you 2 months for free. You can ask friends or even someone in the Business Coach Academy community

### **Step 2: Just Buy It**

#### **If you can't get a free option just pay for it. It will be worth it's weight in gold.**

1. Here is the [sales navigator landing page](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/sales-navigator) explaining all the benefits and linsk to see if you are eligible for free trial.  
2. Here is the [sales navigator purchase](https://www.linkedin.com/premium/products/?utype=sales_ss) page (you only need core not advanced).

### **Step 3: Access Sales Navigator**

Click on "Sales Nav" located in the top right corner of the LinkedIn homepage.

### **Step 3: When You Are Ready To Search Click "Lead Filters"**

Click on "Lead Filters" to start customizing your search for prospects.

### **Objective:**

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to sign up for LinkedIn Sales Navigator and access its lead filters to begin your prospecting journey.

**Remember:** Taking advantage of free trial options can give you a head start without any initial investment but if it's slowing you down it's not worth it, just pay for it and get going. Your first client will pay for it 1000 times over. Once signed up, you'll be equipped to use all the advanced tools available in Sales Navigator to grow your coaching business.
