## Guide
### **What Is This**

The about section is the part of your LinkedIn profile that allows for the most text and copywriting.

### **Why Is This Important**

When someone receives a message from you they may look at your profile to see whether they can trust what you claim. It's vital that your about Section continues to demonstrate how you can help them.

### **How To Do It?**

-   **Step 1:** Complete The Form Below (examples Below Form)
-   **Step 2:** Once we send it back in 24-72 hours login and [edit your about section](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a553140).

### Form Questions:  

**LinkedIn Privacy**: this informs the team what format to write your profile in.

1.  Private - where you need to be discreet that you are a business coach
2.  Public - where you can be public about being a business coach

In both cases also please include:

**You Core Client:** please write out the detail (industry, size etc) e.g. *Engineering Business £1-5M* or *HVAC Companies 11-50 team*.

**Core Skills**: these should relate to the biggest problems your clients have that we will be messaging them about.

**Key Milestones:** list of 3-4 key milestones from your career, be as specific as possible.
