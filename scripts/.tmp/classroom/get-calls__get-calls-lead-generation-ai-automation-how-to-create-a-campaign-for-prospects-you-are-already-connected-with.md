# How to Create a Campaign for Prospects you are Already Connected With

## Body
## **Creating a LinkedIn Campaign with First Degree Connections**

### **Objective**

This training outlines the steps to create a LinkedIn campaign targeting first degree connections already in your network.

### **Key Steps**

**Step 1: Start the Campaign**

* Begin by launching the campaign creation process.  
* Name your campaign (e.g., 'Campaign 1C' for demo purposes).

**Step 2: Add from LinkedIn Search**

* Select the option to 'Add from a LinkedIn search' since you are targeting existing connections.

**Step 3: Access Your Connections**

* Navigate to your LinkedIn homepage.  
* Click on 'My Network'.  
* Select 'Connections' from the options.

**Step 4: Filter Your Connections**

* Click on 'Search with filters'.  
* Set the filter to 'People'.  
* Ensure you select 'First Degree Connections'.  
* Optionally, filter further by job title, location, or company.

**Step 5: Copy the URL**

* Once your filters are set, go to the search bar.  
* Copy the URL of the filtered search.

**Step 6: Paste the URL in the Campaign**

* Return to the campaign setup.  
* Paste the copied URL into the designated field.  
* Click 'Save'.

**Step 7: Adjust Connection Levels**

* Change the connection levels from 'Second' to 'First Degree Connections'.  
* Optionally, enrich emails and set to skip if in other campaigns.

**Step 8: Finalize the Campaign Setup**

* Continue setting up the campaign as per previous training.  
* Add templates and finalize details.

### **Cautionary Notes**

* Ensure that you are only targeting first degree connections to avoid any compliance issues.  
* Double-check the filters to ensure you are reaching the intended audience.
