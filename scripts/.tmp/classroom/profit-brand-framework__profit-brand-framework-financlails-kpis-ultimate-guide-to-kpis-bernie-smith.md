### **Ultimate KPI Guide**

This lesson is going to teach you how to organise and develop the right KPIs for your business, using the ROKS method developed by Bernie Smith. ROKS stands for Results-Orientated KPI System. 

It is laid out in 7 simple steps that you can implement into your own business.

The resource provided will take you through each step in detail, providing you with instructions, checklists and clear explanations.
