## **What Is This?**

This lesson focuses on practicing the pitch that you will deliver at the end of a sales call. It includes resources of an audio version of the pitch by our Head of Sales, Jack, and a detailed walkthrough by Zander, using the slide deck.

## **Why Is It Important/Useful?**

1. **Build Confidence**: Repeated practice will make your delivery smooth and confident.  
2. **Gain Mastery**: Familiarity with the pitch structure ensures you can adapt to any situation.  
3. **Enhanced Persuasion**: A well-practiced pitch is more convincing, increasing your chances of closing the sale.  
4. **Consistency**: Ensures that every sales call ends on a high note with a consistent and compelling message.  
5. **Personal Growth**: Recording yourself and refining your pitch helps you identify and improve areas of weakness.

## **How To Do It**

### **Step 1: Understand the Pitch Content**

* **Review the Pitch**: Start by thoroughly reviewing the pitch document and the accompanying slide deck.  
* **Listen to Jack's Audio**: Put the audios on your phone and listen tot hem over and over. Pay close attention to the audio version of the pitch delivered by Jack, our Head of Sales.  
* **Watch Zander’s Walkthrough**: Watch the main video where our CEO, Zander, walks through the pitch with the slide deck. Take notes on the points he emphasizes.

### **Step 2: Practice Your Delivery**

* **Script Familiarization**: Read through the pitch script several times until you are comfortable with the flow and content.  
* **Mimic the Experts**: Try to mimic the tone, pacing, and emphasis used by Jack and Zander in their recordings.  
* **Use the Slide Deck**: Practice alongside the slide deck to ensure you are in sync with the visual aids.

### **Step 3: Record Yourself**

* **Set Up Recording Equipment**: Use your smartphone or any recording device to capture your practice sessions. You can use loom online recording software.  
* **Record 20-40 Times**: Aim to record yourself delivering the pitch 20-40 times. This might seem excessive, but repetition is key to mastery.  
* **Review and Improve**: Listen to your recordings to identify areas for improvement. Focus on clarity, engagement, and confidence.

### **Step 4: Seek Feedback**

* **Peer Review**: Share your recordings with colleagues or mentors for constructive feedback.  
* **Self-Evaluation**: Compare your recordings to Jack’s and Zander’s versions. Make notes on what can be enhanced.  
* **Iterate and Improve**: Continuously refine your pitch based on the feedback received.

### **Step 5: Finalize Your Pitch**

* **Consistency Check**: Ensure that your final version maintains the core message and structure.  
* **Dry Runs**: Conduct dry runs without recording to build comfort and fluidity.  
* **Prepare for Real Calls**: Once confident, start using your pitch in actual sales calls.

## **Objective**

By committing to this rigorous practice regimen, you will enhance your pitch delivery, leading to improved sales performance and business growth.
