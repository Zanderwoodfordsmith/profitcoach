What is this?

This module guides you on how to watch training sessions using The Academy platform.

### Why is this important?

Understanding how to access and navigate through the training materials ensures you can make the most out of the resources provided and optimize your learning experience.

### Steps to Watch the Training

1.  **Sign into The Academy**: Open your browser, navigate to the [The Academy website](https://business-coach-academy.disco.co/home), and log in using your credentials.
2.  **Navigate to Learning Section**: Once logged in, locate (in the left-hand taskbar) and click on the 'Learning' section.
3.  **Choose Your Course**: Select the course you wish to view.
4.  **Choose Your Module**: Select which module you wish to view.
5.  **Select Your Lesson**: Select the lesson you wish to start.
6.  **Watch the Training**: Click 'Play' to begin watching the selected training session.

By following these steps, you can comfortably navigate through the training materials and enhance your learning experience on The Academy platform.
