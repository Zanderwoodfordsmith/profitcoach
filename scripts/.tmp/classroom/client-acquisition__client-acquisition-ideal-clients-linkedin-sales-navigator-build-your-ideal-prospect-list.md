## Guide
### **What Is This**

This is the primary method to create a targeted list of ideal prospects to market to.

### **Why Is This Important**

* Without a quality list of prospects your messaging will not be effective (x100 when we automate things)  
* You are limited in the number of outreaches you do so you want to be sending messages to the right people  
* You want to be speaking to the right people that will value your experience and expertise  
* If you try to market to everyone, your efforts will fail.

## **How To To Do It**

### **Step 1: Add Your Focusing Criteria To The Base Search**

Before you begin you need to load up your base search () Now add the criteria that will focus the search more.

**Pro Tip:** My favourite way to narrow is In the Current Company filter use the industry/business type and all its variations in here. If you require a phrase e.g. "IT Solutions" make sure you put quotes around it. Current Company search Examples below.

![CleanShot 2024-08-14 at 13.06.24@2x.png](https://qpqqwuxjzwwclebqcrfj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/academy-lesson-images/docs/18baabbeb5624db3.png)

**Trying Other Naming Ideas**

For certain industries you will need to be either less of more specific.

For example we had a coach who had grown a Chai Drinks brand so she was searching for "Drink" and "Drinks". However, not a huge number of people name their company with that word in the company name. Therefore to expand her lists she did two things:

**Less specific:** She decided to expand her market to also include food brands not just drink, but the only people who use food in their name are wholesale food companies or importers/exporters so she also needed to be more specific...

**More specific:** Instead of using the word drink or food she created separate lists with the words "Cookies", "Crisps", "Chips", "Coffee" etc because these companies did include those words in their name.

So are there other things you can try? If it's not working don't worry we have a solution.

**List too small?**  

 For industries that this does not work with you will have to remove this current company filter and instead use the keyword search at the top. Be very careful what you use here though because this searches their entire profile! So it is very difficult to get the ideal list quality (learn more here ).

 However, you can always refine the list manually later though. It's a bit more work but worth it to get a quality list.

---

### **Step 2: Check List & Add Any Necessary Excluding Criteria**

Even though this usually gives an extremely targeted list there are some industries where further refinement is necessary. For example if a coach searched law but they didn't want commercial law they could exclude "Commercial".

You want to look through the list on the 1st page look at 4-5 profiles then jump to some on page 3, page 5 results and check the quality. You can do this by reading the company and details (you don't have to click on their profile though you can) and Ideally 7 out of 10 profiles should be a good match.

To eliminate criteria from the search you type it in the field and hit enter and it will show green, then hover over it and hit the Stop.

![CleanShot 2024-08-04 at 19.43.24@2x.png](https://qpqqwuxjzwwclebqcrfj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/academy-lesson-images/docs/38acedf99736733e.png)

If your list size is too small e.g. below 500 then you can watch the other lessons about advanced searching.

### **Step 3: Save Your Targeted List**

You want to save this search to use in your marketing that you will learn how to do later. Click the toggle and then name it "{Industry} Prospects" List e.g. Engineering Prospects List

![CleanShot 2024-08-14 at 12.38.45@2x.png](https://qpqqwuxjzwwclebqcrfj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/academy-lesson-images/docs/1329ca3387e71f55.png)

### **Step 4: Access Your Targeted List**

To access this later click "Saved searches and chose the one you just saved. You can see how many people are in the search in the top right of the screen just below your profile picture.
