## Body
### **Resources**

* [Actionable steps to increase customer lifetime value](https://www.visiture.com/blog/9-actionable-steps-increase-customer-lifetime-value-ltv-online-brands/)  
* [How to increase customer lifetime value FAST](https://neilpatel.com/blog/increase-customer-lifetime-value/)

Making sure that the people you do business with remain subscribing to your service, using your products, or hiring your expertise is one of the most important things you need to focus on in your business.

Why?

The easiest way to grow a profitable business is to not lose any of the people you do business with.

Putting it like this might make it seem super simple, but it’s something that a lot of business owners forget.  It’s easy to put all your focus on marketing to new audiences and taking the ones you have for granted. 

**Below are 4 strategies that you can implement in your business in order to keep customers for longer.**

### **Pricing strategies**

Deciding on how you are going to price your products or services can be a difficult task. Nick Kolenda has written a great article on the psychology of pricing where he provides 6 main steps for making sure people react positively to your prices.

**1. Prime a small magnitude**

**2. Increase the fluency of your price**

**3. Maximise their reference price**

**4. Emphasise the gap between reference prices**

**5. Reduce the “pain of paying”**

**6. Use discounts properly**

For the full article – [Pricing Psychology](https://www.nickkolenda.com/psychological-pricing-strategies/)

### **Product Ladder**

Developing a product ladder is a great way to retain customers who have enjoyed one of your previous products.

How it works is you will provide a basic “foundational product” that consumers can start with.

Then you can provide other “add ons” that bring more value to consumer. you can create a range of offerings that will guide your clients or customers along a path that will provide them with more value and deliver you increased sales.

The aim of your product ladder is to create a pathway for customers to follow as they move through your sales funnel.,offering them products of increasing value at an increasing price.

This funnel will be structured according to the principle of AIDA which we spoke about in creating a website.

1. People at the top of the funnel, will be offered free and low-cost options. These are low risk for your customers and go a long way to building trust and confidence.  
2. After customers have seen the value you offer, they move into interest and decision mode as they consider buying from you. This is where you can use mid-range offerings to deliver better solutions to their problems.  
3. Finally, your clients or customers will reach the bottom of the funnel and the top of your ladder. This is where they know, like and trust you and are primed to take action and make a significant purchase.

### **Sell a wider range of things- Cross-sells**

To cross-sell is to sell related or complementary products to an existing customer. It is to get the customer to spend more money by adding more products from other categories than the product being viewed or purchased.

An example of cross-selling would be if a person is buying a laptop, a cross-sell would be to advertise laptop bags, speakers, or additional keyboards.

### **Keep customers longer- Retention**

**Send out a Company Newsletter**

Staying in touch with consumers is a great way to build trust and maintain a good relationship.

Besides sending monthly newsletters, giving subscribers early access to sales or deals, you could also personalise any follow-ups and make each customer feel like they do mean something to your business.

**Target customers with special offers**

Reward long-time customers with discounts, or freebies in order to ahow them your appreciation.

This might seem small, but thoughtful gestures like this go a long way.

**Build customer loyalty**

Customer loyalty can be built in many different ways, the top being good customer service. Make sure you reply to emails, phone calls and social media messages.

If a customer has an issue and it is sorted out swiftly and efficiently by your team, they will remember it forever.

Continue to provide excellent service to customers even after they have made a purchase, to maintain a good relationship.
