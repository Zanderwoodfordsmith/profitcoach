# Evaluating Prospect List KPIs

## Body
This lesson teaches you how to evaluate the key performance indicators (KPIs) of your prospect lists for automated marketing, focusing on both size and quality to ensure effective automated testing and high conversion rates.

## Guide
### What Is This

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate the key performance indicators (KPIs) of your prospect lists for automated marketing, focusing on both size and quality to ensure effective automated testing and high conversion rates.

### Why Is It Important/Useful

- **Effective Testing:** Ensure your lists are large enough for meaningful A/B testing.
- **Resource Optimization:** Avoid wasting time and effort on low-quality prospects.
- **Improved Conversions:** Increase the likelihood of converting leads by targeting the right audience.
- **Quality Assurance:** Maintain high standards by regularly checking and refining your lists.

## Principles of Evaluating Prospect List KPIs

### **List Size**

Each automated test requires 2 variations with 300 prospects in each, totaling 600 prospects. Therefore, if you can generate a list that exceeds the thresholds shown below, it means you have a robust list ready for future tests.

**Worried about your core client list size?**

The more ideal prospects the better. However, even with a list of 2000 we can run an entire 6 months of prospecting by recycling outreach to those who don't connect the 1st time. Those don't all have to be found in the 1st list.

## However, it's not all about size!

### **List quality**

It's equally important to ensure the quality of your prospect list. We don't want to be sending messages to the wrong people since the volume you can send each week is limited.

**A search will never be perfect.**

You want to check some profiles and if you feel 8/10 are a good match that list is ok to use. You can always tidy it up further later once you have .

![CleanShot 2024-08-04 at 19.15.41@2x.png](https://cdn.disco.co/media/CleanShot_2024-08-04_at_19_15_41_2x_b5cc5336-0e42-4c7c-984c-d8580477c683.png)

### How To Check List Quality:

1. **Manual Review:** Randomly check profiles to assess their relevance.
2. **Quality Benchmark:** Aim for at least 8 out of 10 profiles being a good match.

## Conclusion

Evaluating your prospect lists KPIs is crucial for successful automated testing and high conversion rates. Aim for a list size that fits your campaign needs and consistently check the quality to ensure you’re targeting the right prospects. By mastering these principles, you'll not only optimize your resources but also increase your chances of converting leads into clients.
