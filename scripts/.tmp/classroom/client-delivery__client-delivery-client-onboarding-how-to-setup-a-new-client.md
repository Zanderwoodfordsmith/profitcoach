## Guide
### **What Is This**

The super simple way a 7 figure coach manages her clients. Pam once had forty-seven 1-1 clients, so if it works for her it can work for anybody.

### **Why Is It important**

You want to be organised when working with your clients to be professional. Also start as you mean to go on, it's easy to manage when you only have 1-2 clients but when you have 6-15 you'll need to have a standard structure.

### **How To Do It**

### **Step 1: Setup A Folder in Google Drive Called "Clients"**

Every time you get a new client add a folder inside that one with their Business Name - Client Name e.g. Business Coach Academy - Zander Woodford-Smith

### **Step 2: Add Files you want your client to have to the folderPro Tip:** If you create a folder called "Example Client" with everything you want them to have, you can copy this each time and save yourself a lot of work

**Example/Recommended Folder Structure and files**

Business Name - Client Name

* **Coaching Session Recordings (Folder)** - put the recordings in here though you can also just copy links to a document.  
* **Coaching Sheets (folder)** - Keep all coaching sheets in once place and organise them with same name and this date format for order Client Name Coaching Sheet - YYYY MM DD e.g. John Bishop Coaching Sheet 2025 08 30 (this allows you to sort them by date). Also keep one they can make a copy of here.  
* **Alignment File**  
* **Other files you add later -** e.g. cashflow forecaster, revenue growth accelerator.

### **Step 3: Invite your client to the folder**

Right click on the folder and click Share, and share again.
