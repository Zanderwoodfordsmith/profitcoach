# How To Use The Coaching Sheet In Sessions

## Body
### **What Is This**

The lessons is Pam featherstone walking through how she uses the coaching sheet with her clients before, during and after sessions to keep them on track to achieving their goals.

### **Why Is This Important**

If you only had this tool and some coaching skills you could keep a client making progress. Everything else is just extra so if you master this you will always ensure you are adding value and your client recognises that value.
