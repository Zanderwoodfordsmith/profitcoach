## Body
## **What Is This?**

This lesson will guide you on how to create a simple yet effective LinkedIn banner that complements your profile and reinforces your professional brand.

## **Why Is It Important/Useful?**

* **First Impressions:** Your LinkedIn banner is the first visual element people see on your profile. A well-designed banner can set a professional tone.  
* **Branding:** It visually reinforces your brand, making your profile more memorable.  
* **Consistency:** A cohesive look with your other marketing materials enhances your professional appearance.  
* **Engagement:** A clean banner can make your profile more appealing, encouraging visitors to stay and learn more about you.

**What The Experts Say: Keep it Simple**  

We spent over £10,000 working with LinkedIn booking experts who send over 1 million connection requests per month. You know what they said for the banner. **Just a plain picture related to your industry no text or fancy headlines (alternative simple logo).**

**Mini Case Study**

One of our coaches had a campaign that was not working well and they simply changed their banner to be a picture related to their industry no text and the campaigns started improving.

## **Step 1: Create Your Design**

### **Option 1: Keep It Plain and Simple**

Using a plain image related to your industry is a safe and professional choice.

* **Example:** Select a high-quality image that represents your industry (e.g., a construction site, architectural blueprints).  
* Use these free websites to find image by typing in your words related to your industry:  
  * [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/)  
  * [Dupe](https://dupephotos.com/)  
  * [Pexels.com](https://www.pexels.com/)

### **Option 2: Use the Profit Coach Logo**

A simple banner with your Profit Coach logo can be an effective branding tool.

* **Example:** Make sure you have a high-resolution version of your Profit Coach logo.

### **Option 3: Customize a Banner Template**

For a more personalized touch, you can edit one of our banner templates or create your own.

* **Example:** Choose a pre-designed banner template from our collection. Customize it by adding your name, title, and any relevant images or colors that align with your brand.

**Resources**

---

### **Step 2: Upload Your Design To LinkedIn**

Once you've designed and selected your banner, go to your profile, click on the banner area, and upload the chosen image.

---

## **Final Tips**

* **Clarity:** Ensure your banner is clear and not overly cluttered.  
* **Relevance:** Choose images and designs that are relevant to your industry.  
* **Professionalism:** Maintain a clean, professional look that aligns with your brand.

*Remember, your LinkedIn banner is a visual representation of your professional identity. Keep it simple, relevant, and aligned with your brand to make a strong first impression.*
