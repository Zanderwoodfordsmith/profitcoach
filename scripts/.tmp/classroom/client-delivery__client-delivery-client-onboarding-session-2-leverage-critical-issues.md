### **What Is This?**

This session is designed to help your client make significant progress on a specific issue or seize an opportunity identified in the previous session using the dashboard assessment or another critical area they have highlighted. The focus is on empowering the client to find their own solutions, while you provide the tools and frameworks to facilitate this process.

### **Why Is It Important/Useful?**

* **Empowerment:** Encourages clients to develop their own solutions, enhancing commitment and ownership.  
* **Flexibility:** Adapts to the client's most pressing needs, ensuring relevance and impact.  
* Skill Development: Enhances your coaching abilities by using various methods to address different situations.  
* **Immediate Progress:** Aims to create a sense of achievement and momentum by tackling the biggest constraint.

### **Pro Tip for Coaches** 

The key to this session is flexibility and empowerment. Use your judgment to select the most appropriate method or tool to address the client's issue. Encourage self-reflection and responsibility, fostering a transformative coaching relationship. This approach not only resolves current issues but also empowers clients to tackle future challenges independently.
