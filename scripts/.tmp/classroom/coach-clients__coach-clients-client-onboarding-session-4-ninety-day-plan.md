### **What Is This?**

This session is about guiding your client to create a focused and actionable 90-day business plan. This plan turns their long-term vision into specific projects and goals that they can work on in the immediate future.

### **Why Is It Important/Useful?**

**Continuous Progress:** Regularly updating helps clients progress towards their business vision.

**Focused Execution:** Helps clients focus on key areas that need attention.

**Ownership and Clarity:** Clients create and own their plan, giving them clarity and commitment.

**Resource Management:** Helps prioritize tasks and manage time effectively.

**Motivation and Energy:** A clear plan energizes clients and keeps them motivated.
