### Support is crucial to your success.

### Support Structure

1. **Courses**: Access courses and materials, using the search bar at the top of the Academy homepage to find what you need and following the course content in order. This is an operating manual for a high-impact business coach.
2. **Community**: Ask our +100 active coaches because it's likely many of them already solved the issue you are facing and it's a fast way to get a response. You can click "Community" in the left hand navigation.
3. **Calls**: We hold 4 weekly live calls with specific experts and themes for each call. Get training and your questions answered.

## Top FAQs

These are the most common questions we get.

1. **How do I launch and manage my campaign or connector effectively?** Everything related to generating leads through connector campaigns and other methods can be found here and then you can specifically go into the Connector Category.
2. **What should I do if I'm having trouble accessing connector or the Academy?** All of our systems have a typical password reset functionality (a little text button saying "Forgot Password" underneath the login fields. So go to the site you need to login and use that. They will send you an email and you can reset your password. Then make sure you save all these passwords in one place. A good password to use is Coach!23
3. **Where can I find training materials and content in the Academy?** You want to go through the Academy in the order that is is laid out. You can find a break down of the curriculum here and you can access all the courses and training from your left side bar and clicking "learning" and then going to the course you want to access, then clicking on the module or lesson name.
