## Body
### **What Is This**

A way to announce you becoming a coach to let people know. This is optional so if you are keeping your coaching low profile you would not do this. 

### **Why Is It useful**

It can lead to some brilliant results. Interested prospects, publication and more. 

**Example**

[Simon Leslie’s Announcement Post -](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/simonleslieuk_businesscoaching-leadership-maximizeearnings-activity-7241740915877294081-p-Zw?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop) Over 100 comments + led to interested prospects and a featured write up in his industry’s largest publication.
