# How to Create Campaigns in Connect Ai

## Body
## **Creating a LinkedIn Campaign Using Connect AI**

### **Objective**

This training outlines the steps to create a LinkedIn campaign using Connect AI, ensuring that team members can execute the process efficiently and accurately.

### **Key Steps**

**Step 1: Create a Campaign**

* Click on **Create Campaign**.  
* Select **I'm an Advanced User** to use a pre-created template.

**Step 2: Name Your Campaign**

* Enter a name for your campaign (e.g., **Campaign 1**).  
* Add a short description relevant to your campaign.  
* Click **Save**.

**Step 3: Add Your Search from Sales Navigator**

**generated-image-at-00:00:59**

* Refer to the academy video for instructions on creating your search in Sales Navigator.  
* Copy the link of your search.  
* Click on **Add Search from Sales Navigator**, paste your search link, and click **Save**.

**Step 4: Configure Settings**

**generated-image-at-00:01:30**

* Review the settings available (these can be changed later).  
* Click **Save and Continue**.

**Step 5: Select a Template**

**generated-image-at-00:02:03**

* Click on **From Template** to view available templates.  
* Navigate to page two and select the template created by Zander.

**Step 6: Edit Connection Request Message**

* Review the connection request message in **Step 1**.  
* Click the pencil icon to edit the message as needed.

**Step 7: Customize Follow-Up Messages**

* Edit each follow-up message (Step 2, Step 3, etc.) by clicking the pencil icon.  
* Ensure to customize messages according to your industry, role, and country.

**Step 8: Format Placeholders Correctly**

**generated-image-at-00:04:23**

* Ensure all placeholders (e.g., first name, last name) are formatted with double brackets (e.g., {{first_name}}).  
* Replace any single bracket placeholders with the correct format.

**Step 9: Finalize Messages**

* Remove any instructional text or brackets that should not be sent.  
* Ensure all messages are ready for sending.

**Step 10: Save and Start Campaign**

* Click **Done** after editing all messages.  
* Click **Save** and then **Start Campaign** to begin.

**Step 11: Monitor Campaign Progress**

* Check the progress of the campaign as it fetches contacts and enrolls them into the workflow.

### **Cautionary Notes**

* Ensure that all messages are personalized and relevant to the recipient.  
* Double-check the formatting of placeholders to avoid errors in sending messages.

### **Tips for Efficiency**

* Familiarize yourself with the templates available to save time on message creation.  
* Use the academy resources for guidance on using Sales Navigator effectively
