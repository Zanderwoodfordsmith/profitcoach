# How to access support calls

## Body
### What is this?

This module provides details on how to access support calls on The Academy platform.

### Why is this important?

Accessing support calls is crucial for having your queries answered and getting additional guidance on your learning journey.

### Steps to Access Support Calls

1. **Navigate to Group Calls Section**: After logging in, click on the 'Events' section (using the left side-bar).
    1. **Alternatively:** In the 'Community' tab click on the 'Group Calls' section
2. **Access Support Calls**: Select the support call you wish to attend.
3. **Click the Attend Button**: This will add the call to your calendar.
4. **View your Calendar**: View the call in your calendar to confirm it is there.
5. **Join the call**: Click on the event in your calendar to join the call.

By understanding how to access support calls, you can ensure that you receive the necessary assistance and continue your learning smoothly.
