## Body
**What Is This**

This lesson is how to write your targeted open InMail message.

### **Why Is This Useful**

Without a compelling message the campaign will not be effective.

## **How To Do It**

**Step 1: Ask to "Write Open InMail message"**

**Step 2: Tell it to make message 10/10 on open InMail message checklist**

When it ask you for your connection message and message 1 then at the end also say "Please make message get a 10/100 on Open InMail message checklist"

**Step 3: Review Message Against checklist**

If the message does not match the checklist below the steps ask it to make any adjustments, or ask it for any other improvements.

**Step 4: Make any Adjustments Manually**

The bot may never get things 100% perfect so you can always tweak things yourself.

**Step 5 (Optional): Have message checked in community, on weekly calls or by your coach.**

---

### **Open InMail Message Checklist**

1. **Personalised Greeting:**  

   Hi {first_name},  

2. **Industry & Location Reference:**  

   I see you run a SaaS company in the UK.  

3. **Engaging Hook with Emoji (2-3 elements):**  

   Are you looking to:  
   1️⃣ increase ARR  
   2️⃣ reduce churn  
   3️⃣ streamline processes  

4. **Proof Statement:**  

   "I ask because..."  

5. **Combined System Introduction:**  

   ⚙️ I've combined that system with other methods to create The [Industry] PROFIT System.  

6. **Rapport through Problem Acknowledgment:**  

   For example, "The big problem we faced at Upshop was how to get more customers in the door (& stop the ones we got from leaving!)"  

7. **Common Industry Struggle:**  

   Many businesses in the [industry] industry struggle with this issue.  

8. **Crossroads Situation:**  

   They're struggling with their sales process and busy replacing lost revenue instead of closing bigger deals and growing ARR. But with The PROFIT System, we eliminated those issues.  

9. **Result Recap:**  

   Repeat the result: [specific result] by [brief description of the approach/method] and then "And I believe with the right system in place, your business can do the same."  

10. **Call to Action (CTA):**  

    ✅ Interested? or  
    ✅ Interested to know more?
