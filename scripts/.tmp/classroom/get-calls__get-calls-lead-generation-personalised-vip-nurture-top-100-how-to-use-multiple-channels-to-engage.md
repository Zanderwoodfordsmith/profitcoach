# get-calls-lead-generation-personalised-vip-nurture-top-100-how-to-use-multiple-channels-to-engage

## Body
### **What Is This**

This lesson focuses on utilizing various communication channels—LinkedIn, email, and phone calls—to effectively engage and nurture your top 100 prospects, ensuring you maintain a personal touch and consistent connection.

### **Why Is It Important/Useful**

**Increases Reach and Engagement:** Using various channels ensures you connect with prospects in their preferred way.

**Adds a Personal Touch:** Phone calls and personalized messages create more meaningful interactions.

**Builds Stronger Relationships:** Frequent and varied communication deepens your connection with prospects.

**Enhances Conversion Opportunities:** Engaging through multiple channels keeps you top-of-mind and boosts conversion chances.

## **How To Do It**

### **LinkedIn:**

**Engage with Posts:** Regularly interact with your prospects’ posts by liking, commenting, and sharing. This shows genuine interest in their activities and keeps you visible in their network.

**Use Direct Messages:** Send personalized messages using the INSIGHT framework. Ensure each message offers valuable insights or resources relevant to their business needs.

### **Email:**

**Send Tailored Content:** Craft emails that provide specific value, such as articles, resources, or checklists addressing their needs. Ensure the content is actionable and easy to digest.

**Regular Updates:** Keep your prospects informed about relevant industry trends or upcoming events that may interest them.

### **Phone Calls:**

**Direct Interaction:** Occasionally call your prospects to discuss insights or offer assistance. This adds a personal touch and demonstrates your commitment to their success.

**Follow-Up:** Use calls to follow up on previous interactions, answer questions, or delve deeper into how you can help them achieve their goals.

### **Objective**

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to effectively use LinkedIn, email, and phone calls to engage your top 100 prospects, providing value and building stronger, more personal relationships.

Remember, the key is to maintain consistent, valuable communication across channels, reinforcing your role as a trusted advisor and increasing your chances of converting prospects into clients.
