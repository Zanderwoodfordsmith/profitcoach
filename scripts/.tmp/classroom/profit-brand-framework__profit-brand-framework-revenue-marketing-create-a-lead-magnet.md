# profit-brand-framework-revenue-marketing-create-a-lead-magnet

## Body
### **Create A Lead Magnet**

### **What are Lead Magnets?**

Lead Magnets refers to the best and most important pieces of content on your website or blog. These are the pages that you want to rank the highest on search engines.

Blogs are a great way to showcase to consumers the extent and depth of your knowledge in any relevant industry. A blog will usually consist of a few pieces of cornerstone content supplemented by other articles.

Below is a list of possible formats you can use as your lead magnets. The point is not to use all the formats but rather to choose those that are most relevant to your target audience and niche. Spend some time researching the best topic and format before you begin.

### **Types of Lead Magnets: Create a webinar explaining your offer**

A webinar is an online presentation or conference held in real time. Viewers sign up to the webinar and are allocated a link and a time slot to attend the event.

Webinars help presenters connect quickly with potential customers, and is a great way to generate more leads. 

When you spend an hour or longer teaching people how to do something for free, they’ll feel obligated to reciprocate. After your webinar, you can then post the video on your blog or use it as a lead magnet. 

### **Great topics to discuss during your webinar:**

* Demonstrate products and services  
* Expand on content published in a blog post  
* Showcase your expertise

### **Do research and write a white paper relevant to your niche**

The purpose of a white paper is to help the reader understand an issue, solve a problem, or make a decision by offering technical information, images and diagrams. 

It’s a lengthy piece of content at approximately 2,000 words or 6 pages.

White papers are a great marketing tool, as they position the author to have authority on the topic. If the whitepaper is well researched and compiled, readers are more likely to trust the author or business that published the paper.

### **Interview one of your clients and write a case study**

A case study is an article focusing on a real life example, featuring extended testimonials on how a product or service helped a customer in the real world. It’s considerably shorter than a white paper, typically measuring around 800 words.

A case study allows you to frame your product in a way that makes it look good and desirable.

Case studies are a great way to show people that a product or service really helped another person improve their life, and allows other people to believe it can improve theirs too. 

Hearing a convincing pitch is great, but seeing how a product or service works in the real world goes a long way to convince people of its validity and building trust in your brand.

[Example of a case study](https://businesscoachacademy.com/10k-per-month-in-35-days-page-9241) 

### **Create an Ebook on a popular query in your niche**

Ebooks are a great way to establish your expertise and authority in any niche. By providing content that is of great value to your readers, you strengthen your relationship with them and build trust.

Before starting your ebook, do some research into your target audience and see what questions they are asking, or what skills they are wanting to learn.

The key of a successful ebook is to cover topics that your audience are genuinely interested in. 

### **Film a video**

Video is a format that is easy to digest, and allows you to engage more with your audience.

Allowing people to put a face and a voice to content is a great way to improve transparency, and allows viewers to feel a sense of familiarity.

Videos can be placed on your website, used on your social media platforms or even placed on YouTube.  

**In his book, [They Ask, You Answer](https://marcussheridan.com/they-ask-you-answer/), Marcus Sheridan discusses 4 types of videos that every business should have.**

These are:

1. **The 80% Video** – Sheridan discovered that 80% of the questions during a first-time sales call are the same. This video addresses these questions.  
2. **Bio Videos for Your Sales Team** – Everybody’s number-one overlooked email marketing opportunity is their email signature. Whether you’re in sales or not, everybody should have a video in their email signature  
3. **Landing Page Videos** – A video on the landing page offers further visual (psychological) confirmation that your prospects aren’t making a bad choice by giving you their private information.  
4. **Whom You’re Not a Fit For** – This video will help prospects decide whether you will be a good fit for them and vice versa.

### **Give your website viewers access to a Free trial of your product**

Creating a special offer or allowing people to have access to your product or service for a limited time before committing to a purchase, is a great way of bypassing any doubts or fears buyers might have. 

Once the free trial is over, make sure that it is easy for your consumer to take the next step and purchase the full version.

If your purchase process is complicated, your customers can leave your page and you’ll have to make an extra effort to attract them again.

Once you’ve got your funnel ready you need to actually get in front of your prospects. The fastest way to do that is through advertising.
